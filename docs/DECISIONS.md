# Decisions

One line per decision that changed the plan, with the date. Newest at the bottom.

- 2026-10-23: Stack fixed by the founder: React + Vite + pnpm on the front, Cloudflare Workers with Hono and D1 on the back, one Worker serving both. No Drizzle; plain SQL migrations.
- 2026-10-23: Anonymous device sessions first; email and password layered on top later. No OTP or Google sign-in until an email provider exists.
- 2026-10-24: Points are the only spendable unit inside the app. Money checkout is recorded in test mode until a payment provider is live. No "Kreators Cash".
- 2026-10-24: Wording is universal, never creator-only. Every action is a checkbox row. Spacing scale is 8, 12, 16, 32, 64, 120 only.
- 2026-10-24: Design tokens come from the freud UI Kit v1.5 in Figma and are frozen in the repo. Dark mode reverses the same hues rather than going grey.
- 2026-10-24: Feature flags resolve on the server (switch, minimum role, rollout, overrides) and the client reads `access` from `/api/me`.
- 2026-10-24: Content rewards pay per thousand verified views. YouTube is read automatically with a key; every other platform is typed by the creator and confirmed by a reviewer. Payouts never exceed the pool and need the finance permission.
- 2026-10-24: Playbook tiers adopted: parity features built plainly, attribution and funnels as the difference, white-label app, marketplace and instalments skipped.
- 2026-10-24: Tracked links write the click after the redirect (`waitUntil`) instead of through a queue; no Queues binding yet.
- 2026-10-24: Auto-DM queues matched comments when Meta is not connected so the flow can be tested with the simulator; live sending needs the Meta secrets.
- 2026-10-24: Circles seat people within twenty kilometres when positions exist, otherwise by city; positions are rounded to three decimals and never returned by any endpoint.
- 2026-10-24: Payment provider sits behind `apps/api/src/payments.ts`; Razorpay is the first adapter, live only when `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID` and `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET` are set. Money orders stay `pending` until the webhook confirms; the booking happens after the order, not inside it, so slot holds are not needed.
- 2026-10-24: Transactional email goes through an outbox table; Resend sends when `RESEND_API_KEY` is set, otherwise the admin can read what would have gone out.
- 2026-10-24: Product analytics and funnels live in our own `analytics_events` table rather than PostHog, so nothing leaves the Worker.
- 2026-10-24: Availability is written in the creator's IANA zone with date overrides, buffers, notice and a booking horizon; `slotsFor` in shared is pure and unit-tested.
- 2026-10-24: Memberships can carry a period in months; entitlement checks the order's expiry, and buying again renews.
- 2026-10-24: Account deletion anonymises the person and keeps money rows for the books; export is one JSON file.
- 2026-10-24: Home is the landing screen and the one dashboard. Results come before actions on it, and its guidance line always names the next box and what it will not work without. Every screen opens with `PageHeader` and is built from `Section`, `StatTile` and the shared charts; card shadows carry a hairline edge so they show on cream and in dark mode.
- 2026-10-24: Circles repeat weekly by default: the next occurrence opens on its own once the current one's tables are set, cancelling the latest ends the series. Joining asks how you will show up (single wears red, committed wears white, or rather not say); the answer is stored per signup, shown only to its owner, and never returned for other members.
- 2026-10-24: Dashboards follow the freud kit's Home and Mental Health Metrics frames (greeting with pills, score and mood card pair, tracker rows with mini visuals, score hero with per-area bars, mood history row) rather than our own layout, so the app reads like the design source.
- 2026-10-24: Contrast rule adopted from the kit: on a solid colour the foreground is white (yellow takes brown); dark elements live only on cream, card and tint surfaces. The orange and green relationship colours switched to white text for the same reason. Dashboards draw with the kit's chart vocabulary (area lines, pill bars, gauges, donuts, bubbles, month grids, emoji rows) rather than plain bars.
- 2026-10-24: Moods are drawn faces in the kit's style, never system emoji; every hero and metric card carries one of the seven characters whose expression follows the number; each metric screen and Home show a thirty-day or fourteen-day line graph, because the founder found the plain versions too flat.
- 2026-10-24: Line charts always draw their frame (y axis with labels, baseline, gridlines) even when empty, so an empty chart still reads as a chart; Home and Life carry a week chart of mood, sleep and calm on one scale.
- 2026-10-25: A local-only demo seed (`POST /api/demo/seed`, gated by `DEMO_SEED` in `.dev.vars`, own account only, replaces its own rows) fills the charts so the dashboard can be judged with data. Line charts keep a small floor above the axis so a zero line is its own line; the service worker takes over on the next load so a new build is seen without closing tabs.
- 2026-10-25: Onboarding asks which floors to work on first and only asks those; the rest are answered later from the Tower (`/vibe-check?floor=`). On the derived screen the person picks the value goal and project per floor, or writes their own, before building. The characters walk along the bottom as questions are answered, driven by GSAP, which is the animation library from here on.
- 2026-10-25: The first three task ticks ever are free; after that a task needs its minimum age and a ten-minute gap since the last tick, so ticks are never back to back.
- 2026-10-25: Apps start off except school, work, army, journal, life, shop (and profile); the Apps page carries the switches and shows on and off states; enabled apps sit in the laptop sidebar under category toggles; Rank and Rewards are part of the game and live under You. Every app screen has a back button and the colour headers use the laptop width.
- 2026-10-25: Circles and Bond chats are anonymous for good: no reveal, member ids stay on the server, a chat from a table is opened by alias. Bond gains friends (request, accept) and anonymous one-to-one chats; every person has a public page reachable from the community feed.
- 2026-10-25: Dark mode wears each app's relationship hue (paper, card and line mixed from the deep tint) instead of plain brown. The Club member badge exists. Demo data is opt-in, greyed and removable.
- 2026-10-25: The Apps page has no switches. It is a grouped list with a colour per group; a grey app asks before it is switched on, and switching off lives only under You, Features (same group colours), so nothing is turned off by accident.
- 2026-10-25: Plain words everywhere, for a teenager: areas (not tower or floors), a pyramid of six layers per area (foundation, rule, feeling, proof, project, task), and a daily small act (not spark). Keys and routes keep their old names. An animated pyramid (GSAP) teaches the layers; onboarding shows it only after the answers, following results first, then what did it, then the one rule it will not work without. Areas, Profile and Apps use the full laptop width; sheets have a close button.
- 2026-10-25: Tabs are Home, Daily, Areas, Apps, Profile (Today and You renamed; the public page module is Public page). App groups are Contribution, Creation, Connection, Reflection, Essentials, Operations. Daily, Areas and Profile are single full-width columns. Search answers inline under the field. The onboarding explainer is a tappable pyramid with one card at a time under it, so nothing lands all at once.
- 2026-10-25: The public page is opt-in part by part: one switch for the whole page, then links, portfolio, products, posts, activity (level, streak, areas, badges), friend requests and chats. The server enforces every switch; a switched-off page returns only the name. Chats from a page need an accepted friendship and the other person's chat switch.
- 2026-10-25: Every screen is one full-width column on a laptop; only Home keeps two. A new page starts at the top. The search field lights its border on focus. Interests are grouped like a dating app (creative, sport, food, music, screens, outdoors, learning, wellness, causes, home), for everyone, ten on at most. The public page shows social icons; the profile tab and Home link to your own page. In a row of equal choices no button is solid until picked. The pyramid's replay button became a line of text; tapping the pyramid replays.
- 2026-10-25: An Internal group, visible only to staff and admins, holds Observability at /internal/observability: people in numbers, people per app, every account with its apps on, role changes by an admin (never their own, audited), and the build map embedded from docs/build-map.html so there is one copy. Each role has a badge on the profile picture and under the sidebar logo; earned badges stay on the profile and public page.
- 2026-10-25: Badges have a switch of their own on the public page (on by default), separate from activity; the page shows earned badges and awarded honors in one Badges section.
- 2026-10-25: School carries two real programs seeded by migration from the skill files in apps/web/public/resources: Editing fundamentals and Skills using ChatCut and AI, seven lessons each plus one assignment, in a self-paced cohort that is always open. Lessons are rewritten in a direct second-person teaching voice (hook, promise, short sections, pro tip, result, bottom line) and link the full skill file. Lesson bodies render as small markdown. The smoke test's placeholder program no longer wears the real program's name, and the migration removes the old placeholders.
- 2026-10-25: The public page is the entry point, so it got a links page like a Linktree: link, header, text, image, video (YouTube only) and carousel blocks, up to thirty, https only, with a switch of its own, taps counted for the owner, and a "Made with kreators.app" footer on every page that links to the site home. Sidebar app groups start folded to cut the noise; the group you are inside opens on its own.
- 2026-10-25: Sign-in by magic link, built in (no auth package, to keep the anonymous-first session model): email in, a one-time hashed link out, fifteen minutes, same answer for any email, rate limited; the link makes the account or attaches the email to the device's progress; a password can be added under Profile afterwards and used instead. Mail needs Resend credentials (RESEND_API_KEY with sending access, EMAIL_FROM on a verified domain, optional APP_URL); without them every mail waits in the outbox.
- 2026-10-25: Magic links are out again (Resend cannot send from a Gmail address, and a sender domain was not wanted yet); email and password is the one way in. Sign-up asks for the password twice; one shared password rule runs on the form and the server; sign-in gives the same answer and takes the same time for an unknown email and a wrong password, with a per-email limit on top of the per-address one; a password change needs the current one and signs out every other device; the API sends the standard security headers. Password reset waits for outgoing mail on a verified domain.
- 2026-10-25: Password reset is in: a one-time hashed link, thirty minutes, the same answer for any email, every session signed out when it is used; without Resend credentials the mail waits in the outbox where an admin can pass the link on. The founders' addresses (apps/api/src/admins.ts, plus ADMIN_EMAILS) become admins the moment they sign up or sign in, audited as "founder email"; until mail verifies addresses that means signing up with them right after a deploy.
- 2026-10-25: Sign out is one shared button, in the laptop sidebar footer and the Profile tab's top row and account card; a device-only account gets a warning and a sign-up link first. New accounts start empty by design: nothing is seeded on sign-up, and demo data stays a local-only, opt-in button.
- 2026-10-25: The site's "Go to app" leads to sign-in (Home when a session exists) and "Start free" to sign-up; the anonymous start stays reachable from the sign-in page. The local dev database was cleared of every test account and listing the smoke runs had left, and the smoke test now runs against its own server and database (port 8788, persisted in the scratchpad) so that never happens again. The admin console became one data table per list: sort, search, column filters, row selection with bulk actions, and in-place editing of role, Pro, flags, rollout, live and featured.
- 2026-10-25: The demo seed is gone: no route, no DEMO_SEED, no buttons under Profile, no grey demo rows. The app holds no mock data; the smoke server keeps its test accounts in its own database.
- 2026-10-25: Link previews and home-screen icons: real PNGs (192, 512, a maskable 512 and a 180 Apple icon) and a 1200 by 630 share card, rendered from icon.svg by scripts/icons.sh, replace the SVG-only set that WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn and iOS could not show. The Worker now serves the site home so og:image and og:url come out absolute on whatever origin the app runs on; other routes keep the relative tags. The two shipped strings that said "one tower" now say "seven areas".
- 2026-10-25: Dark mode audit. The ink fill flips to cream in dark mode, so the on-ink token is the only foreground allowed on it; the public page header, the Rewards balance card, the Leaderboard's own row, Home's header pills and level bar, and the celebration card had literal white there and vanished. The landing page's walking characters are clamped so the row never leaves a phone screen. Landscape phones: md and lg now need 480px of height as well as width, so a phone on its side keeps the bottom tabs and one column; beside the sidebar the column takes the remaining width instead of the full width shifted right, which had overflowed on tablets. Phones get an apps menu button next to the theme button at the top right, a sheet with the same groups the laptop sidebar folds; the bottom tabs stay. Page headers start lower on phones to clear the two buttons.
- 2026-10-25: The "How this works" guide card was the last white-on-ink leftover. A contrast probe now runs in the screenshot tool over every route and the sheets, menu, celebration, laptop and site views in both themes; the audit is clean apart from two kit choices left as they are: white captions on the green fill sit near 2:1 over the hero blobs, and unearned badges are greyed on purpose.
- 2026-10-25: Daily gets a not-to-do list under Tasks: things to stay away from, up to seven, each a checkbox row with its own streak and a week of dots. The streak rule lives in shared (today counts once ticked, yesterday holds the run until then, a slip or a missed day ends it). A kept day pays 5 xp once and is never clawed back; the streak, not the xp, is the prize. Today or yesterday can be marked, and the three-dot button offers a slip and a remove. Migration 0021.
- 2026-10-25: Reviews: a new app under Reflection for weekly, monthly, quarterly and mid-year reviews. A period is pure arithmetic in shared; the numbers for a period come from every app's own table, next to the period before, and the same "By period" panel sits on every dashboard rather than each one growing its own filter. A review is saved with its prompts and a snapshot of the numbers, so history never rewrites itself. Built-in questions per cadence, plus recurring templates of your own. Migration 0022.
- 2026-10-25: The "no marketplace" line is partly reversed, on purpose and within limits: the Store app adds a direct shelf of new gear that only staff stock and that sells through partner links (Kreators earns a share, never holds the goods or the money), a pre-owned shelf where creators sell to each other with offers and a chat (the two settle payment and handover themselves, as on circlestore.in, minus the pickup and verification that site runs), kits by stage and budget, and paid featured spots (a week at the top for 200 points, free from staff). Partner taps go through /out and are counted with a salted hash. Nothing in this is a marketplace that moves money: the money order rule in payments.ts is untouched. The website got feature pages for Reviews and the Store, and the Daily page copy mentions the not-to-do list. Migration 0023.
- 2026-10-25: The product is now called kreators.app everywhere people can read it; identifiers keep kreators-life. Community got a feed with sorts (recent, popular, most replies) and filters (everyone, following, mine), a follow button on every post, and small capped rewards for posting, replying, liking, being liked and being followed, paid once per thing so nothing can be farmed. Sellers carry a trust score built only from what they earned (xp, points, best streak, time here, sales and rating, honors), shown on the public page, the product page and every pre-owned listing.
- 2026-10-25: Back now means back: the way-back button pops one history entry when there is one, scroll positions are restored by the router, and the filters on Community and the Store live in the URL so the page you return to is the page you left. Finance lost its invoice button and list (the API and the public invoice page remain for links already sent) and its actions sit in two columns with shorter names. The vibe check got a close button on every frame. The pyramid's layer labels balance their lines and clamp at two, so no word sits alone. The FAQ rows on Help and on the site share one disclosure style: a round chevron that turns, a tinted open row, and a focus ring only from the keyboard.
- 2026-10-25: Renamed, at the founder's ask: the seven areas are now *pillars* (tabs, screens, site, help, nudges, badges, review prompts), and the three middle layers of a pyramid are a *guiding principle* (was rule), a *value goal* (was feeling) and a *goal outcome* (was proof); the base stays a foundation and the tip a task. The internal node kinds (pillar, principle, value_goal, outcome) were already those words, so nothing in the database or the API moved. The five areas of life keep the word area. Every layer has its own definition under Words we use, and the Pillars how-it-works explains each one.
- 2026-10-25: The floating "How this works" pill and its sheet are gone; every app's main screen now ends with a "How it works" card: numbered rows, three to five steps that say what happens, what it pays and where it goes, then the words it leans on. The card is one button, closed by default, that opens and closes the steps and the words together; each of those is a smaller toggle inside. Circles dropped its hand-written copy of the same list and uses the shared one. Detail pages leave it to the main screen. Rows of pills that scroll sideways share one class that bleeds into the gutters, so a hovered pill and its shadow are no longer cut off (Community was the worst case). The theme button moved from the sidebar footer to beside the role badge at the top.
- 2026-10-25: Kreators Run, a new app under Connection that is Party for runners. The thing that makes its points honest is the start code: every run gets six letters only the host sees, shown at the line, and an entry is accepted only with that code, only inside the run's window (thirty minutes before to three hours after), only once per runner. A logged run pays 20 xp and 10 points, two paid runs a day at most, with the minutes counted as movement in Life so the physical score moves; a host can void an entry and the pay reverses. Three run badges (first, ten, a hundred kilometres) come from the same stats as the others. Brand collabs are a tab of their own, with the giveaway written on the collab before any club applies, and an accepted collab shows its giveaway on the club and on the runs tied to it. The website lists it under Friendship and under Physical. Migration 0025.
- 2026-10-25: The ink went from brown to a near-black grey in both modes, icons and share card included; the seven relationship colours keep their hues. Tasks with a date now wait for it. Products, store items and tools can ask for a points balance, a badge, or both, with one rule and one wording in shared. Search covers apps, help, the store, tools and posts. Every app got a three-step "How this works" pill and the words we made up are defined once, in TERMS, and listed under Help, next to the one list of how everything pays; nudges are single quiet lines that point there. The landing page lists every app a relationship serves and can switch to the five areas of life. Migration 0024.
- 2026-10-25: The smoke test, the screenshot tool, the contrast probe, the spacing and colour scans and the Flesch scorer lived in a session scratchpad and were lost when the repo moved to a new machine. They were rewritten and now live in the repo (scripts/smoke and scripts), with the smoke server's database, log and screenshots beside them and ignored by git, so they move with the code. The smoke checks send their own CF-Connecting-IP so the sign-up and sign-in limits never block a rerun. The school seed generator was not rewritten: migration 0016 is the source now.
- 2026-10-26: The planner docs in plannerdocs (the v0.1 blueprint written before the build) were rewritten to describe kreators.app as built, each ending with a known-gaps list, and the commerce playbook got a status note. The audit behind it found three rules the app does not keep: module flags are resolved on the server but no route refuses a switched-off module (the smoke check accepts a 200), the Club advertises club-only Work listings and a named manager that are not built, and posts, replies and chats carry no rate limit or report button. Those stay as recorded gaps; no code changed.
- 2026-10-26: Six of the gaps the planner audit found are closed. Module flags are enforced on the server: every API prefix maps to its module in access.ts and a switched-off, rolled-out-past or overridden module answers 403 (the smoke check now demands it). Every rate limit lives in apps/api/src/limits.ts, one line each with the route it guards, and posts, replies, likes, follows, chats, offers, listings, tickets, applications and run entries are limited too. Sign-up asks for a phone number and a date of birth and refuses anyone under eighteen (migration 0026); the terms say eighteen. The Club's two paper benefits are built: club-only Work listings only members see and can apply to, and a named manager a reviewer picks (migration 0027). A reward can change after launch through one route that bumps the version, audits the change and tells every entrant. Every long list, admin tables included, is served a page at a time (10, 25, 50 or 100 rows, the page in the URL). Email verification, a staging environment and backups wait.
- 2026-10-26: Every route was rechecked at phone width with a new overflow probe (and a landscape pass). The one real break was the public site: its header links carried `inline-flex` next to `hidden`, which Tailwind 4 orders the other way round, so the links never hid and every site page laid out 742px wide on a 390px phone. The phone header now holds the logo, Go to app and the theme button, the evolve row stacks, and the probe reports 0 hits over all routes. A `shots-all.mjs` prep script gives the screenshot tool a real account with data behind every detail page.
- 2026-10-27: Content is a strategist now, built after a look at sandcastles.ai (short-form research: channel feeds, outlier videos, transcripts, viral analysis, hooks, scripts, a persona, workspaces per niche, strategy audits). One folder per brand holds the links it is fed, the research runs on them and the pieces written from that; each piece says who wrote it. Research goes through Agent Reach (the read-only CLI at github.com/Panniantong/agent-reach) over a small bridge the Worker calls, with plain page reads and the YouTube Data API as fallbacks, and anything else answers "needs Agent Reach" rather than pretending. Writing goes through Claude by the official SDK when a key is set, else Workers AI, else a labelled digest. The attribution screen stays under Results. Only the brand folder goes to the model; folders export and delete with the account; the privacy page says so. Migration 0028.
- 2026-10-27: The Apps page shows only the apps that are on, with one highlighted card at the top that leads to Profile, Features, the one place to switch apps on or off; off apps are no longer listed grey. - 2026-10-27: Onboarding is the strategy session from the product spec, built as self-serve cards before the vibe check: the map of sixteen named levels placed twice per relationship (core and floor), fears and insecurities early, a scorecard checked against behaviour, the real day, a focus of two with floors under Courage locked in, a routine ramped thirty minutes a week, practices, a signed contract and the live-call note last.
