# Superfocus study (2026-11-01)

What superfocus.me offers, what we took, where it went, and what we left. Superfocus calls itself a goal-to-action system: goals, a calendar, focus sessions and a coach in one place. It sells one paid yearly membership.

## How this was gathered, and how far to trust it

A read of the site's own home page and its founding-members page (both loaded). Its sign-in app was not opened, so how any screen works inside is not known; what is below is what the site says and shows. Where the site only names a thing ("Plan strength", "Compass") without saying what it is, we did not guess its meaning: we built our own plain version of the idea, and left the rest. Nothing is copied: no wording, no names of its own, no design.

## What the site lists

- **A week plan with coach suggestions.** The coach drafts the week around your goals and you keep what fits.
- **Plan more effectively.** Big goals broken into seasons, and the actions that move them most in the time you have.
- **Focus timer and focus quality.** Focused time, a quality reading and how many sessions.
- **Focus music.** A lo-fi playlist.
- **App and website blocking across devices.**
- **Time blocking and calendar sync.**
- **Coach recommendations and a goal trajectory.** A timeline per goal, "On track", "Adjust" or "Off track", and a "Day X of Y" count.
- **AI daily and weekly coaching.**
- **Plan strength and Compass.** Named, not explained.
- **Integrations.** Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, Slack, Fathom and Granola.

## What we took, and where it lives

| Their idea | Ours | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Week plan with suggestions | **Plan my week**: a suggested day for each open task that has none, the most important first, each on the lightest day it fits in. You keep or change each one. | Daily |
| Plan strength | **Plan strength** out of 100: days with something on them, days that are not overloaded, tasks that say how long they take. Shown now and with the suggestions kept. | Daily, in Plan my week |
| Coach notes | A few plain notes from rules ("Monday has 5h 30m planned"). No model, so no credits. | Daily, in Plan my week |
| Focus quality | Rate a finished focus session from scattered to deep; the average shows on the focus dashboard. Private, pays nothing. | Mindful minutes |
| Focus music | A synthesized **Lo-fi** background sound, made in the browser. No song, nothing to license. | Mindful minutes |
| Time blocking | **Time blocks**: the day's timed tasks as blocks of their estimated length, with the free time between and overlaps flagged. Since 2026-11-06 the blocks are the person's own (Deep work, Recovery, Sleep...), laid out by day templates and a weekly pattern, and tasks fall into them (docs/time-blocks.md). | Daily |
| Calendar sync | A **private calendar link** to subscribe to from Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar. One way: tasks appear in the calendar. | Profile |
| Goal trajectory | A project's **pace**: on track, slipping or behind, from the share of tasks done against the share of time gone, with "Day X of Y". | Pillars |

## What we left, and why

- **App and website blocking.** A web app cannot block other apps. It would need a phone or desktop app with system permission. Help says so plainly and points to a phone's own focus mode.
- **Two-way calendar sync, and Slack, Fathom, Granola and Outlook integrations.** Each needs a partner account and a sign-in (OAuth) with keys we do not hold. The calendar link covers the common need, seeing tasks in the calendar you already use.
- **A licensed playlist.** Music needs licences. The Lo-fi sound is ours.
- **AI daily and weekly coaching.** The assistant already exists, paid in credits, and the daily brief is rules only. The week plan's notes are rules, so a free person gets them too.
- **"Seasons".** Our words for a stretch of work are project and review period (a week, a month, a quarter, a half year). A project's pace gives the same "am I on track" answer without a new idea to learn.
- **Compass.** The site does not say what it is.
- **Pricing.** Superfocus charges for the whole product. Ours stays free to start and free to stay, with a plan only for typing to the assistant.
