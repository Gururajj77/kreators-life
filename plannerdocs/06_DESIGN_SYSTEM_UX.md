## 1. Experience goal

One coherent product, not a directory of microsites. Every screen is built from the same few parts. Every tappable thing lifts the same way. Every app explains itself the same way at the bottom of its screen. The whole thing reads like one quiet app. The look is calm glass on a soft gradient, not flat fills, set in one typeface on Apple's text styles. Results first, then what did it, then the one thing to do next.

## 2. Information architecture as built

- **Phone:** one column, five tabs along the bottom: Home, Daily, Pillars, Apps, Profile. The top-left corner holds the kreators.app logo (to Home). The top-right corner holds two buttons: theme on the left, an apps menu on the right (a `Sheet` listing the sidebar's groups, which fold like the sidebar). The assistant's round button sits at the bottom right, above the tab bar. The top buttons are `absolute`, not `fixed`, so they scroll away with the page. The first header of every screen starts 64px down to clear them. A phone on its side keeps the phone layout (`md` and `lg` need height as well as width).
- **Laptop:** the tabs become a sidebar (`components/TabBar.tsx`). Open, it is 240px; folded, it is a 76px icon rail (`--nav-w`, set by `html[data-nav]`, kept on the device as `kreators.navMini`). The fold button is a round button on the sidebar's right edge. Folded, the rail shows k with the dot, the theme, the five main tabs and Sign out as a door icon, with names as tooltips. Open, the role is a tag in its pillar's soft colour beside the theme button. **A bell** (2026-11-07, `BellLink`) is a round link to Inbox left of the theme button, and under it above Home when the sidebar is folded. It carries the unread count that used to sit on the sidebar's Apps tab, and it stays still on purpose. A phone has no bell, so its Apps tab keeps the count. Apps that are shown are listed under their group. Contribution and Essentials start open until the person picks otherwise, and the group of the app you are in opens too. A running focus clock sits sticky at the top of the sidebar, or as a round dot on the folded rail. From `lg` the column keeps one `lg` gap after the sidebar, so words never touch it.
- **Tablet (768).** `md` brings the sidebar, so a tablet's column is 464px, narrower than it looks. A grid of tiles, cards or halves that hold words starts its columns at `lg`, and a tablet takes the phone's layout: Home's two columns and halves, badges and honors, plans, a period's tiles, Store's shelves, a client's tiles, business metrics, the Discover add-ons, the focus dashboard and the build map. The water bubble and its lane on Daily start at `lg` too, so a tablet has the section inline. Laptops did not change. Rag and orphan hits at 768, counted for the first time on 2026-11-07, fell from 102 in light and 94 in dark to 16 and 12.
- **Groups and their names (47 modules, counted from `MODULES` in shared, listed in their default order):** Contribution (School, Work, Army, Win, Agency), Reflection (Life, Journal, Reviews, Mindfulness, Fitness, Wellbeing, Mood, Sleep, Stress, Coaching, Companion, Discover), Connection (Community, Kreators Run, Club, Party, Bond, Circles, Games), Creation (Shop, Public page, Content, Audience, Auto-DM, Finance, Fund, TV, Knowledge, Tools, Store, My purchases), Essentials (Home, Daily, Pillars, Search, Leaderboard, Inbox, Help, Assistant), Operations (Admin, Manage, Dev Tools; the team's group, always last). The module `observability` is called **Dev Tools** on screen since 2026-11-07; its route, key and code keep the old word. Each group wears a relationship colour (`MODULE_GROUP_ACCENT`).
- **Apps page:** the Apps page and Profile, Features both draw `components/AppGroups.tsx`. Every group is one big accordion in its pillar colour saying "N of M shown". Opening it lists the apps that are shown: the name opens the app, and a quiet round 44px eye (the arrange arrows' `toy toy-soft` look) hides it. **Hide and unhide, never on and off** (2026-11-06): a hidden app leaves the list for a short, full-width Hidden apps `Fold` at the end of the group, each inside with Unhide. On Apps the count line reads "N shown, M hidden", and while an app is hidden a round `EyeOff` button opens every hidden app in one inline panel (`HiddenAppsPanel`). Home's Go to and Wellbeing have the same eye. The change shows at once, put back on a refusal. The groups are in one fixed order everywhere (`MODULE_GROUP_ORDER`: Contribution, Reflection, Connection, Creation, Essentials, Operations). How each group starts and the order of apps inside it are the person's own, saved on the account (`users.app_prefs_json`). Under Profile, Features each group has a "Starts open on Apps and in the menu" switch. "Arrange apps" turns the rows into a drag list (`SortableList`), with Move up and Move down; apps only move inside their group. Apps the person has not placed come in the **fixed default order**, which is `MODULES`' own. What is on comes first (`useModules`). The founder set this order on 2026-11-07, and it ended the rule that lists moved by context: apps stay where they were last seen, `lib/context.ts` is gone and a device drops its `kreators.appUse` visit times. "Fits you" still marks an app and leads the hidden ones. Rewards live under Profile, not as an app.
- **Every app screen** opens with `BackLink` (or `PageHeader`). It pops one history entry when the tab has one, and otherwise goes up to its parent. `Root` renders `ScrollRestoration`, so a new page starts at the top and Back returns where you were. Screen state that must survive Back lives in the URL through `lib/useParamState.ts`.
- **App headers.** An app whose header has a helper line opens with a cover card (`AppCover`): the back button top left, the title and helper line bottom right, the actions on the helper's line, over the app's own drawn art. An app with a number about the person opens instead with a plain header and a coloured `Hero` holding that number (Life, Sleep, Journal, Stress, Wellbeing, Companion, Coaching, Games). A screen never gets both. The main tabs and headers with no helper line stay plain. Profile wears the cover left-aligned.
- **The privacy card.** Until a person has answered, a card asks once (`ConsentPopup`, mounted in `Root`). See 11.
- **Search** is inline (`SearchBox`): apps and help from the client, everything else from `GET /search`, grouped under the field as you type, never a new page. Its border lights on focus, white on a dark header. A goo layer pours the results panel out of the field (none under reduced motion, reduced transparency or more contrast).
- **Dark mode** keeps the seven hues and turns the surfaces inside out. Each app's dark mode leans the page wash toward its own hue (`data-accent`, set by `Gate`). The theme switch (Auto, Light, Dark) is on every page, including onboarding and the public footer.

## 3. Home and dashboards

Home is the landing screen and the one dashboard, in one call (`GET /home`). Its header is always the night slate (`.night-card`), in both modes. The greeting is a Large Title, wraps and is never clipped. Its page and inbox buttons sit on their own first row. Under it: Breathe and focus while Mindfulness is on, the daily brief (`GET /home/brief`, three signals and three next steps from rules, never a model), the day's three boxes, "Getting started" for the first 14 days (one small toggle that can be folded), Water and reminders with the line of the day under it, the metrics, the game's views, the Emerging block, the "By period" panel, the book shelf and Go to, picture tiles into every app that is shown. Every widget on Home belongs to its app and shows only while that app is on (`isOn` in Home.tsx); Home's numbers, Daily, the pillars, xp, the brief, Emerging, water and Books stay.

The metrics are a bento: a large Life score beside two stacked cards from the laptop width up, and a sideways row on a phone. "Xp, last 14 days" is one full-width section with a `Segmented` By day or By source (`?xp=source`). The half-width sections pair up (Life, Knowledge and Money are the optional ones), and when an odd number is on the last takes the whole row. Your pillars is always full width.

The game's numbers are its results and come first on purpose; every card under them is an action. Numbers come before actions on any dashboard. Home is the only screen that flows into two columns, from `lg`, and its halves are matched (see 11). Every other screen is one full-width column (`Screen` defaults to `columns={false}`).

Every dashboard carries the same "By period" block (`PeriodPanel`: `PeriodFilter` plus `SummaryTiles`): Home, Life, Finance, Wellbeing, Mood, Sleep, Stress, Mindfulness, Journal and the Shop. Agency has its own numbers-first overview and a dashboard for each client.

The Emerging block sits after a screen's numbers and before How it works, on Home (top three, See all, or one quiet line when nothing is ready), Daily, Pillars, each pillar page, Fitness and the tracker apps. It downloads only when there is a line ready. A card is the number, a `StatusTag` in the corner, one sentence, a small chart, the basis line, one outline action and a More menu. Daily also has a Habits section under Not to do: short rows, up to five, with Good habit or Bad habit in the corner.

## 4. Tokens

Everything is a CSS variable on `:root`, defined in the `@theme` block of `app.css` and redefined for dark mode. Never hard-code a colour or a spacing value in a screen. `scripts/scan.sh` fails on a hex colour outside the drawn art, a numeric Tailwind spacing class, an arbitrary pixel value, white on the ink fill, raw spacing in `app.css`, `font-extrabold` outside printed paper, an `<img>` without `alt`, `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, or the old product name.

- **Spacing:** six values only: xs 8, sm 12, md 16, lg 32, xl 64, 2xl 120 (`--spacing-xs` to `--spacing-2xl`). A hairline under 8px between icons and the sidebar width are the accepted exceptions.
- **Ink:** a cool slate, not grey or brown. `--ink` is `#25293a` in light mode (soft `#444a5f`, mute `#565c72`); dark mode is `#eef0f8` (soft `#d6dbe9`, mute `#c0c7db`). `ink-fixed` stays dark in both modes. Text in soft and mute holds 4.5:1 over the lightest and darkest places the page wash reaches.
- **The page:** never a flat fill. `body::before` draws a soft wash of four glows (`--glow-1` to `--glow-4`) over `--page-base-a` to `--page-base-b`. Light mode is periwinkle, seafoam, blush and lavender (`#eceffd` to `#e2f2ee`). Dark mode is deep indigo and teal, made darker on 4 November: the base runs `#07090f` to `#080e14`. The wash fades into the base over its last stretch, so a long page shows no hard line.
- **Surfaces:** `--paper` and `--card` are translucent glass. Only the big surfaces blur (`.bg-card` with `rounded-card` or `rounded-tile`, the nav, dialogs; `--glass-blur` 14px light and 16px dark, saturate 1.3). Small chips do not. `--card-strong` (`bg-card-strong`, white at 97% in light mode, `rgba(15, 18, 30, .985)` in dark) is the card that must stay readable whatever is under it: dialogs, nav, tooltips, buttons on a coloured fill. A `.bg-card` inside a coloured or ink fill turns solid by CSS. The glass turns solid with `prefers-reduced-transparency`, `prefers-contrast: more` and where `backdrop-filter` is missing.
- **Matte finish:** a solid surface (an ink or coloured card, the night slate, a strong card) wears a faint grey grain (`--matte-grain`, about 4%) over `--matte-sheen` or its own gradient. It is gone under reduced transparency and more contrast, and never on printed paper. Controls keep their plain look at rest.
- **On-fill tokens:** `on-fill` (white on any solid relationship colour), `on-yellow` (dark on yellow and orange), `on-ink` (the foreground on the ink fill, which turns cream in dark mode).
- **The seven relationship colours** `r1` to `r7`, calm and mirrored in `RELATIONSHIPS` in shared: Servitude `#C2375B` red (Ember, guide to servitude), Friends `#F08A4B` orange (Pip, guide to friends), Family `#F3CF5A` yellow (Sunny, guide to family), Love `#42714A` green (Clover, guide to love), Yourself `#2A6FAA` sky blue (Dew, guide to yourself), Teachers `#4C57C7` indigo (Sage, guide to teachers), Faith `#5A3FA6` deep purple (Lumen, guide to faith). Red, green and sky blue hold 4.5:1 under white text, even faded to 90 percent. The old brown is no pillar's colour; it stays as `--color-earth` (`#8C6A57`) for the footer's ground band. Each colour has a tint (`--tint-*`, `--soft-*`); in dark mode a tint becomes the deep end of its own family, never grey.
- **Coloured fills are gradients** (`Hero`, `MetricCard`, `.bg-r1` to `.bg-r7`, `.bg-ink`; `--fill-shade` is where they deepen to), never flat. A coloured fill in a screen takes its colour from the tokens (`lib/relFill.ts`).
- **Type: one family, Google Sans Flex.** It is self-hosted as two woff2 subsets in `apps/web/public/fonts`: latin (about 72 kB, the one font preloaded in `index.html`) and latin-ext (which holds the rupee sign). Two axes are kept: weight 300 to 800 and roundness (ROND). SIL Open Font License, listed in `LICENSE.txt` and on `/licences`. `--font-display`, `--font-body` and `--font-mono` all name it, so each role can still be tuned. No third party sees a visitor on page load. There is no serif on a screen. The face is plain (ROND 0) everywhere except the two wordmarks, which are fully rounded (ROND 100): the logo at the top (`BrandMark`, weight 700) and the big fluted wordmark in the public footer. The one other face is Caveat, kept only for a guide's handwritten line on the public site (`.guide-hand`, `site/GuideLine.tsx`).

- **Tracking and leading** are tight, after umbrel's measured curve (5 November, the founder: "check for line heights and kernings to be tighter"): −0.03em at 64 to −0.015em at 20, just under zero down to 13px, zero at 12 and +0.006em at 11; titles lead at about 1.1 to 1.2, display at 1.02 to 1.1, reading text keeps 17/22 and prose 16/23. Kerning, `liga` and `calt` stay on. `optimizeLegibility` is used only on large type. Dark surfaces take grayscale smoothing, so light text does not bloom.
- **Weights follow umbrel and the surface** (5 November, the founder: "the same thickness/boldness style as in umbrel.com", then "the fonts are so much more bolder, but yours are not"). Measured in Chrome: at the same number Flex's strokes equal umbrel's; umbrel reads bolder because it is white on black with macOS smoothing on, and dark ink on a light page reads about 50 weight units lighter. So smoothing stays on everywhere and the weights keep one order, heading over label over tag over reading. The table's weights are the light ones: reading 450 at every size, tags `font-medium` 480, labels and card titles `font-semibold` 550, titles `font-bold` 600, display and figures 550, eyebrows 500. On a dark surface every one is 50 lighter, with reading at umbrel's own 400. The dark surfaces are dark mode, the night slate, the Mindfulness panel, white text on a fill, the fractal glass, and the ink fill in light mode; the orange and yellow fills keep the light weights. the scan still refuses `font-extrabold` outside paper. The site's header links and the footer's policy links take the reading weight. Nothing is under 400. The two rounded wordmarks, the guides' Caveat lines and printed paper keep their own weights (paper's text styles are pinned to the old ones).
- **Type rules on a screen.** A screen uses a role, never a px font size on text. Emphasis is `font-semibold` on a role, never `font-bold`. Text in `ink-soft` or `ink-mute` is Regular; the colour makes it secondary. A link drawn as a button is semibold like `Button`. The public site's card and section headings sit at 480 under its 500 hooks. A selected state never changes weight. Hierarchy comes from size and ink colour first. On the public site the second half of a headline is the quiet half (`.type-hook em`, upright, 400, in `--color-ink-mute`, large type only). Left as they were: printed paper (its weights pinned on its own roots), the two rounded wordmarks, the guides' Caveat lines, chart SVG text, the card table's scaled text, the Mindfulness timer, ruler and activity grid sizes, and icon glyph sizes.
- **Radii, concentric the way Apple draws them:** `--radius-card` 32px, `--radius-tile` 24px, `rounded-inner` 16px for a tile inside a card with 16px of padding, `rounded-inner-sm` 20px with 12px of padding, and `pill`. Continuous corners (`corner-shape: squircle`) where the browser draws them.
- **Shadows:** `--shadow-toy` and `--shadow-toy-soft`, the only two, each a hairline edge, an inner top highlight and a soft drop. No new shadow values.
- **Motion:** GSAP for anything new; `motion/react` only where it already is, inside one `LazyMotion` (`m as motion`). Arrivals use `--ease-open` (`cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`) on every `toy`. A `.toy` presses to 97% and springs back (`--ease-spring`). Reduced motion is honoured with `gsap.set`. See 11, Motion kit.

## 5. The contrast rule

On a solid colour fill (`bg-r1` to `bg-r7`, a `Hero`) every foreground element is white: `text-on-fill`, rings and lines in `var(--color-on-fill)` with an `rgba(255,255,255,0.3)` track, buttons `variant="light"`. Yellow takes `text-on-yellow`, and so does orange, whose foreground is the dark slate (white on the orange fill is only 2.5:1). The rule is in CSS, so a fill set by the class, by `var(--color-r2)` or by the colour the shared model carries all turn their foreground with it. `bg-ink` turns cream in dark mode, so everything on it uses the `on-ink` tokens, never `text-white` or `bg-white`. Dark text and ink buttons sit only on cream, card and tint surfaces. Never a brown ring, brown text or an ink button on a coloured card. In a row of equal choices no button is solid until it is picked.

The target is WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for text under 24px (or under 18.66px bold), 3:1 for larger text, 3:1 for the keyboard focus ring. `scripts/smoke/contrast-probe.js` walks every text node and placeholder and composites its colour onto the real background, at the worst place on the page wash the text can scroll to. It reads a gradient fill's first colour stop. A disabled control is exempt in WCAG and is listed only under 3:1. It also flags fixed or sticky chrome that carries text on a fill under 88 percent opaque. Against AA it first found 3,375 hits (1 November); the last full pass on 5 November came back clean over 100 routes. It still misreads two places, which were checked on screen: the sidebar's active tab (it does not see the sliding pill) and the white labels inside the life map's funnel (SVG fills). It cannot see blurred layers either, so a hero over `MorphShapes` or `FractalGlass` is checked by sampling pixels from screenshots. Text in red on the light page takes the deep red, because the fill red is 4.4:1 on glass.

## 7. States

Every screen is driven by TanStack Query, so loading, error and empty are handled per section: a short "nothing yet" line with the action that fills it, an error line with retry, the `Gate` screen for a module that is off or unavailable, and a 404-shaped answer for a thing you cannot see. An empty chart is one short line, never a tall empty square.

Destructive actions ask first in an in-app `Sheet` with a red yes and an outline Keep. Two older `window.confirm` calls remain (Resources and a pyramid's remove in Realm). Account deletion also needs the word DELETE typed.

Skeletons are used where a whole screen or list waits: `Skeleton`, `SkeletonCard`, `SkeletonList` and `Busy` (`components/Skeleton.tsx`: a quiet pulsing block that stops under reduced motion, hidden from screen readers, with one "Loading" read out by the wrapper). `RouteFallback` shows while a lazy screen downloads and keeps the page frame, so the real screen lands in the same place. If a lazy screen fails to download, the page reloads once and then shows one plain line. Sections still render their frame (a chart's axes, a tile's label) before data lands.

A public client link that does not open says why (`LinkTrouble`): "No invoice at this link" (or document, and so on), "This link was closed" with who to ask, or "Something went wrong on our side".

Keyboard focus shows only from the keyboard (`focus-visible`), as a 3px ring with a 3px offset in the system's focus blue (`--deep-self`: deep sky, lighter in dark mode). It is never ink, because a dark ring beside a solid ink pill read as a second picked pill. A mouse or a finger never draws the ring.

## 9. Accessibility, with status

- Aria labels and roles throughout; headings in order; labels on fields; a toggle is a `role="switch"`; a tab or a view switch carries `aria-pressed`; an inline toggle carries `aria-expanded`.
- Reduced motion honoured in GSAP and the few `motion/react` places. Reduced transparency and more contrast turn glass solid and drop the matte grain.
- Contrast probed over every route in dark and light mode; see 5.
- Phone layout probed over every route (`scripts/smoke/overflow-probe.js` through the screenshot tool): no page scrolls sideways and nothing pokes past the screen, in portrait or landscape. A class may not carry `inline-flex` or `inline-block` next to `hidden`, because Tailwind 4 orders those after `hidden`.
- **Text size.** Every role is in rem and `html` is 100%, so the browser's text size setting applies.
- **Skip link.** `SkipLink` is the first stop on every app screen: out of sight until it has focus, then at the top left. It moves focus to `<main id="main" tabIndex={-1}>` and scrolls to it. `scroll-padding` (80px above, 120px below) keeps the tab bar, the assistant's button and sticky chrome from covering a control the keyboard scrolls to.
- **Popups.** A popup's Close and small round icon actions sit in a rail outside its card, in reach however far the card scrolls (`rail` on `Sheet`; the founder, 7 November). On a tablet or laptop the rail is one small gap off the card's right edge, Close first; on a phone it is above the card's top edge at the right. The assistant's is on the card's left on a laptop, and the Reminders bubble's on its right. A word button or a full-width action stays in the popup, and an inline panel on a page keeps its own close.
- **Dialogs.** `useDialogFocus` (`lib/useDialogFocus.ts`) is the one keyboard rule for every dialog. Focus moves in when it opens (the control marked `data-autofocus`, else the first control, else the box). Tab and Shift+Tab wrap at its ends; a modal one such as `Sheet` also pulls stray focus back in. Escape closes the top one only, and focus returns to the control that opened it. The assistant and the reminders panel use it as non-modal panels.
- **Pictures and tables.** `scripts/scan.sh` fails on any `<img>` without an `alt` (a decorative picture has `alt=""`). Decorative art is `aria-hidden`. The `DataTable` rows can be used from the keyboard. A legal table is real table markup (see 11).
- **Targets.** Every tap target is at least 44 by 44. `scripts/smoke/target-probe.js` reports 0 on 100 routes.
- Keyboard: focus-visible rings, disclosure rows that open from the keyboard, the kanban board's Move button and sheet, the card table's keys to flip and move cards, the Mindfulness keys on a laptop (Space, S, K, ?).
- The target is WCAG 2.2 AA for colour (see 5). Not done: no screen-reader pass has been made; the drawn characters are decorative by design.

## 10. Workflow

The kit came first: tokens were pulled once from Figma (`scripts/figma-pull.py`, `scripts/figma-sections.py`) and frozen, so builds never call Figma. The look was then redrawn in `app.css`; the tokens there are the truth now. A new screen reuses `PageHeader`, `Section`, `StatTile`, the charts, the rows and `RowCard`. It never invents a style.

Screenshots come from `node scripts/smoke/shoot.mjs [routes]` against the smoke server, in light, dark or both, at phone, tablet and laptop widths and a phone on its side. `shots-all.mjs` writes the shot list for about a hundred routes. Each probe runs as a `SHOOT_PROBE` step over that list:

- `contrast-probe.js`: text under AA (see 5). Run it in dark mode after touching colours.
- `overflow-probe.js`: a page that scrolls sideways, or an element past the screen. 0 at phone width.
- `orphan-probe.js`: a block of text that ends on one word. 0 on a laptop.
- `rag-probe.js`: a line under 80% of the widest line above it since the last break made on purpose (the rectangle rule). Run at 360, 390 and 1280.
- `lone-probe.js`: a pill alone on its line. 0 at 360, 390, 768 and 1280, both themes.
- `bento-probe.js`: a row of a tile grid that stops short of the full width, a row whose tiles are not one width, and a row of one beside rows of three or more, and a grid of more than 12 tiles with no `data-tile-list`. 0 at 360, 390, 768 and 1280 in both themes.
- `target-probe.js`: a tap target under 44 by 44. 0.
- `cut-probe.js`: a one-line row of tabs or pills that scrolls or cuts a pill. 0 at 360 and 390.
- `height-probe.js`: a row of sibling cards of unequal height. 0 at phone, tablet and laptop.
- `box-probe.js`: a control that draws a shadow, fill or edge with square corners. 0 with no sheet open.
- `density-probe.js`: copy too heavy for a phone. Hits that stay are reading pages, said so.

The drive scripts check what a still shot cannot: `drive-home.mjs` (Home's halves), `drive-headings.mjs` (a section's action level with its title), `drive-scroll.mjs` (a tab or toggle keeps the page where it is), `drive-plan.mjs` (Plan my week rows), `drive-cards.mjs` (the card table), `drive-signup.mjs`, `drive-keep.mjs` and `drive-talk.mjs`. At the final gate of 5 November, over 100 routes at 360, 390 and 1280 in both themes: overflow, lone, height, cut and box 0 everywhere; target 0 at 390 and 1280 (12 at 360: the strategy session's seven pillar buttons at 40 by 48 and five Journal calendar days at 41 by 44); orphan 0 at 390 and 1280; contrast only the known misreads (the life map funnel's white labels and the sidebar's active tab); rag 6 or 7 at 390, 4 at 1280 and about 20 at 360, on test data, policy text a hair under 80% and narrow labels; density 14 to 16 report-only hits (the public home, Profile, Help, Circles, the Store, Observability, test notifications). drive-home, drive-headings and drive-scroll report 0.

## 11. Patterns added since 0.3

- **Status tags.** Every status is a `StatusTag`: a dot and a word on a pale wash, never colour alone. The tones (`TONE`: neutral, info, good, warn, bad, and calm, warm, deep for facts) come from the maps in shared (`DELIVERABLE_STAGE_TONE`, `CLIENT_STATUS_TONE`, `INVOICE_STATUS_TONE`, `CONTRACT_STATUS_TONE` and the rest).
- **List or board.** A pipeline (clients, deliverables, invoices, agreements) switches between a list and a kanban board with `ViewToggle`; the choice lives in the URL through `useView`. A board column is a page of its own with a count and "Show more". Columns are a fixed 260px (280 from `md`) in a `.scroll-row`, with a fade on the edge that has more. With no view in the URL a phone starts on the list. Cards move by a fluid drag: a mouse lifts after 6px, a finger after a 250ms hold so a swipe still scrolls. A dashed slot stays behind, a copy follows and tilts with the speed, and an allowed lane opens a gap while its cards slide. The 44px Move button opens a sheet of the columns the card may go to; that is the keyboard and touch way. The agreements board puts Move at the card's foot (`moveAtFoot`), so a long title keeps the card's width.
- **Paper is edited in place.** An agreement, another document, an invoice, a house template and Agency settings' business profile are typed on the page that prints. Every heading, paragraph, bullet, table cell and blank is a plain-text editable region. The page saves as you go, with Saved and Undo, and a version guard reloads it if another tab saved first. A document page shows back, the title, one `StatusTag`, one main action for its state, Download and one More menu (4 visible buttons on a draft at 390 and 1440). "Fill in the details" is one sheet listing every blank, grouped by where each is kept. There are no editor sheets and no stack of summary cards.
- **Fold, not a bare details.** A fold-out is `components/Fold.tsx`: a rounded flat button with a chevron, tinted while open. With `adds` (a fold that opens a form to add something) the mark is a plus that turns into a cross while open. `tone="plain"` has no fill until hovered or open, for the laptop sidebar's groups. A browser's own `<details>` is not used.
- **Inline toggles show their state.** A toggle that opens or folds something in place carries `aria-expanded` and a mark from `ToggleMark`: a chevron that turns half a turn, or an icon that turns into a cross (Daily's task pencil, whose label then says "Close the plan for"). Opacity and a turn only, 200ms on `--ease-open`, a plain swap under reduced motion. A sheet, dialog or menu keeps its own close.
- **A tab or a toggle keeps the page where it is.** Anything that only changes what is shown passes `preventScrollReset: true` when it changes the address (`useParamState` does it for all of them). Only a new page starts at the top. `drive-scroll.mjs` checks it.
- **Text is a rectangle (5 November).** No line of a heading, card, list line, button or paragraph is noticeably shorter than the widest line above it, on a laptop or a phone (the founder: "keep all text boxes look like rectangles, not long lines followed by short one"). The shapes allowed are lines about equal, lines that grow going down, and a line broken on purpose after a comma or a full stop. `html` carries `text-wrap: balance` for everything, long reading text too (`pretty` is only the `@supports not (text-wrap: balance)` fallback; `text-pretty` is no longer used on a screen). Where the browser cannot balance (over about six lines, or words that fall unevenly), `lib/rectText.ts` pads the side the text runs out to a little (never past 15% of the width), or holds the first line shorter so the lines grow (never under half the line below it, `FIRST_FLOOR` 0.5, so no stub is left), or adds one line. It keeps the box's outer size, runs again on a resize, a theme or a page change (never for a change only inside `[data-rect-quiet]`, the ticking focus clocks), and leaves printed paper, editable text and code alone. Where words cannot make a rectangle, they break on purpose (`inline-block` parts after a comma or full stop), take plainer copy or get more room; never a hard-coded width. A line clamp switches balancing off, so it is not used on text that can wrap. `tie()` in `lib/text.ts` still keeps a short title's last two words together. `rag-probe.js` measures each line against the widest line above it since the last break made on purpose; `orphan-probe.js` still checks for a lone last word.
- **Print and save as PDF.** Every `@media print` rule is in one section at the end of `app.css`. A document (`.legal-doc`) is always white paper with dark ink, in dark mode too, and only the sheet prints. "Download" is the browser's print and save, with the title set to the number or the agreement's name; the server makes no PDF. An invoice prints with its own colours; agreements and other documents print plain black on white. Daily's month calendar prints as its own landscape sheet.
- **Even rows (2026-11-07, the founder: "evenly distributed to maintain full widths").** Every grid of tiles or cards takes even rows: `evenGrid(count, cols)` on the grid and `evenTile(i, count, cols)` on each tile, from `lib/bento.ts`. `cols` is the column count at each step (`{ base: 2, md: 4 }`) or a container's own (`{ base: 2, '@lg': 3, '@3xl': 4 }`), never both. The one rule is `evenRows`: the fewest rows that hold the tiles, shared so two rows differ by at most one, the shorter rows first, every tile in a row one width, and every row the grid's full width. So 6 in 4 columns is 3 + 3, 5 is 2 + 3 and 7 is 3 + 4. Only two columns can leave a row of one, an odd count's lead, which takes the row. It replaced `bento()`, which left two widths in a row. A list whose count changes takes the rule too. Fields of a form and a picker whose columns line up are not tiles. `bento-probe.js` measures it.
- **Categories past ten (2026-11-07, the founder).** Past ten tiles a block reads in categories through `components/TileGroups.tsx`: a category's name in the quiet eyebrow, then its own even rows. A category with nothing to show goes, and at or under ten it stays one even block with no heading. Home's By period alone stays one even block past ten (`whole`). On a laptop short categories that fit share a line, each taking its tile count of the columns, so every tile on the line is one width. Reviews, Home and Life use Tasks and days, the five areas of life in their one order, Work and learning, People. A paged or person-made list stays whole and says so with `data-tile-list`.
- **Bento.** `.bento` with `.bento-w2`, `.bento-w3`, `.bento-w4` and `.bento-h2` sit on a four-column grid (one column on a phone), with one gap and one radius. The most important cell is the biggest (`.bento-feature`). `BENTO_SIZE` names large, medium, wide, tall and small. Cells arrive once by a scroll-driven animation where the browser has it, and only without reduced motion. A mouse gets a tinted outline on hover, never a new shadow.
- **App tiles (2026-11-07, `components/AppTile.tsx`, `.app-tile`, the `--app-tile-<pillar>` tokens).** Home's Go to. On a phone and a phone on its side each group is one sideways `.scroll-row` of 145px tiles with proximity snap (a group of one or two grows to fill the row). From `md` it is a grid by `GO_COLS` (three, four from `lg`, five from `xl`) through `evenGrid` and `evenTile`, 168px tall. Each tile is one `toy` link: its name in `text-headline` at the top left over a bright graded matte fill in the group's colour under dark ink in both themes, and a picture at the bottom right, 124px, 8px over both edges, in the flow under the name so it can never cover it. A picture is the module's key (`public/app-art/<key>.webp`), one that fails gives way to the app's `AppIcon`, and `scripts/app-art/build.mjs` is the one list. The pictures are Microsoft's Fluent Emoji 3D renders (MIT, credited on `/licences`), the one exception to "lucide, never an emoji", on these tiles only. **Hover:** under a mouse a tile deepens and its name turns white (the founder's own exception to "never a new filled hover"). White needs a fill at 4.5:1, so each hue goes to the lightest shade that holds it (`--app-tile-hover-<pillar>`); yellow leans to amber, since a straight dark yellow reads olive. The fill is a layer that fades in, the weight never changes, and the toy's 3% brighter hover is off on these tiles.
- **The red yes (2026-11-07, the founder: "red yes can be solid on hover or second confirmation tap").** One look everywhere: `RED_YES` and `RED_YES_LG` in `lib/ui.ts` over `.red-yes` in `app.css`. It is the pale red outline at rest, and solid red with white words (5.26:1 in both themes, weight unchanged) on a mouse hover (only under `(hover: hover)`, so a touch never leaves it stuck), on keyboard focus and while pressed. A second-tap control keeps its own look at rest and wears `.red-armed`, the same solid red, only while it asks. `DELETE_BTN` stays the pale button that opens a question, with its faint wash on hover.
- **Confirmations (2026-11-06).** Every destructive action asks first, never in one tap and never with `window.confirm`, in one of two ways. What closes something or is highly destructive or cannot be undone asks in a `Sheet` (`ConfirmDelete`) with a red yes in its own words and an outline Keep. A small, recoverable remove is a second tap: the label turns to "Tap again to …", and it disarms after about four seconds. A popup holds only what needs one; short content lives in the card it comes from as a `Fold`, an in-place edit or a panel under its trigger.
- **The card rule (5 November).** A card in a row that can be stretched ends in one foot (`mt-auto`): its tags or state on the left, its action or arrow on the right, so the feet line up. A tag that would sit on a row of its own goes in the card's top right corner if that corner serves nothing else (`CardHead`). A chart or an empty line takes the leftover room and sits in its middle. A mark one item has, every item has (each badge tile ends in a bar; each tile in a row carries a hint). A hero's word goes under its gauge on a phone, never a sliver beside it. A time stays whole. A list cell that holds one `toy` card stretches it, and kanban lanes stretch to the tallest.
- **Link arrows.** A link's arrow is lucide's `ArrowUpRight`, the 45-degree arrow, everywhere: `SiteArrow`, the read arrows on cards, the end of link rows, `SeeAll`, the round Open buttons, menu items that open a link. Its hover nudge goes up and right (`.read-arrow`). Toggles keep their chevron or cross. Buttons that open a sheet, steppers, arrows between items in a sequence and the chat's next button keep their own arrows.
- **Sounds.** `lib/sound.ts` makes every sound in the browser (soft beats, chimes, breath cues, rain, ocean, wind, a soft hum and Lo-fi). The master switch silences all of them, the volume reaches a sound that is already playing, and the preview follows both. Checked in a real browser, since a type check cannot hear.
- **One style for the link at the top right of a section.** Every link level with a section's title is `SeeAll` (in `components/Section.tsx`): 15px muted text at the label weight, `ArrowUpRight` after it, a 44px tap area, a router `Link`. The action is level with the first line of the title, never down by the line under it. `drive-headings.mjs` measures every heading with an action and fails a link action without its arrow.
- **On this page (2026-11-07, `components/PageContents.tsx`, `.page-contents` in `app.css`).** On a laptop, one button pinned at the top right under the top bar: a stack of 2px dashes, one per heading, the one being read darker and longer. Enter or a tap opens the card instead; a row is 32px under a mouse and 44px on touch. Headings are read from the page's `main`, never from a list of cards, so no screen lists its own. It needs 76px free beside the column, so an app screen shows it from 1160px wide (the founder confirmed narrower screens stay without it, 2026-11-07) and a phone never downloads it. A page with fewer than two headings shows none, and neither does a page that cannot scroll. It comes right after the skip link in the Tab order, not after the page. `scripts/smoke/drive-contents.mjs` is the check.

## 2026-11-03: icons, one primary action, and the print section (the overhaul)

- **Icons are one family.** Emoji in the interface became icons, except inside text people write and in footer links. Static icons are `lucide-react`, drawn only through `components/Icon.tsx` (one stroke, `ICON_STROKE` 2, `currentColor`, decorative unless given `label`) or `components/AppIcon.tsx` (an icon named by a shared registry key, through `lib/appIcons.ts`). An icon never carries meaning alone: a button with only an icon puts its words in `aria-label`.
- **Animated icons.** `components/AnimatedIcon.tsx` plays useanimations.com's line animations (`react-useanimations`, the player in `vendor-useanimations` and each animation in its own chunk, loaded after the first paint) for 44 names. Among them: menu, searchToX, settings, trash, star, copy, maximizeMinimize (focus mode), loading, the tab bar's home and sun, plusToX (a `Button` with `plusToX` and a `Fold` with `adds`) and heart (Community's Love; Like is a thumbs up). The icon reacts to the button or link it sits in. Each has a still lucide fallback, which shows under reduced motion and before the animation loads.
- **Where emoji stay.** In what people write, in chat text, in footer links, on a reward item, on a card game's cover and on Daily's printed month (the boxes). Shared lists carry an `icon` key instead. A badge's glyph is drawn inside the medal, a mood is a `MoodFace`, a rating is `components/Stars.tsx`.
- **Asking again.** A spend of points asks in `components/ConfirmSpend.tsx`, a sheet with what it costs, one solid button and Cancel. Red items in a More menu ask again in a sheet too.
- **Home's halves stay one height.** A half whose content is shorter centres it instead of leaving an empty band at its foot. Home's header links are 44px tall.
- **Folds everywhere.** Help's questions, the public home's questions, the pillar page's layer list and done tasks, and the laptop sidebar's groups are `Fold`s.
- **The wordmark.** `components/BrandMark.tsx`: kreators.app with the dot in the seven pillar colours (`.brand-dot`), no character, rounded since 5 November; `compact` shows k and the dot under 380px. It is the site header and footer, the sidebar and the phone corner.
- **The characters.** Final as of 2026-11-03: friendly flat monsters with a gradient body (`Avatar`), the seven in the rainbow of their pillars, and Bumling, the app's own guide, a neutral grey with a gold antenna.
- **One tag vocabulary.** A state is a `StatusTag`, a fact on a card a `FactTag` (price yellow with coins, free green with a gift, place orange with a map, online sky with a globe, when indigo with a calendar, then places left, checked, kind, status), any other mark an `IconTag`; the word is always shown.
- **No lone pill.** A wrapping row of pills or buttons fills its lines (`.fill-row` on a phone, `.fill-row-all` everywhere); `lone-probe.js` checks it.
- **India first.** A phone field says a number without a code is read as +91 (`PHONE_HINT`).
- **One copy of the form strings and helpers.** `FIELD`, `FIELD_LINE` and `SELECT_SM` in `lib/ui.ts`; `FieldLabel` in `components/Field.tsx`; `errorText` and `ErrorLine` in `components/ErrorLine.tsx`; `copyPlain` in `lib/clipboard.ts`; `longDate` and `dayLabel` in `lib/date.ts`.
- **Calm public pages for clients.** The client link, the team link and the public invoice, agreement and document use `SiteLayout minimal`.
- **One primary action per view.** The rest of a row's actions sit behind `MoreMenu`. A main action beside a More takes the free width. A line and its action sit left and right. A helper line that says what one button does is that button's centred caption. Finance's two primaries are Money in and Money out; Owed, Monthly, Goal and Budgets are outline buttons below them.
- **Segmented controls, not pill walls.** A choice between a few views is `components/fx/Segmented.tsx` (a sliding thumb). A row of tabs that can outgrow its line is `TabRow`, with the rest behind "All". A row that only picks one value is a `FilterSelect`.
- **Hovers in the pillar's colour.** Buttons keep their look at rest; their hover takes `--tint` and `--tint-ink` from the app's `data-accent`, a pillar page's accent (`lib/usePillarAccent.ts`) or a box's own `data-pillar`. No filled hover is added.
