# 06 — Design system and UX

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

**Product:** kreators.app
**Version:** 0.6, reconciled with the app on 1 November 2026 after the launch pass: legal, accessibility and performance (0.5 was the bug sweep the same day, 0.4 was the morning of 1 November, 0.3 was 26 October 2026)
**Source of truth:** the design rules in `CLAUDE.md`; the `@theme` block and the `:root` tokens in `apps/web/src/app.css`; `apps/web/src/components`; `design/figma-tokens.json` (the freud UI Kit v1.5 values, frozen, now only the starting point: the look was redrawn on 30 and 31 October, and the ink and three fill colours were tuned for contrast on 1 November); `bash scripts/scan.sh`; `python3 -I scripts/flesch.py`; the probes in `scripts/smoke/`; `docs/copy-brief.md` for the public site's words.

## 1. Experience goal

One coherent product, not a directory of microsites. Every screen is built from the same few parts, every tappable thing lifts the same way, every app explains itself the same way at the bottom of its screen, and the whole thing reads like one quiet app. The look since 30 October 2026 is calm glass on a soft gradient, not flat fills. Results first, then what did it, then the one thing to do next.

## 2. Information architecture as built

- **Phone:** one column, five tabs along the bottom: Home, Daily, Pillars, Apps, Profile. The top-left corner holds the kreators.app logo (to Home), the top-right corner holds two buttons, theme on the left and an apps menu on the right (a `Sheet` listing the sidebar's groups). The assistant's round button sits at the bottom right, above the tab bar. The top buttons are `absolute`, not `fixed`: they scroll away with the page. The first header of every screen starts 64px down to clear them. A phone on its side keeps the phone layout (`md` and `lg` need height as well as width).
- **Laptop:** the tabs become a sidebar. The role badge and the theme button sit at the top beside the logo. Enabled apps are listed under their group as collapsible headings that start folded; only the group of the app you are inside opens. Sign out is in the footer. Beside the sidebar the column is `md:w-auto`.
- **Groups and their names (46 modules):** Essentials (Home, Daily, Pillars, Search, Leaderboard, Inbox, Help, Assistant), Contribution (School, Work, Army, Win, Agency), Creation (Public page, Shop, Store, Audience, Content, Auto-DM, Finance, My purchases, Knowledge, Tools, TV, Fund), Connection (Games, Party, Kreators Run, Community, Club, Bond, Circles), Reflection (Discover, Life, Wellbeing, Mood, Sleep, Stress, Mindful minutes, Journal, Reviews, Companion, Coaching), Operations (Manage, Admin, Observability; the team's group, always last). Each group wears a relationship colour (`MODULE_GROUP_ACCENT`).
- **Apps page:** lists only the apps that are on, grouped, each group in its colour. One highlighted ink card at the top, "Turn apps on or off", leads to Profile, Features, the one place every app is switched on or off. Apps are listed by context everywhere (`useModules`, `useGroupOrder`): what is on, then what fits the setup chat's answers, then what the person opens most on this device, then the app's own order. Rank and Rewards are part of the game and live under Profile, not apps.
- **Every app screen** opens with `BackLink` (or `PageHeader`), which pops one history entry when the tab has one and otherwise goes up to its parent. `Root` renders `ScrollRestoration`, so a new page starts at the top and Back returns where you were. Screen state that must survive Back lives in the URL through `lib/useParamState.ts` (Community's category, sort and filter; the Store's category, stage and budget; Run's tab; the pipeline view; a tab on Agency).
- **The privacy card.** Until a person has answered, a card sits at the bottom left (`ConsentPopup`, mounted once in `Root`, so it shows on the public site and in the app). On a laptop it is 400px wide, starts to the right of the 240px sidebar and floats 80px above the bottom edge; on a phone it spans the width less the gutters and sits 80px up (clear of the 72px tab bar, plus the device's safe area), at most 70 percent of the screen tall (or 560px) and scrolls inside itself if it must. It is the strong glass (`bg-card-strong`) with a border, never over the assistant's button at the bottom right. See 11.
- **Search** is inline (`SearchBox`): apps and help from the client, everything else from `GET /search`, grouped under the field as you type, never a new page. Its border lights on focus; white on a dark header.
- **Dark mode** keeps the seven hues and turns the surfaces inside out; each app's dark mode leans the page wash toward its own hue (`data-accent` on the document, set by `Gate`). The theme switch (device, light, dark) is on every page including onboarding.

## 3. Home and dashboards

Home is the landing screen and the one dashboard, in one call (`GET /home`). Its header is always the night slate (`.night-card`), in both modes, so its flower and white text show in dark mode; the greeting wraps and is never clipped, and its page and inbox buttons sit on their own first row. Under it: the daily brief (`GET /home/brief`, three signals and three next steps from rules, never a model), the day's three boxes with the guidance line (`nudgeFor`), "Getting started" for the first 14 days (one small toggle that can be folded for good), Water and reminders with the line of the day under it, the metric tiles, the game's own views, the "By period" panel, and the shortcuts into every app that is on. The half-width sections (Life, Knowledge and Money are the optional ones) pair up, and when an odd number is on the last takes the whole row; Your pillars is always full width.

The v0.1 warning against vanity scores stands, with this reading: the game's numbers are its results and come first on purpose; every card under them is an action. Numbers come before actions on any dashboard. Home is the only screen that flows into two columns on a laptop, and its halves are matched (see 11); every other screen is one full-width column (`Screen` defaults to `columns={false}`).

Every dashboard carries the same "By period" block (`PeriodPanel`: `PeriodFilter` plus `SummaryTiles`): Home, Life, Finance, Wellbeing, Mood, Sleep, Stress, Mindful, Journal and the Shop. Agency has its own numbers-first overview and a dashboard for each client.

## 4. Tokens

Everything is a CSS variable on `:root`, defined in the `@theme` block of `app.css` and redefined for dark mode. Never hard-code a colour or a spacing value in a screen; `scripts/scan.sh` fails on a hex colour outside the drawn art, a numeric Tailwind spacing class, an arbitrary pixel value, white on the ink fill, raw spacing in `app.css`, `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, or the old product name.

- **Spacing:** six values only: xs 8, sm 12, md 16, lg 32, xl 64, 2xl 120 (`--spacing-xs` to `--spacing-2xl`). A hairline under 8px between icons and the 240px sidebar offset are the accepted exceptions.
- **Ink:** a cool slate, not grey or brown. `--ink` is `#25293a` in light mode (soft `#444a5f`, mute `#565c72`); dark mode is `#eef0f8` (soft `#d6dbe9`, mute `#c0c7db`). `ink-fixed` stays dark in both modes. Soft and mute were darkened (light) and lightened (dark) on 1 November so that text in them holds 4.5:1 over the lightest and the darkest place the page wash reaches (soft 6.2:1 or better, mute 4.6:1 or better in light mode); the dark wash was dimmed for the same reason.
- **The page:** never a flat fill. `body::before` draws a soft wash of four glows (`--glow-1` to `--glow-4`) over `--page-base-a` to `--page-base-b`: periwinkle, seafoam, blush and lavender in light mode, deep indigo and teal in dark mode.
- **Surfaces:** `--paper` and `--card` are translucent glass. Only the big surfaces blur (`.bg-card` with `rounded-card` or `rounded-tile`, the nav, dialogs; `--glass-blur` 14px light and 16px dark, saturate 1.3); small chips do not. `--card-strong` (`bg-card-strong`) is the card that must stay readable whatever is under it: dialogs, nav, tooltips, buttons on a coloured fill; a `.bg-card` inside a coloured or ink fill turns solid by CSS. The glass turns solid with `prefers-reduced-transparency`, `prefers-contrast: more` and where `backdrop-filter` is missing.
- **On-fill tokens:** `on-fill` (white on any solid relationship colour), `on-yellow` (the one exception, dark on yellow), `on-ink` (the foreground on the ink fill, which turns cream in dark mode).
- **The seven relationship colours** `r1` to `r7`, calm and mirrored in `RELATIONSHIPS` in shared: Servant leader `#C2375B` red (Ember, guide to work and money), Friendship `#F08A4B` orange (Pip), Parent and child `#F3CF5A` yellow (Sunny), Lover and partner `#42714A` green (Clover), With yourself `#2A6FAA` sky blue (Dew), With the guru `#4C57C7` indigo (Sage), With the divine `#5A3FA6` deep purple (Lumen). Red, green and sky blue were taken a step deeper on 1 November so white text on them holds 4.5:1 (even faded to 90 percent); `RELATIONSHIPS` in shared carries the same values. The old brown is no pillar's colour now; it stays as `--color-earth` (`#8C6A57`) for the public footer's ground. Each colour has a tint (`--tint-*`, `--soft-*`); in dark mode a tint becomes the deep end of its own family, never grey.
- **Coloured fills are gradients** (`Hero`, `MetricCard`, `.bg-r1` to `.bg-r7`, `.bg-ink`; `--fill-shade` is where they deepen to), never flat.
- **Type:** three faces, each with one job: Urbanist for reading and every control (`--font-display`, `--font-body`), Fraunces as the serif hook only for the big headlines on the public site, JetBrains Mono for eyebrows and numbers. All three are served from this site as variable woff2 files in `apps/web/public/fonts` (latin first, then latin-ext, `font-display: swap`; SIL Open Font License, `LICENSE.txt` beside them), so no third party sees a visitor on page load. On the public site the second half of a headline is the quiet half (`.type-hook em` in `--color-ink-mute`, large type only).
- **Radii, concentric the way Apple draws them:** `--radius-card` 32px, `--radius-tile` 24px, `rounded-inner` 16px for a tile inside a card with 16px of padding, `rounded-inner-sm` 20px with 12px of padding, and `pill`. Continuous corners (`corner-shape: squircle`) where the browser draws them.
- **Shadows:** `--shadow-toy` and `--shadow-toy-soft`, the only two, each a hairline edge plus a soft drop. No new shadow values.
- **Motion:** GSAP for anything new; `motion/react` only where it already is. Arrivals use `--ease-open` (`cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`) on every `toy`. Reduced motion is honoured with `gsap.set`.

## 5. The contrast rule

On a solid colour fill (`bg-r1` to `bg-r7`, a `Hero`) every foreground element is white: `text-on-fill`, rings and lines in `var(--color-on-fill)` with an `rgba(255,255,255,0.3)` track, buttons `variant="light"`. Yellow takes `text-on-yellow`, and so does orange, whose foreground is the dark slate (white on the orange fill is only 2.5:1; the rule is in CSS, so a fill set by the class `.bg-r2`, by `var(--color-r2)` or by the colour the shared model carries all turn their foreground with it). `bg-ink` turns cream in dark mode, so everything on it uses the `on-ink` tokens, never `text-white` or `bg-white`. Dark text and ink buttons sit only on cream, card and tint surfaces. Never a brown ring, brown text or an ink button on a coloured card. In a row of equal choices no button is solid until it is picked.

The target is WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for text under 24px (or under 18.66px bold), 3:1 for larger text, 3:1 for the keyboard focus ring. `scripts/smoke/contrast-probe.js` walks every text node and placeholder, composites its colour onto the real background (at the worst place on the page wash that the text can scroll to) and lists anything under that line; a disabled control is exempt in WCAG and is listed only under 3:1. It also flags fixed or sticky chrome that carries text on a fill under 88 percent opaque. It used to report only under 2.2:1; against AA it first found 3,375 hits and now finds 30, nearly all of them the drawn art of the consciousness map and a few 12px lines, which are the accepted exceptions together with greyed unearned badges. Text in red on the light page takes the deep red (8:1) because the fill red is 4.4:1 on glass.

Badges: `BadgeArt` draws a medal (shape from the family, finish from the tier in the `--badge-*` tokens in app.css with dark values, a padlock when locked, a one-time GSAP shine for a badge just earned, `gsap.set` under reduced motion; sizes 40, 64 and 120) and `BadgeGallery` lists them in bento tiles under Profile.

Every tracker screen shows a thirty-day line. `MoodFace` draws moods; never system emoji. `Avatar` and `Person` draw the characters; `EvolutionReveal` and `Celebration` animate a stage.
- Status and pipelines: `StatusTag`, `ViewToggle` with `useView`, `KanbanBoard` (see 11).
- Planning: `WeekPlan` (the Plan my week sheet), `DayBlocks` (time blocks), `PaceTag` (a project's pace), `ReviewCalendar`, `FocusRounds`, `FocusDashboard`, `SoundControls`.
- Documents: `InvoiceView`, `SummaryCard` (in `InvoiceView.tsx`), `ContractView`, `ClientLink`, `AgencyPipeline`, `AgencyInvoices`, `AgencyContracts`.
- Teaching: `Pyramid` (the one explainer, GSAP: a tick lands on the tip and a glow runs to the base), `HowTo` and `HowItWorks` (the "How it works" card every app's main screen ends with: one `toy toy-soft` button, closed by default, with the steps and the words as two `toy-flat` toggles inside, opened with GSAP; `span-all` on Home; never a floating button or a sheet), `Nudge` (one quiet 12px line under the thing it talks about, linking to Help), `Guide`, `Markdown` (lesson bodies: `##`, `###`, `-`, `1.`, `>`, `**bold**`; no HTML, no links).

- Words people wrote: `Inert` keeps any web address as plain text (never an anchor) in posts, replies, chat lines, coach notes, inbox lines and the notes in Agency; `Markdown` has no links either.

## 7. States

Every screen is driven by TanStack Query, so loading, error and empty come for free and are handled per section: a short "nothing yet" line with the action that fills it, an error line with retry, the `Gate` screen for a module that is off or unavailable, a 404-shaped answer for a thing you cannot see. Destructive actions ask first: a sheet ("Sign out?"), a two-tap button (remove, void, a fresh link), or `window.confirm` (in about thirty older places); account deletion needs the word DELETE. Skeletons are used where a whole screen or list waits: `Skeleton`, `SkeletonCard`, `SkeletonList` and `Busy` (`components/Skeleton.tsx`: a quiet pulsing block that stops under reduced motion, hidden from screen readers, with one "Loading" read out by the wrapper) and `RouteFallback`, which shows while a lazy screen downloads and keeps the page frame (the same column and the room for the tab bar) so the real screen lands in the same place. Sections still render their frame (a chart's axes, a tile's label) before data lands. A public link that does not exist shows one plain sentence ("No page at this link. Ask the person who sent it for a fresh link."). Keyboard focus shows only from the keyboard (`focus-visible`), as a 3px ring in `ink-soft` with a 3px offset, which is 3:1 or better on the page, on a card and on a coloured fill in both modes. If a lazy screen fails to download, the page reloads once (kept from looping) and then shows one plain line.

## 8. Words

- Universal and plain: a teenager can read it. Flesch 60 or above on any file (`python3 -I scripts/flesch.py <file>`, `--strings` for a TypeScript file's quoted text; the scorer counts class-name strings as sentences, so a component full of Tailwind classes scores low without its wording being hard).
- The glossary is `TERMS` (99 words in the shared model), listed under Help as "Words we use", in named groups. Seven *pillars*, each a *pyramid* of six *layers* (foundation, guiding principle, value goal, goal outcome, project, task), the daily *small act*, the *not to do* list, *reviews* by *period* (a week, a month, a quarter or a half year), the *Daily* tab, the *Profile* tab, the *Public page* module, the *five areas of life*. Never tower, floor or area for the seven, rule, feeling, proof, rung, ladder, spark, vice, sprint, cycle, retro, Today or You as screen names.
- The product is kreators.app, lowercase with the dot, in every heading, footer, email, policy and share card. Never "Kreators Life". The merch brand is "Kreators" ("Kreators tee").
- Every app's "How it works" is three to five concrete steps with the numbers, from `HOW_TO` (49 entries); a new app gets an entry or the shared test fails. The word in Agency is "deliverable".
- No orphan words: see 11.

## 9. Accessibility, with status

- Aria labels and roles throughout; headings in order; labels on fields; a toggle is a `role="switch"`, a tab or a view switch carries `aria-pressed`.
- Reduced motion honoured in GSAP and the few `motion/react` places.
- Contrast probed over every route in dark mode; the accepted exceptions are listed in 5.
- Phone layout probed over every route (`scripts/smoke/overflow-probe.js` through the screenshot tool): no page scrolls sideways and nothing pokes past the screen, in portrait or landscape. A class may not carry `inline-flex` or `inline-block` next to `hidden`, because Tailwind 4 orders those after `hidden`.
- **Skip link.** `SkipLink` is the first stop on every app screen: a pill out of sight until it has focus, then at the top left; it moves focus to `<main id="main" tabIndex={-1}>` and scrolls to it. `scroll-padding` (80px above, 120px below) keeps the tab bar, the assistant's button and sticky chrome from covering a control the keyboard scrolls to.
- **Dialogs.** `useDialogFocus` (`lib/useDialogFocus.ts`) is the one keyboard rule for every dialog: focus moves in when it opens (the control marked `data-autofocus`, else the first control, else the box), Tab and Shift+Tab wrap at its ends (a modal one such as `Sheet` also pulls stray focus back in), Escape closes the top one only, and focus returns to the control that opened it. The assistant and the reminders panel use it as non-modal panels (the page stays usable beside them: they only wrap Tab at their ends and do not steal focus on a new page).
- **Pictures and tables.** `scripts/scan.sh` fails on any `<img>` without an `alt` (a decorative picture has `alt=""`). The `DataTable` rows can be used from the keyboard. A legal table is real table markup (see 11).
- Keyboard: focus-visible rings, disclosure rows that open from the keyboard, the kanban board's Move button and sheet as the keyboard and touch way to move a card, the card table's keys to flip and move cards.
- The target is WCAG 2.2 AA for colour (see 5). Not done: no screen-reader pass has been made; text alternatives on the drawn characters are decorative by design.

## 10. Workflow

The kit came first: tokens were pulled once from Figma (`scripts/figma-pull.py`, `scripts/figma-sections.py`) and frozen, so builds never call Figma. The look was then redrawn in `app.css` (30 and 31 October); the tokens in `app.css` are the truth now. A new screen reuses `PageHeader`, `Section`, `StatTile`, the charts and the rows; it never invents a style. After touching colours, run the contrast probe over every route in dark mode; after touching text or layout, run the orphan and overflow probes (see 09). Screenshots come from `node scripts/smoke/shoot.mjs [routes]` against the smoke server, in light, dark or both, phone, laptop and a phone on its side.

## 11. Patterns added since 0.3

- **Status tags.** Every status is a `StatusTag`: a dot and a word on a pale wash, never colour alone. The tones (`TagTone`: neutral, info, good, warn, bad) come from the maps in shared (`DELIVERABLE_STAGE_TONE`, `CLIENT_STATUS_TONE`, `CLIENT_HEALTH_TONE`, `INVOICE_STATUS_TONE`, `CONTRACT_STATUS_TONE`).
- **List or board.** A pipeline (clients, deliverables, invoices, agreements) switches between a list and a kanban board with `ViewToggle`; the choice lives in the URL through `useView`. A board column is a page of its own with a count and "Show more"; cards move by drag or by a 44px Move button that opens a sheet of the columns the card may go to (the keyboard and touch way). Columns are a fixed 260px (280 from `md`) and sit side by side in a `.scroll-row` at every width, with a fade on the edge that has more; with no view in the URL a phone starts on the list. The page itself never scrolls sideways.
- **Short pages with an Edit button on each part.** The invoice designer, the agreement editor and Agency's settings show a live preview and a short list of summary cards (`SummaryCard`: a title, one or two lines saying what is set now, and an Edit button) or rows; Edit opens only that part in a `Sheet` with a Done button. An edit changes the draft and the preview at once and one Save sends it. Never one long form with every field open.
- **Fold, not a bare details.** A fold-out is `components/Fold.tsx`: a rounded flat button with a chevron, tinted while open. A browser's own `<details>` has no corners and no chevron and is not used.
- **A tab or a toggle keeps the page where it is.** Anything that only changes what is shown passes `preventScrollReset: true` when it changes the address (`useParamState` does it for all of them; the leaderboard, Run's tabs and the public Explore filters do it themselves). Only a new page starts at the top. `node scripts/smoke/drive-scroll.mjs` checks it.
- **No orphan words.** `html` carries `text-wrap: balance` (inherited, so cards, list items and short paragraphs share their words evenly over up to about six lines); long reading text (`prose-long`, `Markdown`, anything `whitespace-pre-wrap`) uses `text-wrap: pretty`; never `text-pretty` on short text; a line clamp (Tailwind's `line-clamp-2`) switches balancing off, so it is not used on text that can wrap into a lone word. Where the stylesheet cannot help (a three-word title in a narrow card), `tie()` in `lib/text.ts` keeps the last two words together. `scripts/smoke/orphan-probe.js` must report 0 hits on a laptop; a phone may keep a rare long word that fills over half its line.
- **Print and save as PDF.** An invoice or an agreement prints as the page itself: `@media print` in `app.css` hides anything with `.no-print` (the menus, the buttons, the assistant, the thread) and leaves `.print-doc` as the one thing on the sheet, with colours kept. The "Download PDF" button is the browser's print and save, with the document title set to the number or the agreement's title; the server makes no PDF.
- **Bento.** A grid of tiles uses `bento(i, count, base, wide)` from `lib/bento.ts`, which gives the first tiles two columns until the last row is full, so there is never a lone tile (`.bento`, `.bento-w2` to `.bento-h2` on a four-column grid for the public site and Home's metrics).
- **Sounds.** `lib/sound.ts` makes every sound in the browser (soft beats, a chime, rain, ocean, wind, a soft hum and Lo-fi). The master switch silences all of them, the volume reaches a sound that is already playing, and the preview follows both. Checked in a real browser, since a type check cannot hear.
- **One style for the link at the top right of a section.** Every link that sits level with a section's title is `SeeAll` (in `components/Section.tsx`): 14px bold muted text, a small chevron after it, a 44px tap area, a router `Link` (never a plain `<a href>`, which reloads the page). "Open", "See all", "Wellbeing" and "Review it" are all that one component; a screen never styles its own. An action that changes something (Edit, Add a task, Invoice this month, the list and board toggle) is a `Button size="sm"`, outlined for a secondary action and solid for the main one. The action is level with the first line of the title, at the top of the block, never down by the line under it. `node scripts/smoke/drive-headings.mjs` measures every heading with an action and also fails a link action that has no chevron.
- **Home's halves match.** On a laptop the two halves of a row are one height (the taller one's), their cards start on one line and fill their half, and the cards inside a half that share a row are one height (the reads under Knowledge). Every half-width section has one short line under its title that fits on one line. A hero card with buttons at its foot (Daily's "Check in" and the round arrow beside it) is a flex column with the button row pushed to the bottom (`mt-auto`), and the two buttons are one height, so a taller neighbour never leaves them floating. `node scripts/smoke/drive-home.mjs` checks all of this at 1100, 1280 and 1600px.
- **Plan my week is a sheet you work in.** Each row is a checkbox, the words and a control. In "Suggested days" the checkbox is centred on the row, the coloured pillar dot sits beside the first line of the title and the helper line ("45m, it had no day yet") lines up under the title text, not under the dot; the day picker is a fixed 148px at the right, with no label (its `aria-label` names the task). In "Worth a look" the text is centred on its checkbox and its Fix or Hide button. In a Fix row the day picker is a fixed width (`shrink-0`) so the task title keeps the rest of the line; a picker that is also `w-full` squeezes the title to nothing, which is the bug `drive-plan.mjs` now measures.
- **Water and reminders box.** On a laptop a bubble at the left of Daily and Journal opens a panel; on a phone it is a section inside the screen. Water is on the left (the day's glasses, a tap, any amount, the seven-day chart, the drink reminder with its hours, every two or four hours) and "Your reminders" on the right: the person's own reminders, then "Tasks from Daily" (the tasks tagged Remember, each with a tick that switches the tag off, and "Remind me about a task", which tags any open task from Daily with a time and, when it has no day, a day). The days of a new reminder sit on one line in a seven-column grid: two letters (Mo, Tu, We, Th, Fr, Sa, Su) on a phone, three letters from `sm` up.
- **A check must be able to fail.** A layout fix is not done until the probe that should catch it has been seen failing on the old build (the Home card heights, the Fix row's title width). Probes that always pass are worse than none.

- **The privacy card, as built.** Asked once, and answered by one of three equal outline buttons in a row, "Reject all", "Save mine" and "Accept all", under three tick rows (`attribution`, `embeds`, `marketing`), all unticked: no choice is solid or pre-picked. The ticks are 24px with a 44px row, the helper lines 12px. The card slides up with GSAP (`gsap.set` under reduced motion). A visitor's answer is kept on the device (`lib/consent.ts`, which also sets the one cookie `kc_attr` the server reads); a signed-in person's is kept on the account and taken onto each device. If the browser sends Global Privacy Control, the cookie tick is greyed off and says why. It returns only when the wording version changes (`CONSENT_VERSION`) or the footer's "Privacy choices" button opens it (a `kreators:open-consent` event). An account made before the terms were written gets a different first step of the same card, "We wrote our rules down", with links to the terms and the privacy policy and one "I agree" button; the terms step shows only for an account that has an email. The answer can be changed under Profile, "Privacy choices" (`PrivacyChoices`), which shows the date of each acceptance.
- **TermsBox.** The box next to the button that makes an account (the sign-up page, the setup chat's account card and `AccountForm`): a 24px checkbox in a 44px `toy toy-flat` row, unticked at the start, with links to the terms and the privacy policy in a new tab and the version in brackets. The button stays off until it is ticked, and the server refuses a sign-up without `acceptTerms`.
- **EmbedGate.** A YouTube or Vimeo video is replaced by a same-shape card (16 by 9) until the person has said yes to videos or taps "Play this video"; it says which site hosts it and what that site sees, and offers "Always show videos", which saves the choice. No request goes to the other site before that.
- **Legal pages.** `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/refunds`, `/contact` and `/pricing` are one `Policy` screen; `/subprocessors`, `/copyright` (the notice and counter-notice forms) and `/licences` are their own. Each table in them (the lawful bases, the retention list, the sub-processors) reads as a table on a laptop and as stacked cards on a phone: the same markup is `block` below `md`, each row a rounded card with the column names hidden, and `table` from `md` up (`legalBits.tsx`), so no page scrolls sideways. Footers carry a "Privacy choices" button beside the legal links. Nothing on these pages names the business unless the owner has set it; empty fields read "Being set up".
- **Fonts and loading.** Self-hosted fonts and the lazy screens are why a first visit makes no request to a third party for type and downloads about 421 kB gzip of script. A screen shows `RouteFallback` while it downloads.

## 2026-11-03: icons, one primary action, and the print section (the overhaul)

- **Icons are one family.** The founder asked for emoji in the interface to become icons, except inside text people write and in footer links. Static icons are `lucide-react` (shadcn's set), drawn only through `components/Icon.tsx` (one stroke, `ICON_STROKE` 2, `currentColor`, decorative unless given `label`) or `components/AppIcon.tsx` (an icon named by a shared registry key, through `lib/appIcons.ts`). An icon never carries meaning alone: a button with only an icon puts its words in `aria-label`.
- **Eight animated icons.** `components/AnimatedIcon.tsx` plays useanimations.com's line animations (`react-useanimations`, loaded lazily) for eight names: menu, searchToX (the search box's magnifier turning into Clear), settings, trash, star, copy, maximizeMinimize (focus mode) and loading. Each has a still lucide fallback, which is what shows under reduced motion and before the animation loads. Used for the copy buttons in the Share sheet (a tick while copied), the scorecard stars in the strategy session and the assistant's Undo while it runs.
- **Where emoji stay.** In what people write, in chat text, in footer links, on a reward item, on a card game's cover and on Daily's printed month (the boxes). Shared lists carry an `icon` key instead (badges, honors, moods, interests, link blocks, Content sources and pieces, life areas, event types, Discover add-ons, love languages, sounds, chat chips); a badge's glyph is drawn inside the medal in its tier colour, a mood is a `MoodFace`, a rating is `components/Stars.tsx`.
- **Asking again.** A spend of points (reserving a reward, unlocking a game) asks in `components/ConfirmSpend.tsx`, a sheet with what it costs, one solid button and Cancel, never the browser's confirm box. Red items in a More menu ask again in a sheet too.
- **Home's halves stay one height.** On the two-column Home the halves of a row stretch to the taller one; a half whose content is shorter centres it (the Companion card) instead of leaving an empty band at its foot. Home's header links (level, streak, Log your mood) are 44px tall.
- **Folds everywhere.** Help's questions, the public home's questions and the pillar page's layer list and done tasks are `Fold`s; only the sidebar groups keep a styled `<details>`.
- **One copy of the form strings and helpers.** `FIELD`, `FIELD_LINE`, `SELECT_SM` and the label class in `lib/ui.ts`; `FieldLabel` in `components/Field.tsx`; `errorText` and `ErrorLine` in `components/ErrorLine.tsx`; `copyPlain` in `lib/clipboard.ts`; `longDate` ("24 Oct 2026") and `dayLabel` ("Sat 24 Oct") in `lib/date.ts`. Some Agency screens still keep a local select class (`FILTER_SELECT`) and field strings, to be moved onto these.
- **Calm public pages for clients.** The client link and the public invoice, agreement and document use `SiteLayout minimal` (the logo and the theme switch, the footer kept for the privacy links).
- **Settings as an inset list.** Agency settings and the parts of a document are `.fx-inset` lists of `.fx-row` rows (56px, an icon, a title, what is set now, a chevron), each opening a sheet.
- **44px targets.** Every tappable thing is at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels (the Pager's selects and buttons, icon buttons, chips).
- **One primary action per view.** A row of equal pill buttons has no hierarchy. The rest of a row's actions sit behind `components/MoreMenu.tsx` (one "More" button that opens a `Sheet` of full-width rows); sharing a document is one Share button that opens `components/ShareSheet.tsx`. Finance's two primaries are Money in and Money out (solid); Owed, Monthly, Goal and Budgets are quieter outline buttons below them.
- **Segmented controls, not pill walls.** A choice between a few views is `components/fx/Segmented.tsx` (a sliding thumb), as in Agency's list and board switch (`ViewToggle`) and Discover.
- **One print section.** Every `@media print` rule is in one section at the end of `app.css`, in order: what never prints, Daily's month, a `.print-doc` page (the client dashboard), then a page that holds a document sheet (`.legal-root`), so the document rule never blanks the month or the dashboard.
- **The page wash ends in a fade.** The fixed wash behind every page (`body::before`) fades out over its last stretch into the page's own base, so a long page never shows a hard line where the fixed layer stops.
