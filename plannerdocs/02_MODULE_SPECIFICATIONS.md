# 02 — Module specifications

Last updated: 2026-11-05

**Product:** kreators.app
**Version:** 0.6, reconciled with the app on 5 November 2026
**Source of truth:** `MODULES` in `packages/shared/src/modules.ts`, `HOW_TO` and `TERMS` in `packages/shared/src/help.ts` (both reached through the shared barrel `index.ts`); one route file per module in `apps/api/src/routes`; the smoke test in `scripts/smoke/smoke.mjs`; `CLAUDE.md` for the detailed rules of each.

## How a module is specified here

Every module is one entry in `MODULES` with a key, a name, a group, a path, a default (on or off for a new person), a minimum role, and an accent colour. There are 46, counted from the code. Its rules are written once in `HOW_TO` (three to five steps with the numbers) and shown at the bottom of its main screen; the words it uses are defined in `TERMS` and listed under Help. The shared test insists every module has a `HOW_TO` entry and only uses defined words.

The flag for a module is its key, in `module_settings`. There are no `module.*`, `feature.*` or `ops.*` flags. Every API prefix maps to its module in `access.ts` (`MODULE_FOR_PREFIX`), and a module that is not available to the person answers 403 on every route; a new route file needs a line in that map or it is treated as core and always open. Every long list a module serves is a page at a time (`page` and `size`, 10 to 100 rows, `Page` in the answer); a module with two lists on one screen prefixes them.

Rate limits are in `apps/api/src/limits.ts`, one line per limit with the route it guards.

Every app's main screen ends with a "How it works" card (three to five concrete steps, then the words it leans on), closed by default.

Each block below gives: purpose, path and default, who can do what, tables, the rules, the smoke checks that prove them, and gaps.

## A. Core platform

**Purpose:** accounts, sessions, modules, inbox, help, search, export and deletion; the daily game; Daily's tasks and planning; Emerging; the setup chat and the strategy session; the leaderboard, rewards and the assistant.

**Routes:** `auth.ts` (sign-up, sign-in, sign-out, anonymous start, forgot, reset), `me.ts` (profile, password, modules, app groups, portfolio, link clicks, export, reset, delete), `activity.ts` ("Your year", `GET /me/activity`), `calendar.ts` (the private calendar link), `inbox.ts`, `support.ts`, `search.ts`, `home.ts`, `today.ts`, `nodes.ts`, `insights.ts` (Emerging), `books.ts` (the public, read-only book list), `onboarding.ts`, `session.ts` (the strategy session: assessment, plan, day, routine), `reminders.ts` (water and the person's own reminders), `leaderboard.ts`, `rewards.ts`, `media.ts`, `connect.ts`, `assistant.ts`, `feelings.ts`, `credits.ts`, `discounts.ts` (checking a team code), `legal.ts` (what a person accepted and chose, under `/me/legal`, and the public business details, copyright notices and unsubscribe) and `vitals.ts` (real-user web vitals).

**Tables:** `users`, `sessions`, `session_evictions`, `relationship_scores`, `onboarding_answers`, `assessments`, `placements`, `routines`, `contracts`, `routine_days`, `nodes`, `subtasks`, `day_types`, `xp_events`, `spark_completions`, `honors`, `badge_grants`, `badge_awards`, `points_ledger`, `redemptions`, `notifications`, `support_tickets`, `not_to_do`, `not_to_do_days`, `password_resets`, `rate_limits`, `reminder_prefs`, `water_logs`, `custom_reminders`, `calendar_feeds`, `media_items`, `media_files`, `app_logs`, `terms_acceptances`, `user_consents`, `consent_records`, `data_requests`, `copyright_notices`, `web_vitals`.

**Rules**
- Sign-up asks for an email, a password, a phone number, a date of birth and a ticked box agreeing to the terms (below); anyone under 18 is refused (`MIN_AGE`, `birthDateProblem`, `phoneProblem` in shared). A phone with no country code is Indian (`normalizePhone`). The phone can be changed under Profile; the date of birth cannot; neither is ever shown to anyone else. It creates the user and seven zero scores and nothing else. A device session can come first; adding an email keeps its progress and asks the same questions.
- The password rule `passwordProblem` is the one rule for the form, the change form and both endpoints (8 to 128 characters, not common, not one character repeated, not your email). Sign-in answers the same for an unknown email and a wrong password and is limited per address and per email; a reset link is one-time, thirty minutes, and signs every device out.
- An account is signed in on at most three devices (`MAX_DEVICES`); `newSession` is the one place a session is made and it signs the oldest out, with a note on that device saying why.
- The game: check in 10 xp and 10 points, every seventh day in a row 50 points more; the small act 15 xp; a task 20 xp with a ripple (project 10, outcome 8, value goal 6, principle 4, pillar 2); clearing a project 30 xp; a kept not-to-do day 5 xp. Six rewarded ticks a day. The first three ticks are free; then ten minutes of age and a ten-minute gap. Level n starts at 60·n·(n−1) xp.
- **Tasks.** A task has a do date (the day it will be done; it opens on Daily from that day and is the only date that holds a tick back) and an optional due date (the deadline: shown as Due and Overdue, never a lock; the do date cannot come after it). A task with neither date waits under Not scheduled yet and is never in the main list. A task can repeat (daily, weekdays, weekly on chosen days, monthly, yearly, every 1 to 52, an optional end day; `parseRepeat`, `nextOccurrence` and `repeatLabel` are the one rule; a month end is clamped, and a monthly or yearly task that fell on the 28th of a short month goes back to the 31st the next time, because the rule keeps the day it started on); ticking it makes the next one. A day must be a real calendar day: 2026-11-31 is refused, never a server error. A priority P0 to P3 tags and sorts and never changes what a tick pays. Subtasks (up to 20) pay nothing and never block a tick. A time estimate feeds **Start focus**, which opens Mindfulness with the task picked. Remember is a tag: with a day it sends one inbox note that day at its time (09:00 by default); with no day it waits in To remember on Daily. A ticked task stays in sight under Done today and a tap unticks it, taking back its xp. A task cannot be ticked before its do date.
- **Habit tag** (migration 0082, `nodes.habit`). A repeating task wears Habit unless the person says no. "Count it as a habit" is a switch in the task form, shown only while a repeat is set and on by default. An empty value keeps the old rule; the next task in a repeat carries the choice. `taskIsHabit` in shared is the one reader.
- **Moving a task.** An open task moves to another pillar with `PATCH /nodes/:id {parentId}`, only under one of the person's own active projects, and takes that project's pillar. A ticked task stays where it is.
- **Kind of day** (migration 0077 `day_types`, `DAY_TYPES` in shared `dayTypes.ts`, `GET` and `PUT /today/day-type`, limit `prefs`). One dropdown at the left of Daily's Sort by row picks the kind of day, for today or tomorrow only. Each kind names the book and pages it comes from, or says it is the app's own. It pays nothing. The By context sort keeps P0 above everything and, inside each priority, puts the day's lead pillar first.
- **Daily's task list.** Sorts are day, priority, due date and By context (`TASK_SORTS`, kept in the URL). On a task row the priority sits in its corner and the pillar at its foot. Every other mark (Remember, Habit, For review, From pillar, the time, the due day, subtasks) sits at the right of the row from the tablet width, and across the row's foot on a phone, so the title keeps its width. "Add a task" is a fold under the list: it puts the cursor in the title, folds again after saving and says where the task went. A **Habits** section sits under Not to do, with up to five good or bad habit rows (see Emerging).
- **Plan my week** (`planWeek`, `GET /today/week-plan`): the next seven days. A task with a do date or only a due date is already placed (a late one counts on today); each task with no day gets a suggested day, the highest priority first and then ones made from a pillar, on the lightest day it fits in, never past the hours a day the person set (1 to 12, default 4). A **plan strength** out of 100 (days with something planned 40, days not overloaded 40, tasks with an estimate 20) is shown now and with the suggestions kept, with up to four plain notes. Rules only, so no credits. It writes nothing; keeping a suggestion is the ordinary edit of a do date. **Time blocks** lay the day's tasks that have a time out by their estimate (30 minutes when none) with the free stretches between and overlaps flagged, and a faint "Focused 25m" bar beside a task with focus sessions today. A task can be **brought to the review** with one flag.
- **Plan my week is a sheet you work in** (`WeekPlan.tsx`). Each suggested day is a row: a checkbox, the title with its coloured dot, the helper line under the title, and the day picker at the right (148 pixels). Under **Worth a look**, each plan note has a tick for "dealt with" and a Fix that offers what the note is about right there (move a task off a heavy or late day, give a task a length, fill an empty day, place one that did not fit); a Fix row keeps room for the task title. **Add your own** finds any open task by name and opens the same form a task has on Daily. Nothing is written before one **Keep**, which applies the suggestions, the fixes and anything added as one `PATCH /nodes/:id` per task.
- The routine's wake-up and lights-out are eased toward the targets by `rampTime`: thirty minutes a week of keeping the contract, never past the target, and an early riser is left where they are. `zonedToUtc` puts a local time that does not exist (the hour skipped when clocks go forward) an hour later, not earlier.
- **Month calendar** (`TaskMonth.tsx`, `GET /today/month`): a closed fold under Tasks on Daily holding a Monday-first month. Each day is a button with the count of tasks still to do (a tick when all are done) and up to two titles on wider screens; a task sits on its do date, else its due date. Tapping a day opens a sheet with that day's tasks (the ordinary tick and untick) and an add form that creates a task through the same call as a pillar page, with the do date set to the day; with no project yet it says tasks need one and links to Pillars. **Print this month** prints a landscape paper calendar (a box per day, ☐ or ☑, the time, the title, P0 to P3 as text, a legend) through the browser's print. The route is the person's own, bounded to 500 tasks a month, and writes nothing.
- Not to do: at most 7 items, titles up to 60 characters, one row per item per day, only today or yesterday markable, the streak rule in `notToDoStreak`; the section is on Daily under Tasks and again on Pillars.
- **Emerging** (`insightsFor`, `insightsForEvery` and `insightsForPlace` in shared `insights.ts`; `GET /insights` in `routes/insights.ts`, core; limit `insights`, 120 an hour per person). Patterns read across the person's own apps, by rules only, computed on read and stored nowhere. One request answers the whole list, with each pillar's own balance line. The client keeps it once per device day and each screen picks its share. An ordinary write only marks it stale; the habit and not to do sheets, task edits on Daily, the month calendar, Plan my week and a pillar page's add and edit fetch it again. A signal runs only while every app it reads is on. The journal is read only by when an entry was written; wellbeing by kind, day and number; money only in the person's own currency. A line says things went together, never that one caused another. Nothing reaches a coach, the assistant, staff or the leaderboard. One block sits after a screen's numbers on Home (the top three), Daily, Pillars, each pillar page, Mood, Sleep, Stress, Mindfulness, Wellbeing, Life, Journal, Finance, Reviews and Discover. A card has the number, a tag in its corner, one sentence, a small chart, the basis line, one outline action and a More menu (Not now, kept on the device). **Make it a habit** opens a sheet with a suggested repeat, time and first day, saved through the ordinary task routes. **Good and bad habits**: a task ticked on 3 or more days in 8 weeks, or an item of the not to do list, is set against the person's own days with it and without (mood, sleep that night, stress, other tasks, focus minutes; each only while its app is on). A clear lift is a Good habit, a clear dip a Bad habit, and too few days is Still watching. A good habit offers Make it a habit; a bad one offers Add to not to do. `/insights` is a page, not an app: two counts, a filter, the patterns, a Good and bad habits block and Still watching. `insights.golden.test.ts` pins the whole output, so a speed change cannot change what a person is told.
- **Water and reminders** (`reminders.ts`, `Reminders.tsx`): a water goal, glass size, taps and a seven-day chart, plus the person's own reminders (a line, a time, the days). Owner only; water pays no xp. How often water reminds is two or four hours (`WATER_EVERY_CHOICES`); anything shorter is refused. Water has its own two-hour cron trigger and sends one inbox line a day that each reminder rewrites (new count, back to unread, newest first); an inbox water line from a day that is over is deleted by the cron, read or not. The panel's "Your reminders" side lists the open tasks tagged Remember (`GET /reminders` returns `tasks`), each with a tick that switches the tag off, and **Remind me about a task**, which tags any open task with a time (and a day when it has none) through the ordinary `PATCH /nodes/:id`. It is one thing seen from three places: the Daily tag, this list and the one inbox note. Daily's own section leaves the list out, since Daily shows those tasks under To remember. A reminder set between 23:46 and 23:59 goes out at the last tick of the day, 23:45.
- Home: every link at the top right of a section heading is `SeeAll` (a router link, so it never reloads the page). Home's paired halves are one height and their cards start on one line. Results first (xp today, streak, points, level), the daily brief (`GET /home/brief`, three signals and three next steps from plain rules, never pointing at an app that is off), Getting started for the first days (a small reversible toggle), one "Xp, last 14 days" section with By day and By source (`?xp=source`), the founder's three books (`BookShelf`), Emerging's top three, a line of the day (a philosopher and a line written for the app, alternating, none repeating for about a year) and the person's apps in the order of context.
- **Inbox** has tabs by kind, Unread and Archived views, and an archive. In Archived each note can be restored or deleted, and "Delete all archived" empties the tab; each asks first in a sheet. Only archived notes can be deleted.
- Leaderboard: boards by everyone, goal, pillar, city, identity tag and experience, ranked by xp, this month, streak or badges, paged, over the top of the people who opted in and finished setup; nobody blocked either way is listed; never followers, never journal or wellbeing, never a date of birth or age band. A monthly season keeps its top three and gives them an honor.
- Rewards: a catalog in code (`REWARDS`, shelves in `REWARD_GROUPS`: books, card games, apparel, tech, drinkware, stationery, lifestyle, novelty, experiences), every cost in whole points; a redemption is a reservation, and the points are taken first with a guard (`points >= cost`), so two taps at once cannot spend the same points; cancelling moves the reservation out of `reserved` first and then returns the points once; a card game is a reward of kind `unlock` that opens the game at once and for good. Reserving or unlocking asks once more in a sheet (`ConfirmSpend`). The reservations are paged on the server, with a pager under Your reservations.
- **The founder's books** (`BOOKS` in shared `books.ts`, his own words only; `GET /public/books`, public and read only). Three books: The Relationship Method, Dictionary of Mistakes for Creators and Dictionary of Mistakes for 20s. They show as `BookShelf` on Home, the public home and the foot of Features. Each opens its place on `/features/books` or, in the app, on `/book` (not a module). That place links the book's Shop product (a draft until the founder prices it, shown as "Book: coming soon"), its School course and the Rewards shelf.
- **Your year** (`ActivityGrid` on Profile, `GET /me/activity`, core and owner only): a year of days like a code contribution grid, counting xp or tasks ticked in the person's zone, never what they were for.
- Apps listed anywhere are ordered by group first, then inside a group by the person's own order, then by context: what is on, then what fits the setup answers, then what the person opens most on this device, then the app's own order.
- **The setup chat and the strategy session.** The setup chat (`SetupChat`, `matchSetup` in shared) is a few taps on small grouped lists; the match picks up to five apps with a reason each, switches them on and the rest of the switchable groups off, and offers the strategy session or the Discover quiz. The identity tags include Agency or studio owner, Agency team member, Brand or client, Life coach, Run club host, Video editor and Videographer; a tag can lean toward Agency and its Team tab. The strategy session (thirteen parts, `session.ts`) is the pillars' own onboarding. Love, teachers and faith can be marked "not part of my life right now" (migration 0066, `OPTIONAL_PILLARS`): such a pillar is placed at Neutrality and left out of the focus and the order, and can be turned back on from the life map. Both chats keep their place for the tab. Detail of the placement, focus, routine and contract rules is in `CLAUDE.md`.
- **The assistant.** A round button on every screen opens a panel; its free flows write through the same routes the screens use; with a plan, typed lines go to a model that acts through the app's own routes as the person, each change coming back with Undo, removals and money waiting for Confirm, Stop honoured before each model call and each tool. Journal and tracker reads only for a model that keeps no data. Typing is paid in credits (`PLANS` Pro, Pro Max and Ultra, priced in rupees; top-ups; limits per agent). Until payments are live only an admin starts a plan. The Companion is the third agent on the same model layer and ledger (see M).
- **Private calendar link** (`calendar_feeds`, `calendar.ts`, `/me/calendar`, public `GET /api/public/calendar/<token>.ics`): off until the person switches it on; a 24-character secret address to subscribe to from Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar; a fresh link closes the old one. It carries each open task that has a day (a title, the pillar and the priority, and the time in the person's zone when it has one), the reviews the person scheduled and, while Agency is on, Agency pieces' due and go-live days (title and client only). It never carries a note, the journal, a mood, anything from a tracker or a fee. The file escapes a lone carriage return and folds long lines before 75 bytes, never inside a character. One way: tasks show in the calendar and nothing in the calendar changes a task. In the app the Calendar card sits on the Profile tab (`/you`).
- **Terms at sign-up** (migration 0059, `routes/legal.ts`). Sign-up needs `acceptTerms: true` or the server answers 400 ("tick the box to agree..."). A `TermsBox` (an unticked checkbox with links to the terms of use and the privacy policy, and the version) sits next to the button on the sign-up page, on the setup chat's account card and in `AccountForm`; the button stays off until it is ticked. `recordAcceptance` writes one append-only row in `terms_acceptances` for each document (`terms`, `privacy`) at `LEGAL_VERSION` (`2026-11-05`); a repeat for the same version adds nothing. An account that accepted an older version gets a one-time notice in the privacy card (`POST /me/legal/accept`, recorded as `notice`), shown only to an account that has an email and again only if the version changes. The version moved on 5 November, when the terms gained "Wellbeing, not medical care", "Money tools, not advice" and a line under Acceptable use, so every account sees the notice once. `GET /me/legal` says what the person accepted and chose.
- **Privacy choices** (`ConsentPopup`, `PrivacyChoices`, `lib/consent.ts`). Three choices, all off until the person says yes (`CONSENT_PURPOSES`): `attribution` (the thirty-day cookie that tells a creator which tracked link sent you), `embeds` (YouTube and Vimeo videos) and `marketing` (news and offers by email). A card at the bottom left asks once ("Your privacy", a line pointing questions to the Grievance Officer, a quiet "Choose for each", then Reject all and Accept all side by side and equal; on a phone it floats above the bottom). The answer is kept on the account (`user_consents`, one row per purpose, plus an append-only `consent_records` line with where it was made: `popup`, `profile` or `unsubscribe`, and the wording version) and, for a visitor, on the device. It returns only when `CONSENT_VERSION` changes or the footer's "Privacy choices" button asks. Under Profile, **Privacy choices** shows the same three ticks, each saving at once, and the date the terms were accepted (`PUT /me/legal/consent`). A browser that sends Global Privacy Control keeps the cookie off. `/go` sets `kl_click` only outside `CONSENT_FIRST_COUNTRIES` (India under the DPDP Act, the EU, the rest of the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, which wait for a yes) or after a yes, and never under Global Privacy Control, even after a yes (5 November). A video waits behind `EmbedGate` until the person has allowed videos or taps Play; nothing is requested from the other site before that.
- **Marketing email and unsubscribe.** `sendEmail({ marketing: true })` sends only to someone with a recorded yes on `marketing`; otherwise nothing is sent and the outbox row keeps the reason. It is also held back while no business address is set, because every marketing mail ends with the business name and postal address and an unsubscribe link, and carries the `List-Unsubscribe` headers (one-click). The public page `/unsubscribe/<token>` (`GET` and `POST /api/public/unsubscribe/:token`, limit `unsubscribe`) turns the choice off and logs it as `unsubscribe`. Receipts, password resets and "password changed" mails are never marketing. The reset mail's body is not kept in the outbox.
- **The legal pages** (site, no sign-in): `/terms`, `/privacy` (India first: the DPDP Act, the data principal's rights, a ground for each use in a table, the Grievance Officer and then the Data Protection Board of India; the EU and UK ground and rights below, labelled for those countries; how long we keep things in a table, the three choices, cookies and Global Privacy Control, children (18 and over), the AI statement, and a note for each of Agency, the calendar link, life coaches, payments and KYC, the content strategist and the assistant), `/refunds` (UPI, card or bank through Razorpay first), `/contact`, `/pricing` (rupees, GST shown before paying; a plan lasts 30 days and does not renew by itself), `/subprocessors` (`SUBPROCESSORS` in shared), `/copyright` and `/licences`. The numbers on them come from shared, not from the page: `RETENTION` is the retention table and `RETENTION_DAYS` is what the cron applies. The business name, address, registration, tax number, Grievance Officer and copyright agent come from Worker variables (`BUSINESS_*`, `DMCA_*`, served by `GET /api/public/business`) and read "not set yet" until the owner fills them in. A reply time is never stated unless the business sets one. The public footer carries an emergency line (Tele-MANAS 14416 in India).
