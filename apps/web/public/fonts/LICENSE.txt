The files are the woff2 subsets (latin and latin-ext) that Google Fonts serves for each family (Caveat: latin only, for a guide's handwritten line),
saved here so that no request goes to a third party when a page loads. They keep two of the
family's axes, weight (300 to 800) and roundness (ROND), which holds the latin file near 72 kB;
no glyph is changed. The full license text is at https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/
