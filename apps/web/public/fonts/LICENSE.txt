The files are the woff2 subsets (latin and latin-ext) that Google Fonts serves for each family,
saved here so that no request goes to a third party when a page loads. inter-currency.woff2 is
the currency signs (the rupee among them) cut from Inter's latin-ext file with pyftsubset, so a
page with a price loads 8 kB instead of the whole latin-ext file; no glyph is changed.
