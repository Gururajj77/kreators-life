# Daily, part one: tasks and your week
Your pillars say why. Daily says what, today. It is where your plan turns into ticks. This lesson covers the top of Daily and your tasks. The next one covers habits, the not to do list, reminders and your routine.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- How to check in and do your small act.
- How to plan a task with a do date, a due date, a priority and an area of life.
- How to sort the list, add a task, and use the month calendar.
- How Plan my week spreads your tasks so no day is too full, and gives each one a block.

## The top of Daily

Daily opens with **Show up**. Tap **I showed up today** to check in. It pays 10 xp and 10 points and keeps your streak going. Every seventh day in a row pays 50 bonus points. Once you tap it, the row reads "Checked in, day" and your streak count.

Next is your guide's small act for today, like "Dew's small act for today". It is one two-minute thing for your weakest pillar, and it pays 15 xp.

## Your tasks

Under **Tasks** you see what is open for today, from the pillars you set up. Three tabs sit over the list, each with its count:

- **Today:** what is set for today. Six show at a time. The rest wait in a fold that says how many, like **8 more waiting**.
- **Past due:** open tasks from days gone by, oldest first, ten a page. Tick one, or give it a new day with **Plan my week**.
- **Upcoming:** what is set for later, soonest first, ten a page, with the day over each run. One with a do date waits for its day.

Tap the box at a task's left to tick it. It pays 20 xp and sends a ripple up its pyramid.

Your first three ticks have no wait. After that, a task must be ten minutes old, and ten minutes after your last tick. So you do the thing first, then tick it. Up to six tasks a day pay xp. The rest still count as done.

## Planning a task

Tap the task itself, or the small pencil at its top right. Its plan opens right under it. The first field, **Task**, is its name, so you can change the words right there.

1. **Do on:** the do date, the day you will do it. The task shows on Daily from that day, and cannot be ticked before. **Today**, **Tomorrow** and **Next week** are quick picks.
2. **Due by:** the deadline. After it the task shows Overdue, but you can still tick it.
3. **Priority:** P0 Do now, P1 Important, P2 Normal or P3 Whenever. It helps you choose and sort. It never changes what a tick pays.
4. **Area of life:** No area, or Physical, Financial, Mental, Emotional or Spiritual. The task wears that tag, and a tick counts toward that area in Life.
5. **Time block:** leave it on By its time, or pick a block of your day, like Deep work. Part three of Daily covers blocks.
6. **Time** and **Minutes:** when you will do it, and how long it should take. Quick picks run from 15 minutes to an hour and a half.
7. **Subtasks:** small steps inside the task, up to twenty. They pay nothing and never stop you ticking the task.

Tap **Save plan**. Once a task has minutes, **Start focus** opens Mindfulness with the task picked and the timer set to that length. **Delete task**, at the foot of the plan, asks for a second tap: it turns red and reads **Tap again to delete**.

> **Pro tip:** the do date and the due date are not the same thing. The do date is when you will work on it. The due date is when it must be done. The do date can never come after the due date.

## Sorting the list

- **Kind of day:** a dropdown that starts on **Follow your week**, with the day template your week gives today, like Everyday. Pick another, like Deep work day, Family day or Low energy day, to change today only. Your kind of day is a day template. Part three of Daily covers them.
- **Area:** shows once a task has an area. Pick one area, every area, or No area.
- **Sort:** Day, Priority, Due date, By context, By time block or By area, at the right beside **Area**. By context keeps P0 on top. Inside each priority, it puts first the pillar your day template leads with. **Past due** keeps its own sort.
- **By time block** and **By area** show your tasks in coloured boxes you can open and fold, with P0 first in each box.
- **View:** the eye at the end of the row. It hides parts of Daily you do not use, like **Habits** or **Month calendar**, on every device. A quiet line says how many are hidden, with **Show all**.

## Place my day

Under **Your day**, a line says how many of today's tasks with no block or time fit your blocks. **Place my day** gives each one a start in the block that fits, P0 first, never on top of a task that already has a time. Nothing is saved until you tap it. A task that repeats waits for a time from you, since its time would carry to the next ones.

## Adding a task

Under the list, open **Add a task**. Type the task, pick which project it holds up, and add it. A line under it says where it went. A task always hangs from a project, so it always has a reason.

## The lists below

- **Not scheduled yet:** tasks with no do date and no due date. They stay out of Tasks until you pick a day. Then they move up.
- **Done today:** what you ticked, struck through, with the time. It starts folded, with the day's count beside it; tap it to open. Tap one to untick it. That takes back its xp. It shows ten at a time. Pick 25 or 50 under it, or go to the next page.
- **Hidden tasks:** tasks you hid from your lists. In a task's plan, tap **Hide this task**, then **Until tomorrow** or **Until I unhide it**. A hidden task sends no reminder and keeps its days. It waits in this fold, last under Tasks, with **Unhide** under it. A tick there still counts.

## Close the unfinished

Things you left open tend to stay on your mind: a course you stopped, a talk you put off, a choice you keep turning over. Any task can be marked as one of three kinds. Tap the task and pick under **Bottleneck**:

- **Unfinished:** you started it and did not finish. Do you still want it enough to give it more time?
- **Unresolved:** it is with other people, like a talk to have or a fight. Have the talk, or decide to let it go.
- **Undecided:** a choice you keep thinking about. Get what you need to decide, or decide that you cannot yet.

Once it has a kind, the same panel offers three ways to end it, under **End it**. **Do it** puts it on today; tick it when it is done. **Give it a time and date** picks a day and a time, with a reminder. **Let it go** takes a second tap. It leaves your lists and reminders.

To see only these tasks, pick **Bottlenecks** in **Sort**, or tap **Close the unfinished** on Home, which shows while one of these is open. Each kind sits in its own coloured box with its question. Under the boxes, **Let go** lists what you let go, with **Bring back**. A kind is a tag like the area, and **By context** puts these tasks after every priority.

## The month calendar

Open **Month calendar** under the Tasks heading.

- Each day shows how many tasks are still to do, or a tick when all are done.
- Tap a day to see its tasks, tick them, or **Add a task on this day**.
- Each day wears a small mark in its kind of day's colour. In the day's sheet, **Kind of day** sets the kind for that date, past or future. **Follow your week** puts it back.
- **Print this month** gives you a clean paper copy.

## Plan my week

Beside the Tasks heading sits **Plan my week**. It looks at your open tasks with no day and suggests a day for each, in the next seven days, and a time block too.

1. Set **Hours of planned work a day**. It starts at four.
2. Higher priority tasks are placed first, each on the lightest day it fits.
3. **Plan strength** says Strong, Fair or Light. Days with something planned, days that are not overloaded, and tasks with a length all help.
4. **Worth a look** lists anything to fix, like a heavy day, a block that is too full, or a task with no length. Each line has a **Fix**.
5. Each suggestion shows its block under the title, picked from the task's area of life and pillar. A select beside it changes the block.
6. **Add your own** finds any open task by name and plans it here too.
7. The gear at the top opens **Time blocks**, where you shape your days.

Nothing changes until you tap **Keep what fits**. **Skip them all** leaves your week as it was.

## Why it works this way

A list that grows forever stops being useful. Do dates keep today short. Due dates keep deadlines in view without holding you back. The wait between ticks keeps the xp honest.

## What emerges and what develops

After a few weeks, Daily starts to reflect you back. Your streak grows and your pillars level up. On Home, the daily brief notes when you ticked more than the week before, or when tasks run late.

What develops is a skill: planning a day you can actually finish. That small skill, kept up, is what moves the bigger things in your life.

> **Result:** you check in, do your small act, plan your tasks with clear dates, and lay out a week that fits.

## The bottom line

Daily is three small things: check in, do the small act, tick a task. Give each task a do date, a priority and, if you like, an area. Let Plan my week spread the rest.
