# How to keep going
Starting is easy. The hard part is week three, when the newness wears off. This last lesson is a plan for your first week and month, and a look at what grows when you stay.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- A simple rhythm for your first seven days.
- What to do at the end of a week and a month.
- What slowly grows in the app, and in you.
- What to do after a bad week.

## Your first week

Keep it small. The aim is to show up, not to do everything.

1. **Open Daily each day and tap I showed up today.** It pays 10 xp and 10 points, and the button then reads "Checked in, day 1", then day 2, and so on.
2. **Do the small act.** Your weakest pillar gets a two-minute act each day, for 15 xp. It is your easiest win.
3. **Tick one or two tasks.** A task pays 20 xp, and six a day pay. A task cannot be ticked before its do date. After your first three ticks, a task must be ten minutes old, and ten minutes after your last tick.
4. **Pick one thing for your not to do list.** One thing you want to stay away from, like scrolling in bed. Each kept day pays 5 xp, and the run of kept days is the real prize.
5. **Follow Getting started on Home.** For your first 14 days it lists a few first steps. Each one ticks itself when you do the thing.

On the seventh day of your check-in streak you get 50 points more. That bonus comes again every seventh day.

> **Pro tip:** at the start of a week, tap **Plan my week** under Tasks on Daily. It suggests a day for each task that has none, and nothing changes until you tap **Keep what fits**.

## The end of a week

Show **Reviews** in the Reflection group if it is hidden. Then look back.

1. Open the week that just ended. The app brings your numbers from every app you use, next to the week before.
2. Answer the questions one at a time: your projects, pillar by pillar, what went well, what got stuck, and which pillar needs a next task.
3. Tap **Save this review**. It is saved with the numbers as they were.

A task you marked **Bring it to my review** waits under **Tasks you brought**. To make this a habit, tap **Schedule a review**, pick a day and a time, and the inbox reminds you.

## What grows over the weeks

### Your pyramids fill

Each pillar is a pyramid that stands on its point. When you tick a task, its glow runs up through the project, the goal outcome, the value goal and the guiding principle to the foundation. Each layer above gets a little xp too. Finish every task in a project and you get 30 xp more.

### Your streaks and your year

Your check-in streak counts the days in a row. On Home, **Xp, last 14 days** shows your last two weeks. On the Profile tab, **Your year** fills in a grid of days, darker on your busier ones. Only you see it.

### Who you are becoming

Daily and Profile both show **Who you are becoming**. Each form needs xp and time: Seed, then Sprout at 300 xp and 3 days, Climber at 900 xp and 14 days, Steady hand at 2,000 xp and 45 days, and Luminary at 4,000 xp and 120 days. Time is part of it on purpose, so nobody rushes it in a weekend.

### Badges

About sixty badges in ten families arrive by their own count, like First tick, Three in a row and Full week. A badge pays nothing. Once the app gives you one, it never takes it back on its own.

### Patterns on Emerging

After a few weeks, Emerging starts to speak. It might notice a task you keep coming back to and offer **Make it a habit**. It might see that your mood is higher on days you tick "Evening walk". That needs at least five days with the walk and seven without. Until then, it sits under **Still watching**, with what it still needs. Every line says things happen together, never that one causes the other.

### Trust

Your public page carries a trust score. It grows from your xp, points, best streak, time here and more. Nothing in it can be typed in. It only grows by doing.

## The end of your first month

1. **Write the month's review.** It walks your goal outcomes, pillar by pillar, then what came from your effort and what the next month needs.
2. **Look at Life.** The five areas of life always go physical, financial, mental, emotional, spiritual. Each one holds up the next, so a weak one pulls down the one after it. Start with the first weak one.
3. **Check your life map.** On Pillars, if your life has moved, tap **Run the session again**. Or tap **Build the pillars for your focus** when you are ready for more.
4. **Tidy your apps.** Hide what you did not open this month.

## After a bad week

You will miss days. Your streak goes back to zero, and that is all. Your xp, your badges and your pyramids stay.

- Start with the smallest thing: the check-in and the two-minute small act.
- Look at Home's daily brief. Its **Next steps** pick a few things that fit where you are today.
- A slip on your not to do list ends that run. Tick tomorrow and a new run starts.

> **Result:** you have a plan for a week, a month and a bad week. And you know what slowly grows: pyramids, streaks, badges, patterns and trust.

## The bottom line

kreators.app is seven relationships played as a daily game. You set them up once, then show up a little each day. Daily holds the small steps. Pillars hold the big picture. Reflection shows you what is changing, and Connection puts you among people who are growing too. None of it needs to be perfect. Come back tomorrow, tick one thing, and let the rest grow.
