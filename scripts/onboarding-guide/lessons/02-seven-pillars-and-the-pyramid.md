# The seven pillars and the pyramid
Your life is not one thing. It is work, friends, family, love, yourself, the people who teach you, and what you believe in. When one of them is weak, the others feel it. kreators.app gives each of these a place of its own, called a pillar.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- What the seven pillars are, and who guides each one.
- How every pillar is built as a pyramid with six layers.
- What happens when you tick a task.
- How a pillar grows over weeks and months.

## The seven pillars

Open the **Pillars** tab. It is titled **Your seven pillars**, one for each relationship in your life. Each one has a colour and a guide, a small character who looks after it.

- **Servitude**, with Ember, guide to servitude: your work, your money, and the people who count on you.
- **Friends**, with Pip, guide to friends.
- **Family**, with Sunny, guide to family.
- **Love**, with Clover, guide to love.
- **Yourself**, with Dew, guide to yourself: your body, your rest, your mind.
- **Teachers**, with Sage, guide to teachers: the people you learn from.
- **Faith**, with Lumen, guide to faith: your practice and your sense of meaning.

Each guide asks you one plain question. Sunny asks, "Did family hear from you?" Sage asks, "What did you learn on purpose?"

## Why pillars, and not just a to-do list

Most apps start with tasks, and tasks fade in a week. Here, every task hangs from something you care about. When you know why a task matters, it is easier to do on a tired day.

## The pyramid: six layers

Every pillar is a pyramid that stands on its point. The wide layer at the top is the foundation. The small point at the bottom is today's task.

1. **Foundation:** one thing you will not let break, ever. For Family, it might be "Family gets the first call".
2. **Guiding principle:** a line you live by, so the foundation holds.
3. **Value goal:** how you want to feel more often. No numbers, just the feeling.
4. **Goal outcome:** what you would see when that feeling is real.
5. **Project:** a bunch of tasks with a finish line.
6. **Task:** one small thing you can tick off today.

Read it from the top down and it tells you why. Read it from the bottom up and it tells you how.

> **Pro tip:** keep each task small enough to finish in one go. "Call Mum for ten minutes" works better than "Fix things with family".

## Setting up a pillar

There are two ways in.

- **The strategy session**, on the dark card at the top of Pillars. It sets up the pillars you most need to work on, along with a routine and a plan. The next lesson covers it.
- **One pillar at a time.** Tap a pillar card that says "Not asked yet". Its guide asks you three short questions. Then you see what was built from your answers, and you tap **Set up 1 pillar**. You get a foundation, a guiding principle, a value goal, a goal outcome, a project and three tasks.

If you answered the questions but did not set it up, the pillar's page offers the same pyramid again. It also has **Or start by hand with a foundation**, so you can write each layer yourself.

## Inside a pillar

Tap a set-up pillar to open its page.

- **Tasks to do** lists the open tasks for this pillar, with **Add a task** under them. A task needs a project to hang from.
- **The pyramid** shows each layer. Open a layer to see what is there, change it, or add to it. A layer only opens for adding after the one above it has something in it.
- A project with a due day shows its pace: **On track**, **Slipping** or **Behind**. That comes from how many of its tasks are done, set against how much time has gone.

Tap any item to change or remove it. Removing asks first, since it takes everything under it too.

## What happens when you tick a task

This is the heart of the app. When you tick a task on Daily, the glow lands on the task and runs up the pyramid to the foundation.

- The task pays **20 xp**.
- A smaller ripple of xp goes to each layer above it: 10 to the project, 8 to the goal outcome, 6 to the value goal, 4 to the guiding principle and 2 to the foundation.
- Clear every task in a project and you get **30 xp** more.

Xp is progress. It fills your level and your pillars. It is never spent. Up to six tasks a day pay xp. The rest still count as done.

## What emerges over time

As you tick, things start to show.

- **Levels:** each pillar card has a ring that fills as its xp grows. Each level needs a bit more than the last.
- **Your weakest pillar:** the one with the least xp sends your small act on Daily, a two-minute task to help it catch up.
- **Badges:** setting up all seven pillars earns the All seven badge. Others come from clearing projects.
- **Patterns:** after a few weeks, the app may notice a task you keep doing, and whether those days went better. It says what went together, never what caused what.

## What develops

The real change is not in the app. It is in you. A strong foundation makes hard weeks easier, and a clear guiding principle makes choices faster. The pyramid simply makes that growth visible.

You do not need all seven at once. Start with one or two. The others wait until you are ready.

> **Result:** you know what each pillar is for, how its six layers fit, and why a small tick today makes the whole pyramid stronger.

## The bottom line

Seven pillars, one for each relationship. Each is a pyramid, from the foundation at the top to today's task at the point. Tick the task, and the glow runs all the way up.
