# Home: your day on one screen
You open the app with a busy day ahead. You do not want to hunt through ten apps to see where you stand. Home puts the day on one screen. It shows what changed, what to do next, and the numbers that tell you how you are doing.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- What sits at the top of Home, and why it comes in that order.
- How the daily brief and Getting started point the way.
- How Breathe and focus and the line of the day fit in.
- How to read your xp, your level and your Life score.

## Why Home is there

Every app in kreators.app does one job. Home is where they meet. It reads your own numbers and shows them back in a calm order: first where you stand, then what to do.

## The header

Home opens with a dark card. It shows the date and a greeting with your first name. Under the name are three small links:

- **Your level:** opens your pillars.
- **Your streak:** how many days in a row you checked in. It opens Daily.
- **Your mood:** today's face, or "Log your mood" if you have not picked one. It shows while Mood is on.

Beside them sit **My page**, your public page, and a bell for your Inbox. Your picture opens the Profile tab. **Search anything** sits at the foot of the card, with results right under it as you type.

## Water, then a line for the day

Next comes **Water and reminders**. Tap **+ Glass** each time you drink. Three round buttons switch the card between **Water**, **Reminders**, where you add your own, and **Settings**, where you set your goal and your glass.

Under it sits the line of the day. It takes turns: a line from a philosopher one day, then a line written for the app the next. Every line shows once before any comes back. Tap the round **Another line** button to see a new one. It then takes turns between a tip, a small thing to do today, and a line to think about.

## Breathe and focus

Next is **Breathe and focus**, the same panel you find in Mindfulness. It has four tabs: **Breathe**, **Timer**, **Pomodoro** and **Stopwatch**. It shows here while Mindfulness is on. The Mindfulness lesson covers it in full.

## Your daily brief

**Your daily brief** shows two short lists:

- **What changed:** three things that moved in your own numbers.
- **Next steps:** three small things to do next.

The brief can also note a good week of movement, offer a short walk on a day you have not moved, and remind you of your eating check-in and the day's gratitude, each only while its app is on.

The brief runs on plain rules, not AI, so it never costs credits. It never points at an app you have hidden. If you take the Discover quiz and allow it, the steps start to fit how you work.

## Getting started

A new account also sees **Getting started** for its first 14 days. It is a list of seven first steps:

- Check in on Daily.
- Tick your first task.
- Do a small act.
- Pick two interests.
- Choose your username.
- Join a circle, an event or a run club.
- Take the strategy session.

You cannot tick them by hand. Each one ticks itself when you do the thing. Tap the heading, which shows a count like "3 of 7", to fold the list away or open it again. It goes by itself once every step is done, or after 14 days.

> **Pro tip:** do the Getting started steps in order on your first day. Each one opens the place where you do it, so the list doubles as a tour.

## Your numbers

Below the brief, Home shows numbers before actions. Results come first, and everything below is what moves them.

- **Your metrics:** your Life score, your mood and your wellbeing. Tap one for the detail.
- **Your numbers:** your day streak, xp today, your points and your level, with a bar for how far you are into it.
- **Emerging:** up to three patterns from your own logs, or one quiet line and **See all** while nothing is ready. A later lesson covers it.
- **Daily:** today's boxes, to tick right here.
- **Up next:** events, runs and circles you joined.
- **Xp, last 14 days:** pick **By day** for a line with a green dot on each day you ticked a task, or **By source** for a ring of where your xp came from.
- **Your pillars:** a bar for each of the seven.
- **Go to:** every app you have not hidden, as picture tiles: a sideways row on a phone.

Everything on Home that belongs to an app shows only while that app is not hidden.

## Xp and levels

Xp is how the app counts what you do. A check-in pays 10 xp. A small act pays 15. A ticked task pays 20. A line under Your numbers says how much xp you need for the next level. Levels get further apart as you climb, so each one means a little more.

## The Life score

The Life score is one number out of 100. It is the average of your five areas of life: physical, financial, mental, emotional and spiritual. Each area is built from what you already log, like sleep, money, stress, mood and mindful minutes. Only you see it. The next lesson explains why the five areas come in that order.

## What emerges over time

On day one, Home is mostly empty lines. That is fine. Each check-in, task and log fills it in. After a week, the brief has real changes to talk about. After a few weeks, Emerging starts to spot patterns, and your xp line shows the shape of your weeks.

## What develops

Home does not ask you to do more. It shows you what is already true. Over time you learn to read your day at a glance, then act. That habit is what Home builds in you.

> **Result:** in one scroll you drink a glass of water, read a line, start a focus clock, check your brief and tick today's boxes. You never leave Home.

## The bottom line

Home is the day in one place. Read the brief, do one next step, and let the numbers fill in as you go. The more you use the other apps, the more Home has to show you.
