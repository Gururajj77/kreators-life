# Connection: the people around you
Most of what you build here, you build with people. A friend who checks on you. A run club that waits for you on Sunday. The Connection apps are where that happens, and each one asks for a yes before anything moves.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- How to post, react and reply in Community, and when to stay anonymous.
- How Bond turns people you meet into friends.
- How events, runs and circles pay you for showing up in person.
- What the Games app is for.
- Which people make a community work, and how you might become one.

## Find them

**Community**, **Bond**, **Party**, **Kreators Run**, **Circles**, **Club** and **Games** start hidden on a new account, unless the setup chat showed them for you. Open **Apps**, open the **Connection** group, open **Hidden apps** at its end and tap the eye beside each one you want.

## Community: posts and replies

1. Open **Community** and tap **Write a post**.
2. Under **Pick a category**, pick the one that fits, like Creative or Support.
3. Write your post and tap **Create post**.

Under any post you can react with Like, Celebrate, Support, Love, Insightful or Funny. Tap the same one again to take it back. Tap the reply count to answer. Type @ and a username to tell someone. **Show**, **Sort** and **Topic** above the feed help you find things.

Small acts here pay small amounts:

- **A post:** 5 xp, up to three a day.
- **A reply:** 2 xp, up to five a day.
- **A like you give:** 1 xp, up to ten a day.
- **A like on your post:** 1 point for you.
- **A new follower:** 2 points for you.

The pay is small on purpose. The point is to show up, not to farm.

## When to stay anonymous

Some questions are hard to ask with your name on them. Turn on **Post anonymously** before you post. Readers see a made-up name, the same all through that thread. They never see your name, your page or your followers. If someone reports a post, the team can still act on it, so anonymous never means anything goes.

Beside the feed sits **Channels**: chat rooms, one topic each. Join one to write in it, anonymously too if you like.

## Bond: from met to friends

Bond is for the people you actually know.

1. Open **Bond** and find **Your friend code**. It is eight letters and numbers.
2. Share it with a friend. They type it under **Add a friend by code** and tap **Send request**.
3. You say yes, and you are friends.

Chats in Bond always run under two made-up names, even between friends. Bond's own **Channels** are different: small private rooms where names show, and only friends you ask can join.

Under **Introductions**, turn on **Open to introductions** to meet people from events you went to. Your details move only after the other person says yes.

## Blocking

You can block anyone, any time, from a post's **More** menu or under a line in a channel. A block works both ways. Neither of you sees the other's posts, replies or lines, and neither can send the other a request. The other person is not told. Lift it under **People you blocked** with **Unblock**. A block you made from a made-up name is listed under that name, so it never tells you who wrote it.

## Events, runs and circles

- **Party:** meetups, workshops and bigger days. Tap **RSVP**, or **Join the waitlist** when it is full. Anyone can **Host a meetup**.
- **Kreators Run:** run clubs near you. Join one, or **Start a club** of your own. At the start line, tap **Enter the start code** to log your run. **Discussions** has one thread per run, for its people, open until thirty days after it. On your own, **Log a run or walk** under **My runs** needs no club and no code. It counts as movement and pays nothing.
- **Circles:** small groups that meet each week, matched by interests. Everyone at the table has a made-up name.
- **Club:** a membership you apply to. People get in on the quality of their work and how they show up, never on follower counts.

A host can set xp for guests and show an **attendee code** at the event. Type it where it says **The host's code**. An event or circle pays up to 50 xp, once each, for up to three events in any 24 hours. A run pays up to 50 xp and 10 points, two runs a day. This xp feeds your Friends pillar, and a run's minutes count as movement in your Life score.

## Games

**Games** holds nine card games, like FunFlow and the 7RP Challenge. You unlock one with points in **Rewards**, and it is yours for good. Tap **Draw a card** and play with the people around you.

## The people a community needs

A good community does not run itself. It needs people in a few roles:

- **Hosts** who plan a meetup or start a run club.
- **Guides** who keep Community calm and kind.
- **Life coaches** who walk beside someone for a while.
- **Mentors** who give honest feedback in School.
- **Creators** who make things others learn from.
- **Brands** who bring real work and rewards.

Anyone can host a meetup or start a club today. Guide, mentor and life coach are roles you can grow into once people know you. If you coach people already, open **Coaching** and look for **Are you a life coach?** The next lesson walks through coaching.

## What grows over time

Show up and your Friends pillar fills in. Your trust score climbs as your xp and streaks grow. Community badges arrive as you post and reply. And your Friends board on the Leaderboard starts to count you, if you opt in.

> **Pro tip:** start with one place, not seven. Join one channel or one run club and show up there every week. A small habit with people beats a big burst you cannot keep.

> **Result:** you have a friend or two in Bond, a room where you can ask for help, and a reason to leave the house each week.

## The bottom line

Connection here is built on consent. Friends need a yes, introductions need a yes, and blocks need none. Start small, show up often, and the people around you become part of your life, not just your feed.
