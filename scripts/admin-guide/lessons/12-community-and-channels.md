# Community and channels: posts, reactions and guides
Riya edits videos all day, mostly alone. She wants a place to ask a question, share a win and help someone newer. Community is that place, and guides keep it kind.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- How Riya writes her first post and earns from it.
- How reactions, replies, mentions and follows work.
- How anonymous posts stay anonymous.
- How channels work beside the feed.
- How a guide holds, pins, hides and mutes, and how you make one.

## Switch it on

Community is off on a new account. Riya opens **Apps**, taps the **Connection** group and turns on the switch beside **Community**.

## Step by step: Riya's first post

1. Riya opens **Community**. It opens on **Feed**, with **Channels** beside it.
2. She taps **Write a post**.
3. Under **Pick a category** she taps **Creative**. The others are Self care, Mindfulness, Stories, Support, Learning, Stress, Affirmation and Awareness.
4. Under **Your post** she writes: "Finished my first brand edit in Pune this week. What do you charge for a reel?"
5. She leaves **Post anonymously** off, so her name shows.
6. She taps **Create post**.

## What a post pays

- **A post:** 5 xp, up to three a day.
- **A reply:** 2 xp, up to five a day.
- **A reaction you give:** 1 xp, up to ten a day.
- **A reaction on your post:** a point for you.
- **A new follower:** two points, up to twenty points a day from both.

Each thing pays once. If Riya changes her reaction, nothing more is paid. The full list is under **How posts pay** on the cover.

## Reactions, replies and mentions

- **React:** tap **Pick a reaction** under a post and choose Like, Celebrate, Support, Love, Insightful or Funny. Tapping the same one again takes it back.
- **Reply:** tap the speech bubble with the reply count. A sheet opens. Type in **Write a reply** and tap **Send**. Tap **Reply** under someone's reply to answer them. Answers nest up to four levels deep.
- **Mention:** type @ and a page name, like @arjun_cuts. That person is told. A post can tell up to five people.
- **Follow:** open a post's **More** menu and pick **Follow the post** to hear about new replies. The writer and everyone who replies follow it on their own.

Use **Show**, **Sort** and **Topic** above the feed to filter. Show is Everyone, Following or Mine. Sort is Recent, Popular or Most replies.

## Anonymous posts

Riya wants advice on a client who will not pay. She does not want her name on it.

1. She taps **Write a post** and picks **Support**.
2. She ticks **Post anonymously**.
3. She taps **Create post**.

Readers see a made-up name, the same all through that one thread and different in the next. They never see her name, page, followers or rank. An anonymous post never shows under Following, on a public page, or in a search by name. Staff keep the link to her account, so a report can be acted on. She can still delete her own post, because the app knows it is hers.

## Channels

Channels are chat rooms, one topic each, beside the feed.

1. Riya taps **Channels**. A dot shows rooms with lines she has not read.
2. She opens a room and taps **Join** with the room's name.
3. She writes in the box at the bottom. She can tick **Post anonymously** here too.
4. Under any line, **Reply** answers it. **More** gives **Report**, **Block** and, for a guide, **Mute**.

**Leave** takes her out, and she can join again later.

Only a guide opens a room. A guide taps **New channel**, fills in **Name** and **What it is for (optional)**, and taps **Make the channel**. Inside a room, a guide has **Edit** and **Close channel**. A closed room can still be read, and **Open again** brings it back. Guides can tick **Show closed channels** in the list.

## Guides and ranks

A guide is anyone who holds the **Moderation** permission. A creator can hold it, so Riya can become one.

There are four ranks: member, guide, staff and admin. A guide can mute or hide only someone of a lower rank. Undoing it needs the same rank or a higher one. So a guide acts on members, staff act on guides and members, and an admin acts on everyone except another admin.

## What a guide does

Once Riya is a guide, she sees more on the Feed.

- **For guides:** a switch, **Hold a new member's first post until a guide says okay**. It is off by default. While it is on, a new member's first post is seen only by them until a guide taps **Say okay**. Only then does it pay, and its mentions are told.
- **Pin:** in a post's **More** menu. Up to three posts are pinned at the top of each category.
- **Hide:** in **More**, asked first in a sheet. The post leaves the feed for everyone, and **Unhide** brings it back.
- **Mute:** in **More**, asked first. The person can read but cannot post or reply. **Muted members** lists them, and **Lift** ends a mute.
- **Take down:** in a channel, under a line. A takedown by someone else is audited and wipes the words.

A report on a channel line becomes a help ticket with the line's words. Every post, reply and line shows web addresses as plain text, never as links.

> **Pro tip:** before making someone a guide, read a few of their posts. A good guide is calm, replies often, and never argues in public.

> **Result:** Riya posts, reacts and earns small amounts each day, asks a hard question without her name, and, once trusted, helps keep Community kind.

## What the admin checks

1. **Make a guide:** open **Admin**, then **People**. Search for Riya and tap her row. Under **Permissions**, tap **Moderation**. Taking a permission away asks first in a sheet.
2. **The queue:** a staff member with Moderation sees a **Moderation** tile on **Manage** that opens Community. A creator guide does all of it from Community itself.
3. **Switches:** in **Admin**, **Flags**, the Community row sets **On**, **Minimum role**, **Rollout %** and **Overrides**. In **People**, **What does this person see?** sets it for one person.
4. **Audit:** every role and permission change is in **Audit**.

## The bottom line

Community works when people feel safe to speak. Teach members to post, react and reply, and to use anonymous posts for the hard things. As an admin, choose guides with care, give them Moderation, and let the rank rules keep every guide in their lane.
