# Home: your day at a glance
Riya opens kreators.app on a busy morning. She has two edits due and a Reel to post. She does not want to hunt through apps. Home puts the day on one screen.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- What sits at the top of Home, and in what order.
- How Riya sets up water and her own reminders.
- How Breathe and focus, the daily brief and Getting started work.
- How to read xp and Up next, and what an admin can change.

## Step one: the header

Home opens with a dark card. It shows the date, "Hi, Riya!" and three quick links: her level, her streak and today's mood. Beside them:

- **My page:** opens her public page.
- **The bell:** opens her Inbox, with a count of what is unread.
- **Her picture:** opens the Profile tab.
- **Search anything:** a search box. Results show right under it as she types: apps, help, her tasks, her journal, people and more.

## Step two: water and reminders

Next is **Water and reminders**. Riya taps **+ Glass** each time she drinks. The card opens into three tabs.

1. **Water:** a glass that fills up, the count against her goal, and the last seven days.
2. **Settings:** her **Daily goal**, the size of **A glass**, **How often** (every two or four hours) and the hours, **From** and **To**. **Remind me to drink** sends notes to her Inbox until the goal is met.
3. **Reminders:** her own reminders. She taps **+ Add a reminder**, types "Back up the drive" under **Remind me to**, sets a time and picks the **Days**. Each one lands in her Inbox on time.

The Reminders tab also lists her tasks tagged Remember.

> **Pro tip:** a water note never stacks up. One line a day is rewritten with the new count, and it is cleared once the day is over.

## Step three: the line of the day

Under it sits one short line for the day. It takes turns: a line from a philosopher, then a line written for the app. Every line is shown once before any comes back. It costs nothing to set up. It is just there.

## Step four: Breathe and focus

Next is **Breathe and focus**, the same panel as in Mindfulness. It has four tabs.

1. **Breathe:** breathe with circles that fill. Calm exhale is the first pattern. A finished breath logs mindful minutes.
2. **Timer:** one countdown, no breaks.
3. **Pomodoro:** focus parts with breaks. Presets like Classic 25/5 live in the settings sheet.
4. **Stopwatch:** counts up from zero.

Riya picks Pomodoro, sets 50 minutes on the ruler and taps **Start**. The clock keeps going while she moves around the app. A small pill in the corner shows the time left.

## Step five: the daily brief and Getting started

**Your daily brief** shows three things that changed and three next steps, from her own numbers. The steps never point at an app she has off. It runs on set steps, so it never spends credits.

Under it, a new account sees **Getting started**. It lists seven first steps:

- Check in on Daily.
- Tick your first task.
- Do a small act.
- Pick two interests.
- Choose your username.
- Join a circle, an event or a run club.
- Take the strategy session.

Each one ticks only when Riya does the thing. It pays nothing. She can fold it into a small toggle, and it goes by itself after fourteen days.

## Step six: the numbers

Below that, Home shows her numbers before any actions:

- **Your metrics:** her Life score, mood and wellbeing. She taps one for the detail.
- **Daily:** today's boxes, to tick right here.
- **Up next:** events, runs and circles she joined. With nothing booked, it says where to find them.
- **Xp, last 14 days:** **By day** shows a line, with a green dot for a day she ticked a task. **By source** shows a ring of what the xp came from.
- **Your pillars:** a bar for each of the seven.
- **Go to:** every app she has switched on.

Some parts show only when their app is on, like Life, Knowledge and Money.

> **Result:** in one scroll, Riya drinks a glass, starts a 50-minute focus part, reads her brief and ticks her check-in. She never left Home.

## What an admin checks

Home is core and always on. What shows on it follows the apps that are on.

1. **Flags:** in **Admin**, the **Flags** tab switches an app for everyone, for a share of people, or for one person. If the team turns Mindfulness off for everyone, Breathe and focus leaves Home.
2. **One person:** in **People**, tap Riya's row, then use **What does this person see?** to allow or block an app for her alone.
3. **For the team:** staff see an extra block in their own brief, **For the team**, about work waiting for them.
4. **Up next:** it fills from Party, Kreators Run and Circles. If those are off, there is nothing to book.
5. **Water and reminders:** these notes are sent by the server's timer. Every timer run that did something shows under **Logs**, which only a super admin reads.
6. **The line of the day:** it lives in the app's code. It cannot be changed from Admin.

> **Pro tip for admins:** before you switch an app off for everyone, open **Observability**. It shows who is here and which apps are on.

## The bottom line

Home is the day in one place: water, a line, a focus clock, a brief and the numbers. Riya sets it up once and it keeps up with her. As an admin, you shape Home through the apps you switch on and off.
