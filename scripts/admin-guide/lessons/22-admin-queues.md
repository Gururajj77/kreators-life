# Admin: the queues

Riya now sells a short editing course, reserved a Kreators tee with her points, wants her payouts and hosts a meet-up for editors. Each of those lands in a queue the team works through. This lesson walks one week of Riya's life through every queue.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- Where each queue lives and what each button does.
- Which ones ask first, and which cannot be undone.
- Where the help queue and Community tools sit outside Admin.

## Where the queues are

Most queues are tabs in Admin: **Listings, Redemptions, Orders, KYC, Life coaches, Discount codes, Attendee codes, Agency templates and Webhooks.** Every list is a table you can sort, search, filter and page through. Tick rows to act on many at once.

Staff without the Admin role use **Manage** instead. **Your queues** shows a tile for each permission they hold, with how many are waiting.

## Listings

Listings are what the public site shows on Explore and each feature page.

1. On the **Listings** tab, the buttons start with **New**: New event, New program, New tool and so on.
2. Make one, then turn **Live** on in its row to publish it.
3. Turn **Featured** on to lift it to the top.
4. Tick several and use **Publish, Hide, Feature or Unfeature**.

Brands make their own campaigns public from Army, so there is no New campaign here.

## Redemptions

Riya spends points on a Kreators tee. Gear stays a reservation until a drop ships, and her points are held until then.

1. Open **Redemptions**. The tab shows how many wait.
2. When the tee ships, tick her row and tap **Mark fulfilled**.
3. If it cannot ship, tap **Cancel and refund**. A sheet asks first, and her points go back.

Card games are not here. They unlock at once when redeemed.

## Orders and refunds

A buyer of Riya's course asks for their money back.

1. Open **Orders** and search by order, payment, buyer or page.
2. Tap the row to see its timeline and the provider's events.
3. Type a **Refund reason**.
4. Tap **Refund in full**. The sheet says the amount and asks again: **Yes, refund it** or **Keep the order**.

A refund cannot be undone. A money order becomes paid only after the payment provider's signed message arrives. The **Webhooks** tab lists every one of those messages, and **Replay** runs one again.

## KYC

Riya wants payouts, so she fills **Payout details** on her Shop's Customers screen.

1. Open **KYC**. Only the last digits of her PAN and bank account are stored.
2. Check that the bank account and PAN match her legal name.
3. If she should hear why, type a line under **Note to them** first.
4. Tap **Approve** or **Reject**.

She sees the note beside her payout details.

## Discount codes

Riya's course launch gets a code from the team.

1. Open **Discount codes** and tap **Make a code**.
2. Under **What it takes off**, pick a percent or an amount.
3. Under **Where it works**, pick the places, like Shop products. Use **Only one item** to tie it to her course.
4. Set **Times it can be used**, **Times per person**, **First day** and **Last day**.
5. Add a **Note for the team** and tap **Make the code**.

The platform pays the discount, so Riya still gets her full price. A code's word and worth never change; a new deal is a new code. **Switch off** stops one at once.

## Attendee codes

Riya hosts a Party meet-up. Guests claim xp with a code.

1. Open **Attendee codes**. It lists Party, Run and Circles events with guests, soonest first.
2. Set the **Xp each**, up to 50, until the code opens thirty minutes before the start.
3. Set a **Code**, or tick rows and tap **New random codes**.

Only the event's own guests can use the code, once each. The host is told about your change.

## Life coaches

Riya's friend asks to become a life coach.

1. Open **Life coaches**. **Asked to be a life coach** lists the asks.
2. Tick a row and tap **Make life coach**, or **Say no**.
3. Under **Life coaches**, **Take the badge** ends every link of theirs, tells their students and closes their invite link.
4. **Every link** lists them all, with **End**.

To link a coach and a student, use **Connect people** (see Admin: People). It offers **Ask them** or **Link now**. Link now only if the student already agreed.

Making someone a life coach also switches Coaching on for them. **Link now** starts with Patterns off, since the student never saw that switch. A student who says yes sees all six and picks. A student can have up to three coaches.

## Agency templates

These are house templates every Agency user can copy. A super admin saves one from their own paper with **Save as house template**. It never carries who it was for, and carries business details only when **Include my business details** is on. Turn **Active** off to retire one.

## The help queue

Help requests sit on the **Help** screen, under **Open tickets**, for anyone with the Support permission. Each ticket has **Send reply**.

## Community tools

Guides are anyone with the Moderator permission. On Community:

- **For guides** has the switch **Hold a new member's first post until a guide says okay**. A held post waits with **Say okay**.
- A post's More menu has **Pin** (up to three a topic), **Hide** and **Mute**.
- Hide and Mute ask first. You can only mute someone of a lower rank.

## What an admin checks

- **The Audit tab:** who changed what, with the before and after.
- **Ops:** funnels counted from the app's own tables, and the **Email outbox**.

> **Pro tip:** clear Redemptions and KYC first each week. Those are the queues where a real person is waiting on points or money.

> **Result:** Riya's tee ships, her refund is fair, her payouts are checked and her meet-up pays its guests.

## The bottom line

Each queue has one job and plain buttons. Anything that takes back money, points or power asks first, and every change lands in the audit log.
