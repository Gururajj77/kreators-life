# Party, Run and Circles: events, codes and meeting people
Riya spends her days at a screen. She wants to meet creators in Pune, and maybe run again. Party, Kreators Run and Circles get people out of the house, and one rule ties them together: the attendee code.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- How Riya hosts a meetup and checks guests in.
- How attendee codes pay xp, and who can see them.
- How a run club works, from the start code to Discussions.
- How Circles seat people under aliases.
- What the team runs, and what an admin checks.

## Switch them on

All three are off on a new account. Riya opens **Apps**, taps the **Connection** group and turns on **Party**, **Kreators Run** and **Circles**.

## Step by step: Riya hosts a meetup

1. She opens **Party** and taps **Host a meetup**.
2. Under **What kind** she picks **Meetup**. Party, Workshop, Camp and Kreators Day are run by the team.
3. She fills in **Title** ("Editors' chai, Koregaon Park"), **What to expect**, **Starts** and **Minutes**.
4. She types a **Venue**. Left empty, it is an online event with an **Online link**.
5. She sets **Seats** to 12. Blank means no limit.
6. She leaves **Price in points** at 0.
7. Under **Who can see it** she picks **Everyone**. The other choices are **Club members** and **People with the link**.
8. Under **Xp for each guest, optional**, she types 20 in **Xp** and leaves **Your own code** empty for a random one.
9. She taps **Publish event**.

Guests get a reminder a day and an hour before.

## Guests sign up

Arjun opens the event. He ticks **Introduce me to other guests** and taps **RSVP**. A priced event reads **RSVP for** and the points. A full room reads **Join the waitlist**. If a seat opens, the first person waiting gets it and a note. **Cancel my RSVP** gives back any points paid.

## The attendee code

Only the host, events staff and admins ever see the code.

- **When it works:** from thirty minutes before the start to three hours after the end.
- **Who can use it:** only the event's own guests, once each.
- **How often:** up to three events in any 24 hours per person.
- **The xp:** up to 50. It is fixed once the code opens. The code itself can change any time.

On the event page, Riya's card shows the code, the xp and "0 of 12 guests claimed it". **Set xp**, **Use it** (her own word, four to twelve letters or digits) and **New random code** change it. At the door she shows the code. Arjun types it in **The host's code** and taps **Get 20 xp**. A Party claim also checks him in. Riya can also tap **Check in** beside a guest's name.

## After the event

- **Mark done** closes it. **Cancel event** asks first, then tells everyone.
- **Run it again** opens a date field and **Make the new event**. The xp carries over with a fresh code. Guests do not.
- Guests rate it under **How was it?** and tap **Send feedback**.
- Under **Photos**, the host shares one folder link with **Share the folder**. It must be Google Drive or Photos, Dropbox, iCloud, OneDrive or WeTransfer.
- **Meet other guests** lists those who ticked **List me so other guests can connect with me**. **Connect** sends a friend request.

## Kreators Run

Riya starts a club.

1. She opens **Kreators Run** and taps **Start a club**.
2. She fills in **Name**, **City**, **Pace**, **When and where you usually meet** and **About the club**.
3. Under **Who can find it** she picks **Everyone** or **People with the link**.
4. She taps **Start the club**. She is its host and first member.
5. On the club page she taps **Add a run**, sets **Title**, **Starts**, **Minutes**, **Kilometres** and **Meeting point**, and taps **Add the run**. **Xp for each runner** is 20 unless she sets it, up to 50.

Each run gets a start code only the host sees: six letters and numbers, or a word she picks. A member taps **Enter the start code**, types it in **Log this run**, and adds kilometres and minutes. A logged run pays its xp and 10 points, up to two paid runs a day. Its minutes count as movement in Life. Riya never logs her own run.

The app has four sections: **Clubs**, **My runs**, **Brand collabs** and **Discussions**.

- **My runs:** kilometres over thirty days, a weekly streak, and badges.
- **Brand collabs:** posted by events staff or a brand's own team. Only a host applies, with **Apply with a club**.
- **Discussions:** one per run, for its host, club members and runners who logged it. Open for thirty days after the run. A line can be anonymous.

## Circles

Circles are small weekly tables, run by the team.

1. Riya opens **Circles**, finds this week's in Pune and taps **Join**.
2. A sheet asks **How will you show up?**: Single (wear something red), Committed (something white) or Rather not say.
3. She taps **Join this week's circle**. Signups close thirty-six hours before.

Two days before, tables are set from interests that fit. Everyone has an alias and keeps it. The table chat opens a day before, and they agree on the place. At the meetup she types the code on her table page, from thirty minutes before to five hours after the start.

Staff with the Events permission see **Circles you run**. They tap **Open a circle**, fill in **Title**, **City**, **Area (optional)**, **Starts**, **People per table** and **Venue hint**, and can turn on the weekly switch. **Open it** saves it. **Set the tables now** works once three people sign up. A circle's host sees counts, never names.

> **Pro tip:** set the xp when you make the event. Once the code opens, thirty minutes before the start, the xp is fixed.

> **Result:** Riya hosts a chai meetup with a code at the door, runs on Sundays with a club she started, and sits at a weekly table under an alias.

## What the admin checks

1. **Event hosts:** give **Events** in **Admin**, **People**, under **Permissions**. Events needs the Staff role.
2. **Codes:** **Admin**, **Attendee codes** lists every Party, Run and Circles event with guests, from a week ago on. You can set any xp or code by the same rules. The host is told, and it is audited.
3. **Switches:** in **Flags**, each app's row sets **On**, **Minimum role**, **Rollout %** and **Overrides**.

If a code fails, check the time window and the guest list.

## The bottom line

Events pay only when people show up. Teach hosts to set xp early and show the code at the door. As an admin, give Events to the people who run the big days, and use Attendee codes when a host needs help.
