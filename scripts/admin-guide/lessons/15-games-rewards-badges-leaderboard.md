# Games, Rewards, badges and the Leaderboard
Riya has been checking in for three weeks. Her points are piling up. What are they for? This lesson covers the four places that turn showing up into something real: Rewards, the Games app, badges and the Leaderboard.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- How Riya earns points and spends them in Rewards.
- How a card game is unlocked and played.
- How badges are earned and which ones stay private.
- How the Leaderboard works and how to opt in.
- What an admin can give by hand, and how to handle a reservation.

## Points and xp

These are two different things.

- **Xp:** shows effort. Ticks, posts and events pay it. It moves your level and the Leaderboard.
- **Points:** can be spent. A check-in pays 10 points, and every seventh day in a row pays 50 extra. Likes on your posts and new followers pay a few more.

Riya sees both on the **Profile** tab, under **Points**, with two buttons: **Rewards** and **Rank**.

## Step by step: Riya's first reward

1. On the **Profile** tab, under **Points**, she taps **Rewards**.
2. **Your balance** shows her points at the top.
3. She scrolls the **Catalog**. Its shelves are Books, Games, Apparel and wearables, Tech accessories, Drinkware, Office and stationery, Lifestyle and wellness, Novelty and fun, and Experiences.
4. Under **Books** she finds The Relationship Method, 2,000 points. She taps **Reserve 2,000 pts**.
5. A sheet asks again before any points move. She says yes.
6. The book now shows under **Your reservations**, above the catalog.

Gear is a reservation until a drop ships. **Cancel** on a reservation gives the points back, and asks first.

## Card games

The **Games** shelf holds nine card games, such as FunFlow, HealFlow and the 7RP Challenge. A game is different from gear.

- **It opens at once:** the game is hers for good. Nothing is posted.
- **It cannot be cancelled:** the points are spent.
- **It may have a lock:** some games need some xp first. A locked one shows a padlock and says what is missing, like "Unlocks at 500 xp".

Riya unlocks the 7RP Challenge for 1,000 points.

1. She taps **Unlock 1,000 pts** and says yes in the sheet.
2. The **Games** app switches itself on.
3. She opens **Games**. Her game wears **Yours**, and **Play** opens it.

## Step by step: playing a game

1. At the card table, she picks a deck from the row of decks.
2. She taps **Draw a card**. Each tap on the pile deals another.
3. She taps a card to flip it. Some cards have two sides, like Truth and Dare.
4. She drags a card to move it. The card she touches comes to the front.
5. **Shuffle**, **Flip all** and **Clear table** sit beside Draw a card.
6. **Focus mode**, the button with four corners, hides the menu and fills the screen. Escape leaves it.

## Badges

Badges are medals the app gives by its own count. Riya finds them on the **Profile** tab, under **Badges**.

- **Ten families:** Daily habits, Pillars, Focus, Reflection, Community, Creator, Money, Fitness, Learning and Milestones.
- **Four tiers:** bronze, silver, gold and platinum.
- **One rule each:** a single count reaching a number, like ten projects cleared.
- **Kept for good:** once earned, a badge is never taken back.
- **Private families:** Reflection, Focus and Money show only to her. They never appear on her page, the Leaderboard count or an unlock.

Tapping a badge opens a sheet with its next step and a bar. A badge pays nothing. Some products, store items, tools and games ask for a badge before they open.

Honors are different. The team gives them, or time in the app unlocks them. They show under **Awarded**.

## The Leaderboard

The Leaderboard is on for everyone, but nobody shows on it until they say so.

1. On the **Profile** tab, under **Profile**, Riya ticks **Show me on the leaderboard**.
2. She taps **Save profile**.
3. She opens **Leaderboard** (or **Rank** under Points).
4. Under **Board** she picks Everyone, By goal, By pillar, By city, By who they are or By experience.
5. Under **Rank by** she picks All time, This month, Streak or Badges.

Her spot and her xp sit at the top. Below are **Boards by goal** and **Boards by pillar**, each with its top three. A pillar board ranks only the xp earned in that pillar.

The rules:

- **Opt in and setup:** she shows only after she opts in and finishes setup.
- **Blocks:** nobody she blocked, and nobody who blocked her, is listed.
- **Never counted:** journal and wellbeing never count, and her date of birth is never used.
- **Seasons:** each month is a season. Its top three get the **Season top three** honor.

> **Pro tip:** tell new members to check in daily for a week before they spend. The seventh day pays the bonus, and a card game is often their best first reward.

> **Result:** Riya reserved a book, plays a card game with friends, has her first badges, and sees where she stands among editors in Pune.

## What the admin checks

1. **Reservations:** **Admin**, **Redemptions** lists what people reserved. Points are held until you tick rows and pick **Mark fulfilled**, or **Cancel and refund**, which asks first.
2. **Give by hand:** in **People**, tap a row. Under **Points**, **XP** and **Badges given by hand**, type an amount or pick a badge, then **Add** or **Give**. A minus in XP takes some away, down to zero. Under **Honors**, pick one and **Grant**. Each keeps a note in the audit log.
3. **Taking back:** a badge you gave can be removed, which asks first. One the person earned is never taken away.
4. **Super admins:** an admin is never locked out of a game, product, item or tool.
5. **Switches:** in **Flags**, the Games row sets **On**, **Minimum role**, **Rollout %** and **Overrides**.

## The bottom line

Points and badges reward showing up, not gaming the system. Teach members to opt in to the Leaderboard if they want, and to spend points on things they will use. As an admin, fulfil reservations on time and give by hand only with a note.
