# Reviews: looking back by period
Riya worked hard last month. But did she move the things that matter, or only the urgent ones? A review answers that. The app brings the numbers from every app she uses. She brings the words.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- How Riya picks a period and writes her first review.
- How templates give each kind of review its own questions.
- How the review calendar reminds her on the day.
- How a task is brought to a review, and how business metrics work.
- What an admin can check.

## Switch it on

Reviews start off on a new account. Riya opens **Apps**, taps the **Reflection** group and turns on the switch beside **Reviews**.

## The four periods

A review covers one period:

- **Week:** seven days, Monday to Sunday. The built-in one is the Weekly review.
- **Month:** a calendar month, the Monthly review.
- **Quarter:** three months, the Quarterly review.
- **Half year:** six months, the Mid-year check-in.

Each period has one review per person. A period that has not started yet cannot be reviewed.

## Step by step: Riya's first monthly review

1. Riya opens **Reviews** and uses the period picker at the top to choose Month and last month.
2. The hero shows the period, with her xp, tasks done and check-ins.
3. Under **The numbers**, each tile sits next to the month before. She taps a tile to go where it comes from.
4. **Day by day** shows her xp per day, and a ring of xp by pillar.
5. Under **Your review**, she answers the questions. Short answers are fine.
6. Under **How did it go?** she taps a face.
7. She taps **Save this review**.

The review is saved with the numbers as they were. New data or a changed template never rewrites it. She can change her words and save again. Her saved reviews show under **Past reviews**.

## Templates

The built-in template for each period stays, and cannot be edited or removed. Riya can add her own.

1. Under **Templates**, she taps **New template**.
2. In **Name** she types "Money month".
3. In **Comes back every** she picks Month.
4. In **Questions, one per line** she writes "What did I earn?" and "What did I waste?"
5. She taps **Add template**.

Next month, a **Questions from** box lets her choose between the built-in questions and hers. She can keep up to twelve templates.

## The review calendar

1. Under **Review calendar**, Riya taps **Schedule a review**, or taps a day on the calendar.
2. Under **Which review** she picks Month.
3. She sets **First day** and **Time**, and an optional **Name**.
4. Under **Repeat** she picks Every month. It can also be Every day, Weekdays, Every week on chosen days or Every year, with an end day.
5. She taps **Schedule it**.

On that day, at that time, one note lands in her inbox. On the first day of a period, it opens the one that just ended. A bell on the calendar is a reminder. A tick is a review she wrote. Each scheduled review has a **Remind me** switch, and removing one asks first in a sheet. Reviews she wrote stay. She can schedule up to twenty.

## Bring a task to the review

Riya has a task, "Raise my day rate", that she wants to think about at month end.

1. On Daily, she opens the task's plan.
2. She turns on **Bring it to my review**.
3. In that month's review, it shows under **Tasks you brought**, done or still open.
4. She taps **Add to my answer**. It adds a line to her answer and never overwrites it.

A repeating task carries the flag to the next one.

## Business metrics

Riya runs her freelance work like a small studio, so she uses this.

1. Below The numbers on Reviews, under **Business metrics**, she turns on **Show business metrics**. It is off until she does.
2. She taps **Add a metric**.
3. In **Where does the number come from**, she picks **I type it in**, or a number read from Agency, such as Pieces published, Approved first time, Published on time, Billable hours, Utilisation, Invoiced, Paid or New clients.
4. She names it, sets a target and says whether more or less is better.
5. She taps **Add this metric**.

Each metric then sits against its target for the period on screen. She can keep up to twelve.

> **Pro tip:** schedule the weekly review for Sunday evening and the monthly one for the first of the month. The note opens the right period, so it takes one tap to start.

> **Result:** Riya has a review for every month, the same questions each time, a reminder on the day, and the tasks she flagged waiting for her.

## What the admin checks

- **Private words:** a review is the owner's. Journal and wellbeing numbers in it go only to its owner.
- **Coaches:** a coach sees saved reviews only while the student's Reflection switch is on, and never the snapshot of numbers.
- **Business metrics:** the switch is the person's own. While it is off, the server refuses everything but the switch. The Agency numbers come from that person's own Agency records.
- **Reminders:** the cron sends each reminder once. A day missed while nothing ran is skipped, never sent late.
- **Switches:** in Admin, **Flags**, the Reviews row sets On, Minimum role, Rollout % and Overrides. In **People**, **What does this person see?** sets it for one person.

If someone says a reminder did not come, check that the scheduled review's **Remind me** switch is on and its time is in their own zone.

## The bottom line

Reviews turn a busy month into a clear look back. Teach people to pick a period, answer a few questions and schedule the next one. As an admin, keep Reviews switched on for those who want them, and remember the words are theirs alone.
