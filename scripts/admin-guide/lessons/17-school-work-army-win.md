# School, Work, Army and Win: learning and earning
Riya is good, but she wants to be better and paid more. Four apps in the Contribution group help: School to learn, Work to find gigs, Army for brand campaigns and Win for content rewards. This lesson follows her through each, then shows what the team runs behind them.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- How Riya enrols in a cohort and hands in work.
- How the team makes a program and sets its state.
- How Work, Army and Win move from brief to payment.
- Which permission runs each step, and what an admin checks.

## School: Riya the learner

School, Work and Army show on a new account. Win starts hidden.

1. Riya opens **School**. Under **Programs** she finds Editing fundamentals.
2. Beside a cohort date she taps **Enrol**. A program priced in points asks again in a sheet before spending.
3. Under **Your cohorts** she taps **Open**.
4. Under **Lessons** she opens each one in order. A lesson shows its words, then its ways in at its foot, up to six: **Download resource**, **Watch video**, **Open resource** or a book. A video plays right in the lesson.
5. Under **Assignments** she reads the brief, adds **Link to your work** or writes in **Or write it here**, and taps **Hand in**.
6. Under **Live sessions**, **I am here** checks her in while a session is live. Check-in opens fifteen minutes before.
7. In **Discussion** she starts a thread with **Start a thread** and **Post**. She picks Private, so her handle stays hidden, or Public, so others can open her page.

A mentor leaves feedback and taps **Approve** or **Ask for changes**. Completion is the cohort manager's decision against the criteria, never a timer. When the manager marks her complete, the cohort page says Completed. Well done.

## School: the team's side

Making programs needs the **Cohorts** permission (cohort manager). Giving feedback needs **Mentor**, which a creator like Riya can hold.

1. A cohort manager taps **New program** on School.
2. They fill in **Name**, **Description**, **Price in points**, **How it runs**, **What people leave with** and **What they need before starting**.
3. Under **Who can see it** they pick Draft, Private or Public, then tap **Create program**.
4. In the program's menu: **Add lesson**, **Add assignment** and **Open a cohort**. Whoever opens a cohort becomes its manager, scheduling sessions, giving feedback and marking completion.
5. Whoever may edit a program changes it in place: its name and words, and a lesson's title, kind, words and order. They add and remove lessons too.
6. **Add a resource**, at a lesson's foot, takes up to six: a YouTube or Vimeo video, a book, a file or another link, each with an optional **Label**. **Change resources** moves or changes one, and removes it on a second tap. A lesson from one of the founder's books shows **The book** until it has a resource, so add the book too to keep it.
7. An admin moves a program with **Show first**, **Move up** and **Move down**. Every change is audited.

A program has four states:

- **Draft:** only its maker, its cohort managers and admins see it.
- **Private:** only invited people see it and enrol. Its maker picks **Invite people** and adds an email, @username or friend code.
- **Public:** listed on School and Explore.
- **Archived:** hidden; no one new enrols, and people already in keep their cohort.

The menu moves it with **Make it public**, **Make it private (invite only)**, **Move it back to draft** and **Archive**. Taking it off the public list or archiving asks first. Only an archived program shows **Delete for good**, which asks once more and takes its cohorts, lessons, hand-ins and discussions with it.

## Work

1. Riya opens **Work** and finds a gig from a brand.
2. She taps **Apply**, writes **A short note** and taps **Send application**. Her public portfolio goes with it, one application per person.
3. Each change, from submitted to shortlisted, selected or declined, lands in her inbox. **Withdraw** takes it back, after a sheet asks.

Brands and staff with **Talent** tap **Post one**. The form ends in **Save as draft**. **Publish** from **Your listings** puts it live, **Close** ends it, and **Back to draft** pulls it back. **Club only** shows it to Club members first. On each applicant, the poster picks reviewing, shortlisted, selected or rejected, with an optional **Note to send with a decision**.

## Army

Army is brand campaigns run properly.

1. A brand taps **New brand**. Its owner can add teammates by email.
2. They tap **New campaign** and fill in the brief, budget, dates, **Usage rights**, **Exclusivity** and **Restrictions**, then **Send to the team**.
3. The team moves it under **Move it along**: New lead, Brief written, Scope and budget, Creators shortlisted, Approved by brand, In production, In review, Delivered, then Reported and invoiced. The brand can approve the shortlist or cancel.
4. Staff tap **Assign** to offer Riya a role and a fee.
5. Riya taps **Upload a version** with a link. The brand taps **Approve** or **Request changes** with a comment.

**Show on the public site** lists a campaign under Campaigns. The brief itself stays private.

## Win

Win is content rewards: a brand funds a pool of points and pays per thousand verified views.

1. Riya unhides **Win**, opens a reward and reads the brief.
2. **What could I earn?** shows the payout for any view count.
3. She posts her video, taps **Enter**, pastes the link and taps **Submit my video**.
4. YouTube views are read by the app. Other platforms are typed in and confirmed by a reviewer, one report every six hours.

A reviewer types **Confirmed views** and taps **Verify** or **Reject**. Finance then taps **Pay** with the amount, which asks first. Nothing pays on a claim. A pool that runs dry pays nobody more. If a reward changes after launch, its version goes up and everyone who entered is told.

> **Pro tip:** before giving someone Mentor, enrol them in a cohort yourself and read their feedback on one hand-in.

> **Result:** Riya finished a cohort, got shortlisted for a gig, delivered an Army campaign and earned points from a Win reward, each step with someone checking it.

## What the admin checks

1. **Permissions:** in **Admin**, **People**, tap a row. **Cohorts**, **Talent**, **Review** and **Finance** need the Staff role. **Mentor** can go to a creator.
2. **Every program:** on School, an admin sees **Open to me** and **Every program**. Every program shows each program from anyone, in every state, with who made it, and you can change any of them.
3. **Queues:** **Manage** shows tiles for Talent, Cohorts, Mentor, Review and Finance, with what is waiting.
4. **Listings:** **Admin**, **Listings** sets what Explore shows.

## The bottom line

Every path here has a person checking it: a mentor, a reviewer, finance. Teach members to enrol, apply and enter. As an admin, give each permission to someone who will use it well, and use Every program to see the whole of School.
