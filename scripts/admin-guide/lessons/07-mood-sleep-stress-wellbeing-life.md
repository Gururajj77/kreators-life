# Mood, Sleep, Stress, Wellbeing and Life
Riya can tell when a deadline week hits her. She sleeps late, her mood dips and her stress climbs. Five small apps turn that feeling into numbers she can see, and one dashboard ties them to the five areas of life.

In this lesson you'll learn:

- How Riya finds the trackers and logs her first day.
- How Wellbeing turns four trackers and a streak into one score.
- How Life scores the five areas of life, in their fixed order.
- What an admin can see and switch for these apps.

## Find them

Life shows on every new account. Wellbeing, Mood, Sleep and Stress start hidden on a new account, unless the setup chat showed one for her.

1. Riya opens **Apps** and opens the **Reflection** group. It says how many of its apps it shows.
2. She opens **Hidden apps** at its end and taps the eye beside **Wellbeing**, **Mood**, **Sleep** and **Stress**.
3. She can do the same under the **Profile** tab, in Features.

## Mood: one face a day

1. Riya opens **Mood**. The top asks "How are you feeling today?"
2. She taps a face: Low, Sad, Neutral, Happy or Overjoyed.
3. She taps **Set mood**. A card asks why she feels that way.
4. She moves three sliders: **How active were you?**, **How well did you eat?** and **How was your sleep?**
5. She can type a short note in **Anything else**. It is optional.
6. She taps **Save today's mood**.

Below sit **This week**, **Last 30 days** and **This month**. The month is a calendar. She taps a past day to add, change or remove its mood. A future day is refused.

## Sleep: last night in hours

1. Riya opens **Sleep** and finds **Last night**.
2. She drags the ruler to the hours she slept, in half hours.
3. Under **Quality** she taps Rough, Light, Okay, Good or Deep.
4. She taps **Save last night**. If she already logged it, the button reads **Update last night**.

The hero shows her average this week against eight hours. Seven to eight hours scores full marks. Under **Nights** she can delete a night, and a sheet asks first.

## Stress: the level and what is behind it

1. Riya opens **Stress** and finds **Right now**.
2. She moves the slider. The word above it changes: Calm, Fine, Tense, Stressed or Overwhelmed.
3. Under **What is behind it** she taps Work and Money.
4. She taps **Save today**.

The bubbles in **What is behind it** show which stressor came up most in thirty days. Lower is calmer. A line that climbs for a week is worth a word with someone.

## Wellbeing: one score

Wellbeing pulls the four trackers together.

- **The score:** a number out of 100, scored over the last seven days.
- **What makes it:** a ring shows the parts. Mood counts most, then sleep, then stress, then mindful minutes and the streak.
- **The rows:** Mood, Sleep, Stress and Mindfulness, each with today's value and a small line. Riya taps one to log.
- **Two doors:** Journal to write it down, and Companion to talk it through.

A part with nothing logged sits in the middle. It does not drag her down.

## Life: the five areas

Life scores the five areas of life, always in this order: physical, financial, mental, emotional, spiritual. Each one holds up the next. When one fails, it pulls down the one after it.

1. Riya opens **Life**. The hero shows her Life score, the average of the five areas.
2. **The five areas, this week** shows a bar for each, and which went up or down.
3. **This week, day by day** shows sleep, calm and mood as scores out of a hundred.
4. Each area has its own card with a ring, its parts and a link to its tools.

The parts come from what she already logs:

- **Physical:** Sleep, Movement and Breath, and Eating once she logs it.
- **Financial:** This month, Budgets, Invoices and Goals, from Finance.
- **Mental:** Stress, Focus from ticked tasks, and Learning from School.
- **Emotional:** Mood, Journal and People pillars.
- **Spiritual:** Stillness, Small acts, and Teachers and faith.

Each part is a link to the place she acts on it. A part with no data says "no data" and does not count against her.

## Log movement

Life has a quick way to log minutes. Fitness has the full one.

1. Riya scrolls to **Log movement**.
2. She types 40 in the minutes box for her evening walk.
3. She taps **Add minutes**.

A hundred and fifty minutes a week gives full marks for movement.

> **Pro tip:** tell new people to log for one full week before they judge a score. Seven days of mood, sleep and stress make the Wellbeing ring and the Life bars honest.

> **Result:** Riya sees in one place that her deadline weeks cost her sleep and calm, and she can tap straight to the fix.

## What the admin checks

Wellbeing numbers are private, like the journal.

- **No staff view:** mood, sleep, stress and the Wellbeing score never reach brands, staff, the leaderboard or Club picks. There is no admin screen for them.
- **Coaches:** a life coach sees these numbers only if the student leaves the Reflection switch on for that coach. Never a tracker's note.
- **The assistant:** tracker reads are offered only through a model that keeps no data.
- **Switches:** in Admin, **Flags**, each of the five apps has its own row: On, Minimum role, Rollout % and Overrides.
- **One person:** in Admin, **People**, use **What does this person see?** to tick or untick an app for one person.
- **No pay:** logging these trackers pays no xp, so there is nothing to take back or check for cheating.

If someone says a score looks wrong, ask what they logged this week. A score is built only from their own logs.

## The bottom line

Mood, Sleep and Stress take a minute a day. Wellbeing and Life turn those minutes into a picture. As an admin, keep the apps switched on for the people who need them, and keep their numbers out of every staff view.
