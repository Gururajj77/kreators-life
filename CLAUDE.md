# kreators.app

Seven relationships as a daily game, with a creator super app on top: public site, learn-and-earn modules, content rewards, a shop with attribution, finance, circles, a life dashboard.
Full spec and history: README.md, docs/DECISIONS.md, docs/build-map.html. Read the relevant section before changing anything.

## Stack
pnpm workspace. apps/web: React 19, Vite 7, Tailwind 4, PWA. apps/api: Hono on Cloudflare Workers with D1, plain SQL migrations in apps/api/migrations, cron every fifteen minutes. packages/shared: the domain model both sides import, with Vitest tests.

## Non-negotiable rules
- The product is called kreators.app, lowercase with the dot, in every heading, footer, email, policy and share card; never "Kreators Life". Identifiers keep their old names: the Worker and database `kreators-life`, the `@kreators/*` packages, storage keys and the repo.
- Money is integer cents or integer points. Use `money()` and `feeBps()` from shared. Never floats for money.
- Times are stored as UTC ISO strings. Zone conversion happens only through `zonedToUtc` and `slotsFor` in shared.
- Auth is hand-built on Hono and D1, no package: Bearer session tokens in `sessions` (two UUIDs), PBKDF2-SHA256 at 100,000 iterations (the Workers free-plan ceiling) with a 16-byte hex salt from `randomHex`, constant-time compare. `passwordProblem` in shared is the one password rule, used by the sign-up form, the change form and both endpoints. Sign-in answers the same for an unknown email and a wrong password, hashes against a decoy salt when the email is unknown so the timing matches, and is limited per address and per email by the numbers in `apps/api/src/limits.ts`. `POST /me/password` needs the current password and deletes every other session. `secureHeaders()` runs on every API answer. Password reset: `POST /auth/forgot` answers the same for any email and mails a one-time link (32 random bytes, stored as a SHA-256 hash in `password_resets`, thirty minutes, one open per account); `POST /auth/reset` checks the rule, claims the token, sets the hash and deletes every session. Founder addresses in `apps/api/src/admins.ts` and `ADMIN_EMAILS` get the admin role on sign-up or sign-in (`promoteBootstrapAdmin`, audited); until mail verifies addresses, whoever signs up with one first gets it. Magic links were tried and removed (migrations 0018 and 0019). Sign-up also asks for a phone number and a date of birth (`phoneProblem`, `birthDateProblem` and `MIN_AGE` 18 in shared, migration 0026): nobody under eighteen gets an account, the phone can be changed under Profile, the date of birth cannot, and neither is ever shown to anyone else.
- Every owner-scoped query filters by the session user id. Staff powers come from `can(user, permission)` in apps/api/src/db.ts.
- A switched-off module is refused by the server, not only hidden: `MODULE_FOR_PREFIX` and `modulesForPath` in apps/api/src/access.ts map every API prefix to its module or modules, and the middleware in index.ts answers 403 when none of them is available to the person (global flag, override, rollout or minimum role). The person's own on/off is a preference the client keeps with `Gate`. A new route file gets a line in that map or it is treated as core and always open.
- Rate limits live in one file, `apps/api/src/limits.ts`: `LIMITS` holds every name with its count and window, `ROUTE_LIMITS` says which method and path each one guards, and index.ts wires them (the doors without a session per address before the session check, everything else per person after it). Change a number there and nowhere else; `rateLimit(name)` in ops.ts reads it. Posts, replies, likes, follows, chat messages, offers, listings, tickets, applications, run entries, attendee code claims (`claim`), coaching asks (`coachAsk`) and what a coach writes (`coachWrite`) are limited as well as the auth doors.
- Lists are served a page at a time: `PAGE_SIZES` (10, 25, 50, 100) and `Page` in shared; `pageOf(c, prefix)` and `paged(rows, ask)` in db.ts on every list route (one extra row is fetched so `more` says whether a next page exists); `Pager` and `usePage` in `components/Pager.tsx` on the screen (the page lives in the URL, a prefix tells two lists on one screen apart); `page` and `onPage` on `DataTable` for the admin tables. Never a bare `LIMIT 100` on a list a person scrolls.
- Club benefits are code, not words: an opportunity can be `club_only` (migration 0027) and is then listed, opened and applied to only by an approved, unexpired member (`isClubMember` in db.ts), and never shown on the public site; a reviewer names a staff member as a member's manager (`POST /club/memberships/:id/manager`), shown on their Club page and sent to their inbox. A reward can change after launch only through `PATCH /win/:id` (its creator, or review or talent staff): the version goes up, the audit row says what changed, every entrant gets an inbox note, and the pool never drops under what was already paid.
- Payment code talks to apps/api/src/payments.ts, never to a provider SDK directly. A money order becomes `paid` only in the webhook after signature verification.
- Webhooks: verify the signature on the raw body, store the raw payload in `webhook_events`, then process. Duplicates are no-ops.
- The /go redirect writes the click after the redirect is sent and never stores a raw IP, only a salted hash.
- Journal and wellbeing data never reach brands, staff, the leaderboard or Club selection. They reach the assistant's model only for their owner, the journal only when the owner asks about it, through providers that keep no data (see the assistant below). The one other door is a life coach the person said yes to: wellbeing numbers (never a tracker's note) and saved reviews (never their snapshot) reach that coach only while the person's own Reflection switch on that link is on; the journal never reaches a coach (see Life coaches).
- Everything that pays out has a wait or a reviewer: task age, daily tick cap, verified views, finance permission for payouts. A task with a date cannot be ticked before that date: `POST /nodes/:id/complete` takes the person's date and refuses an early tick, and `TaskRow` locks the box with "Tick it on the day". A ticked task stays in sight: `GET /today` returns the tasks ticked on the person's day (`done`, bounded by their zone through `zonedToUtc`), and Daily lists them under Done today, struck through with the time; tapping one unticks it, which takes back its xp.
- Unlocks: a product, a store item or a tool can ask for a points balance, a badge, or both (`min_points`, `badge`, migration 0024). `unlockFor` in shared is the one rule and the one wording; the server computes the viewer's `Unlock` (`badgesNow` plus `honorKeys`) for the product page, every store item and every tool, refuses a gated checkout with 403, and blanks a locked item's or tool's link. Creators set it on their products, staff on items and tools, from the same two inputs (points floor, badge select over `BADGES` and `HONORS`).
- Nudges: one quiet line (`components/Nudge.tsx`, 12px, soft) under the thing it talks about, never a banner, each linking to Help, "How you earn" (`earningGuide` in shared, the one list of what pays). Every app's main screen ends with a "How it works" section (`HowItWorks` in `components/HowTo.tsx`, rendered by `Screen` from the module key that `Gate` and `explained` in App.tsx provide; it shows only when the path is the module's own path, so detail pages inside an app leave it to the main screen) with three to five concrete steps from `HOW_TO`, each a sentence or two with the numbers and the rules, then the words it leans on from `TERMS`. The section is one card that is a button (`toy toy-soft`, lifts and tints on hover, stays tinted while open, closed by default); tapping it opens the steps and the words together, each a smaller flat toggle inside (`toy-flat`, tints on hover, darker while open), and tapping it again folds everything away. Opened content slides in with GSAP (`gsap.set` under reduced motion). Never a floating button or a sheet; the section carries `span-all` so on Home's two-column grid it takes the full width. Help lists every word under "Words we use". A new app gets a `HOW_TO` entry (the shared test insists) and new words go in `TERMS`; a screen that must not show it passes `howTo={false}`.
- Search (`SearchBox`) shows apps and help from the client and tasks, journal, knowledge, products, people, store items, pre-owned listings, tools and community posts from `GET /search`, grouped, under the box as you type. Life's five bars are labelled by name. The public site is three pages: `/` tells the story in the PASTOR order (the problem, the five villains and what they cost, the story where the person is the hero and the guides help, the five Ws, how each villain is beaten with the apps that do it, before and after with the live numbers, the offer, the one next step), `/features` lists everything a creator needs by group, and `/7rp` lists every app a relationship serves and can switch to the five areas of life (`LIFE_AREA_FEATURES`). Claims on `/` are only what the app does; it never shows a made-up quote or number.
- Leaderboard (`leaderboard`, group Essentials, on for every account, path `/leaderboard`): boards by `LEADERBOARD_SCOPES` — everyone, goal (`SETUP_GOALS`, read from `intake_json`), pillar (that pillar's own xp from `relationship_scores`), city, identity tag and experience level — each ranked by xp, this month, streak or badges, paged, over a pool of the top `LEADERBOARD_POOL` people who opted in. People appear only after they opt in and finish setup; nobody blocked either way with the viewer is listed; journal and wellbeing never count; no date of birth or age band is ever used. `GET /leaderboard/options` lists what can be picked, `GET /leaderboard/blocks` gives a small top-three board for every goal and pillar. - Media, event extras and blocking (migration 0037, 0036): every listing type and School lesson can carry pictures, GIFs and videos (`media_items`, `MediaOwner`, `MAX_MEDIA_ITEMS` 8). A picture or GIF is uploaded (`POST /media/upload`, `MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES` 1 MB, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF or GIF, checked by its first bytes, base64 in D1 `media_files`, served from `/api/public/media/:id`; the browser shrinks pictures first, `lib/shrink.ts`) or linked; a video is only ever a link (`videoEmbed`: YouTube and Vimeo in their own player, a direct .mp4/.webm). `canEditMedia` in routes/media.ts is the one rule for who may change an owner's set; `listingsFor` attaches `media` so cards show a cover (`MediaCover`), and `MediaSection` is the block every detail page drops in. Uploading to R2 for video is not built. Inside Party events and runs (`EventApp`; circles stay anonymous and have none of it) `components/EventPanel.tsx` gives the host's photo-folder link (`photoLinkProblem`, `PHOTO_HOSTS`: Google Drive and Photos, Dropbox, iCloud, OneDrive, WeTransfer; guests are told once), a conversation for guests and host (`event_messages`, open until `CHAT_DAYS_AFTER` after the end, limit `message`) and the guests who chose to be listed (`event_networking`, `networking_opt_in`) to send a friend request through `requestFriend`. Anyone blocks anyone (`POST /bond/block`, ends a friendship, hides posts, replies, event lines and leaderboard rows both ways; `GET /bond/blocks`, `DELETE /bond/block/:userId`). Muting from Community is by rank (`COMMUNITY_RANKS` member, guide, staff, admin; `canMute`): only someone of a lower rank, and lifting needs at least the muter's rank.
- Anonymous and threaded conversations (migration 0038): a Community post, a reply and a line in an event conversation can be written anonymously (`anonymous`, `AnonToggle`): the reader gets a made-up name from `anonAlias` (`anonAliasFrom`: a word, an animal and two digits, hashed from the writer and the conversation, so it is the same all through one thread and different in the next), and never the id, handle, follower count, Pro mark or rank; staff keep the id so a report can be acted on, and the writer's own `mine` flag lets them delete theirs. Every post and reply leaves the server through `presentPosts` and `presentReplies`; an anonymous post never shows under Following, on a public page, in search by name, or in a notice by real name, and is blocked by post or reply id (`/community/posts/:id/block-author`) so nobody learns who it is. Replies thread (`parent_id`, `depth`, `MAX_REPLY_DEPTH` 4, `threadOrder`): an answer sits under what it answers and past the deepest level lands beside it. Event connections need the other person's yes like any friend request, and the request says which event it came from.
- Usernames and friend codes (migration 0036): every account has a unique `handle` (username, `user_xxxxxx` until changed, never blank, `isAutoHandle`) and a unique 8-character `friend_code`. A code sends a friend request in Bond (`POST /bond/friends` with `code`) and starts a coach link (the coach's box takes an email, @username or friend code).
- Not to do: at most `MAX_NOT_TO_DO` (7) items a person, titles up to `NOT_TO_DO_TITLE_MAX`; one row per item per day in `not_to_do_days` (kept or slipped; no row means missed). `notToDoStreak` in shared is the one streak rule: today counts once ticked, the run ends at yesterday until then, and a slip or a missed day ends it. A kept day pays `XP.notToDo` once per item per day and is never clawed back. Only today or yesterday can be marked (`dateNearToday`), and the request says which day is the person's today so the streak is counted as of that day. The section sits under Tasks on Daily and again on Pillars under the seven pillars: one block, `NotToDoBlock` in `components/NotToDoList.tsx`, reading Daily's own data so a tick in one place shows in the other. - Periods and reviews: `periodFor`, `periodShift`, `periodLabel` and `periodDays` in shared are the one period arithmetic (weeks start on Monday, quarters and halves follow the calendar, both ends inclusive, labels like "19 to 25 Oct 2026"). `GET /reviews/summary` pulls one period's numbers from every app's own table (`numbersFor` in reviews.ts) next to the period before; journal and wellbeing numbers go only to their owner. One review per person per period (`UNIQUE (user_id, kind, period_start)`), saved with the prompts and a snapshot of the numbers so a changed template or new data never rewrites it; a period that has not started is refused. Each cadence has a built-in template in shared (`REVIEW_PROMPTS`, `builtInTemplate`) that cannot be edited or removed; a person's own templates (`review_templates`, up to `MAX_REVIEW_TEMPLATES`) recur with their cadence. `components/PeriodFilter.tsx` is the one period control and `components/PeriodPanel.tsx` (the filter plus `SummaryTiles`) is the "By period" block every dashboard carries: Home, Life, Finance, Wellbeing, Mood, Sleep, Stress, Mindful, Journal and the Shop. Reviews live in the Reflection group, off by default.
- Only spacing values 8, 12, 16, 32, 64, 120 (tokens xs, sm, md, lg, xl, 2xl). Never hardcode hex colours in screens; use the theme tokens so dark mode works. The ink family is grey, not brown: `#262626` (85% black) for ink in light mode, `#121212` paper and `#1e1e1e` card in dark mode, greys for soft, mute, line and edges; the seven relationship colours and their tints keep their hues, including the brown of With yourself, and the icons and share card use the same ink.
- Wording is universal and plain, readable by a teenager (Flesch 60 or above; `python3 -I scripts/flesch.py <file>` scores a file, `--strings` scores only the quoted text of a TypeScript file). Every admin list is a `components/DataTable.tsx`: sort by any column, search, filter by a column's values, tick rows for bulk actions, edit a cell in place (`edit` on the column says which call runs), and page through the server's rows (`page`, `onPage`); the public site's "Go to app" goes to sign-in without a session and to Home with one, and "Start free" goes to Welcome, where the session starts and the account is made inside it. Numbers come before actions on any dashboard. No new shadow values; use `shadow-toy-soft` or the `toy` classes.
- Teaching: `components/Pyramid.tsx` is the one explainer for how a pillar works; onboarding shows it after the answers (results first, then what did it, then the one rule), and Pillars and each pillar page show it too. - Kreators Run (`run`, group Connection, accent friendship, off by default): clubs, runs, entries and collabs live in migration 0025. A run's `code` is the host's own word (`normalizeCode`, `ATTENDEE_CODE_MIN` to `ATTENDEE_CODE_MAX` letters or digits) or `randomCode(RUN_CODE_LENGTH)`, set by `newRunCode` and `setRunCode` in attendee.ts (`POST /run/runs/:id/recode` takes `xp`, `code` or `fresh`, a fresh code when nothing is asked), and is only ever returned to the host and events staff (`toRun(r, mine, showCode)`); an entry needs membership, the exact code, the window from `runEntryState` (thirty minutes before the start to three hours after the end) and is unique per run and person. The pay is the run's own xp (`runs.xp`, set by the host before the start, `ATTENDEE_XP_DEFAULT` unless they say, up to `ATTENDEE_XP_MAX`) plus `RUN_REWARD.points`, through `xp_events` source `run` and `points_ledger` reason `run`, capped at `RUN_REWARD.perDay`; a void writes the reverse. Whoever is shown the code (the club's host, events staff, admins) never logs the run. A run marked done still takes entries inside its window and stays on the club's list until the window closes; a run added after its start keeps its code but pays `ATTENDEE_XP_DEFAULT`, and the create answer says so (`codeError`). The pay is capped inside the batch that pays (`payRun`), so two entries at once cannot both slip under `RUN_REWARD.perDay`, and it counts toward the friendship pillar like a claim; erase keeps run entries, so a run cannot pay twice. Entry minutes are a `move` wellbeing log so Life's physical score counts them. Collabs carry a `giveaway` and are posted by staff with `events` or a brand's own team; only a club's host applies. No self-reported run ever pays.
- Attendee codes, for the Connection apps that hold events with guests (Party events, runs, circles; Community, Bond and Club have none of their own, and Club's events are Party events): the host sets the xp each guest gets (`ATTENDEE_XP_MAX`, one cap for hosts and admins) when the event is made or later, and a code, their own word or a random one; the xp is fixed once the code opens, thirty minutes before the start (`attendeeXpLocked`), and the code can change any time. `apps/api/src/attendee.ts` is the one rule (`setEventCode`, `setRunCode`, `claimEventCode`): only the event's own guests (Party: registered or attended; circles: seated at one of its tables; runs: club members, through the start code), inside the run window (`runEntryState`; a circle counts as `CIRCLE_MINUTES` long), once each, never anyone the server shows the code to (the host, events staff, admins), at most `ATTENDEE_CLAIMS_PER_DAY` event codes in any 24 hours, checked inside the batch that pays (runs keep their own cap), the claim and its xp in one batch (`attendee_claims` keyed by app, event and person, migration 0033), paid as `xp_events` source `attendee` to the friendship pillar; a Party claim also checks the guest in. Codes are normalised the same way everywhere (`normalizeCode`, 4 to 12 letters or digits) and reach only the host, events staff and admins: a guest sees the xp, whether they claimed it and when it opens and closes (`AttendeeCodeView`, `codeWindow`; the card works the state out from its own clock), and a circle's host sees counts, never who. Circles are run by events staff as a team: `GET /circles` gives them `hosting`, every open or matched circle in any city until its code closes, paged with prefix `host`, and the code card lives there. A weekly circle's next week keeps the xp with a new random code (`rollWeeklyCircles`). Claims stay through an erase (so a code cannot pay twice) and go with the account. Hosts set codes on the event page (`HostCodeCard`), the run card and a circle's card; guests claim on the event page and the table page (`ClaimCard`); admins see every event with guests and set any of them under Admin, Attendee codes (`GET /admin/attendee-codes`, paged, filtered by app and title; `PUT /admin/attendee-codes/:app/:id`), by the same rules, audited as `attendee_code.set` with the hosts' own resource names, and the host is told; New random codes touches only events that already have a code. A create form that cannot set the code says why (`codeError`) on the event page or the Circles page. The limit is `claim`.
- Life coaches (`coaching`, group Reflection, accent guru, off on a new account, path `/coaching`): an admin makes someone a life coach with the `coach` permission, which a creator can hold (like `mentor`) and which, alone among permissions, an admin does not hold unless given it (`isStaffFor`); the badge then reads Life coach (`BADGE_META`, 🎯). Never write a second version of an action for the assistant; give the route a tool. The user comes from the token, never from the model; every id a model gives passes `seg` (no slash, no dot) and a path the URL parser had to tidy is refused. The person's date and zone come from the device, never the model. A tool is `read`, `write` (logged in `assistant_actions` with the reverse route for Undo) or `confirm` (removing from a pyramid or the not to do list, settling a due: a card waits for Confirm, an hour at most), and is offered only when its app is on. Each change comes back as a row with Undo (`Receipts`), and the client clears the query keys the tool names (`refresh`). `assistant_actions` keeps tool names, ids and plain labels, never journal text or a log's note (a review that replaced an older one has no Undo, so the older words never sit there); it is in the export and goes with erase and delete. The model is provider neutral (`assistant/model.ts`): any OpenAI-compatible endpoint from `ASSISTANT_BASE_URL` (OpenRouter by default) and `ASSISTANT_API_KEY`. For now the panel has a model picker under its header whenever a model is connected, plan or not (kept in the browser, `useAssistantModel`), over `ASSISTANT_MODELS` in shared, OpenRouter's free models that take tools; the server takes a pick only from that list and otherwise uses `ASSISTANT_MODEL` (the first of the list unless set). A free model costs nothing (`priceOf`), so it spends no credits; the plan is still needed. Privacy follows the model: one with a zero-retention host (`private`, Xing4.0 among the free ones) is held to hosts that keep nothing and collect nothing, and only then are the journal and tracker reads (`sensitive` tools) offered; a model that keeps data is sent without that ask (asking would find no host) and never offered those reads, and the panel says so under the picker. A model off the list is asked for zero retention unless `ASSISTANT_ZDR=off`. Every model is asked not to reason (`reasoning: { effort: 'none' }`, since reasoning shares `max_tokens` with the answer and can leave it empty) and is sent no temperature; a model whose reasoning cannot be switched off (`thinks`, Flint and LFM 2.5) gets `MAX_OUT_THINKING` instead. A reply cut off before it said anything, or stopped on an error, is a `ModelError`; a model failure before any tool ran answers 502 and the line is not kept for the model. Each model reply is drawn as small markdown with "Answered by" and the model's name, so it is never mistaken for a built-in line, and a $0 message still writes a usage row with its tokens and model. Arguments are read kindly (`checkArgs`: case and spacing in a choice, a pillar by its name, "true" as text, one string for a list, a code fence around the JSON, ₹ and grouping commas in an amount); a tool result too long to read is replaced by a note, never cut mid-JSON, and every tool call past eight in one turn is answered with a note to call it again. The model is told to finish a job in one go (look it up, then do it, without asking leave), and when it still ends a turn asking leave before changing anything (`asksLeave`: "Do you want me to…?", never a question that offers a choice) the server answers "Yes, go ahead" for the person once and goes on; removing and settling still wait for Confirm. Stop: while a message runs the send arrow is a Stop square (`ChatComposer` `onStop`); the browser names each message (`runId`), `POST /assistant/stop` marks it in `assistant_runs` (migration 0032, rows live only while a message runs), and the loop looks before each model call and before running any tool, so nothing changes after the mark is seen. The request is never cut off (a dropped request could end the Worker before its Undo rows and its charge), so the reply still lists what was done before the stop, each with Undo. The model reads the journal only when the person asks about it. The chat is kept for the tab (scope `assistant:<user>`, its open state kept apart so Start over leaves it open); Close folds it into the button and keeps the chat. Without a plan a typed question about a word still gets `explainTyped`, and anything else gets the plan card. `node scripts/smoke/drive-talk.mjs` (`TALK_FROM`, `TALK_TAPS` as button text, `fill:<label>=<value>`, `submit`, `type:<text>`, `TALK_WIDE=1`) opens it on a screen and reports where the panel sits against the tab bar. `scripts/assistant/eval.mjs` sends thirty-odd real messages to a real model and checks the tool calls each one led to (`SMOKE_MODEL=real SMOKE_RUN=eval ASSISTANT_API_KEY=... bash scripts/smoke/blueprint.sh`, `ASSISTANT_MODEL` to compare), so a model is swapped on numbers, not by feel. The same tool list is what an MCP server would serve later.
- Credits and plans: typing to the assistant (and later every agent that moves onto a model) is paid in credits, one credit a tenth of a US cent of model use, worked out from the tokens with whole numbers only (`creditsFor`, prices in micro-dollars per million tokens in `MODEL_PRICES` or `ASSISTANT_PRICE_IN` and `ASSISTANT_PRICE_OUT`; a model with no price is charged `FALLBACK_PRICE`, high on purpose). The only three plans are `PLANS` (Pro US$15 for 10,000 credits, Pro Max US$125 for 100,000, Ultra US$500 for 400,000, each for `PLAN_DAYS`; plan credits do not carry over), top-ups are `TOPUP_PACKS` (extra credits that stay until used and are spent after the plan's), and each plan's limits per agent are `AGENT_LIMITS` (tokens read per message, credits per message, credits per 24 hours, steps per message), all in shared. `apps/api/src/credits.ts` is the one ledger: `credit_accounts` (the plan, its period, the plan and extra balances) and `credit_ledger` (every grant, every use with its tokens, every expiry), migration 0031; a plan whose time is up closes the next time the account is read. `POST /assistant` refuses with a `paywall` (402 `plan` or `credits`, 429 `day`), stops a message before a model call that could pass what it may spend, and takes the cost once at the end, plan credits first, never below zero. A plan wears the Pro mark (`is_pro` and the `pro` honor follow it). Until payments are live (`PAYMENTS_LIVE`), plans and top-ups are shown and picked on `/plans` (`screens/Plans.tsx`, linked from Profile) but `POST /credits/checkout` answers that nothing was charged; only an admin starts a plan (`POST /admin/users/:id/plan`) or adds credits (`POST /admin/users/:id/credits`, positive, up to `MAX_CREDIT_GRANT`), from Admin, People, and every change is audited; the old Pro toggle starts or ends a plan. The free flows never spend credits.
- The daily reminder is sent by the cron: `sendReminders` in cron.ts reads every `reminder_time`, finds the person's local day in their zone, skips anyone who checked in that day, and within sixty minutes after the due instant writes one `reminder` notification linking to Daily, never twice for the same due time. Inbox sets and clears the time (`PATCH /me` with `reminderTime`); the contract check-in time sets the same field.
- Social: the Community feed sorts by `FEED_SORTS` (recent, popular by reactions, most replies) and filters by `FEED_FILTERS` (everyone, following, mine); a person can be followed from a post (`POST /community/follow/:userId`, the same `follows` table as the public page). Every social action pays through `rewardSocial` in apps/api/src/rewards.ts with the amounts and daily caps in `SOCIAL_REWARDS` (a post 5 xp up to three a day, a reply 2 xp up to five, a like you give 1 xp up to ten; a like on your post pays the author a point, a new follower two, up to twenty a day), each once per thing (`source_id`/`ref`), so unliking and liking again never pays twice. Trust: `trustScore` in shared is the one formula (xp, points earned, best streak, time here, sales with rating, honors; weighted, 0 to 100, four labels), computed server-side by `trustForMany` in one query and shown with `components/TrustBadge.tsx` on the public page header, the product page and every pre-owned listing; nothing in it can be typed in.
- Do not build the white-label app, instalments or native chat transport (see docs/DECISIONS.md). The Store's chat and offers are polled tables, not a transport.
