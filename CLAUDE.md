# kreators.app

Seven relationships as a daily game, with a creator super app on top: public site, learn-and-earn modules, content rewards, a shop with attribution, finance, circles, a life dashboard.
Full spec and history: README.md, docs/DECISIONS.md, and the build map (Observability, from apps/web/src/lib/buildMap.ts). Read the relevant section before changing anything.

## Stack
pnpm workspace. apps/web: React 19, Vite 7, Tailwind 4, PWA. apps/api: Hono on Cloudflare Workers with D1, plain SQL migrations in apps/api/migrations, cron every fifteen minutes plus a second trigger every two hours that sends only water. packages/shared: the domain model both sides import, with Vitest tests. Since the wave 9 cleanup (2026-11-04) `src/index.ts` is only a barrel of `export * from './<domain>.ts'` lines in load order (a file after the files it reads at load time), over one file per domain (core, pillars, rewards, work, community, dates, api, events, store, badges, tasks, periods, money, invoice, agency, design, discover and the rest); the `.ts` extensions stay, because the smoke scripts load it with Node's strip-types. A new name goes in its domain's file and a new file gets a line in the barrel; "in shared" in these notes means through the barrel. A top-level constant may only call a function declared with `function` or defined above it, and after any edit `node --experimental-strip-types -e "import('./packages/shared/src/index.ts')"` must load. The subpath exports (`journalPrompts`, `agencyEmail`, `focusRun`) are their own files as before.

## Non-negotiable rules
- The product is called kreators.app, lowercase with the dot, in every heading, footer, email, policy and share card; never "Kreators Life". Identifiers keep their old names: the Worker and database `kreators-life`, the `@kreators/*` packages, storage keys and the repo.
- Money is integer cents or integer points. Use `money()` and `feeBps()` from shared. Never floats for money. An amount a person types is read from its digits with `typedCents` in shared (never `Math.round(Number(x) * 100)`: 1.005 would be 100). The one older exception is a TV or Fund pitch's `budget_amount`, kept in whole units; it too is read with `typedCents` (`budgetOf` in routes/proposals.ts, `budgetUnits` on the screen), so ₹ and lakh commas work. Anything that moves points takes them first with a guarded `UPDATE … WHERE points >= ?` and checks `meta.changes`, and anything that pays or refunds moves its status first (`WHERE status = 'verified'`) and checks the change, so two taps at once pay or refund once. A key a person can type is looked up in a `byKey` object (no prototype), so "constructor" finds nothing.
- Times are stored as UTC ISO strings. Zone conversion happens only through `zonedToUtc` and `slotsFor` in shared. A day typed or sent (`?day=`, `date`, `anchor`, `until`) is checked as a real calendar day (`DATE_RE.test` in db.ts is that check, so 2026-11-31 and 2026-00-00 are refused, never a 500). A repeat is monthly or yearly from the day it started (`Repeat.anchor`, set by `spawnNext`), so a task that fell on the 28th of a short month goes back to the 31st. The cron's last tick of a day is 23:45 (`LAST_TICK`), so a reminder set later goes out then. A local time that does not exist (the clocks jumped over it) lands an hour later in `zonedToUtc`.
- India first, open to all (2026-11-03): India is the default and someone abroad changes it. `LOCALE` (`en-IN`) and `DEFAULT_ZONE` (`Asia/Kolkata`) in shared are the one locale and the one zone fallback: every date and number on a screen passes `LOCALE` (`lib/date.ts`: "3 Nov 2026", `numericDate` 03/11/2026, "6:30 pm"), counts go through `formatCount` (12,34,567), and `money()` groups rupees in lakh and crore and other currencies in thousands from integer paise. A person keeps their money in `users.currency` (migration 0070, INR unless changed under Profile with `PATCH /me {currency}`, one of `CURRENCIES` by `isCurrency`, read with `currencyOf` in db.ts); Finance, Life's money line and a review's period money add up only that currency and list the rest unadded, never converted. A phone without a country code is Indian (`normalizePhone`: a bare ten-digit number from 6 to 9, or one with a leading 0, gets +91; one typed with a plus is kept; `PHONE_HINT` under every phone field). KYC and a client's address take a PIN code (`pinCodeProblem`) and a state from `INDIAN_STATES`, beside PAN and IFSC; an INR invoice with GST not split prints IGST (18%). An event made with no zone (a Party event, or one the team posts) takes its host's zone, then `DEFAULT_ZONE`; any IANA zone can still be picked. Plans, top-ups and the live call are priced in `PLAN_CURRENCY` (INR, see Credits and plans).
- Auth is hand-built on Hono and D1, no package: Bearer session tokens in `sessions` (two UUIDs), PBKDF2-SHA256 at 100,000 iterations (the Workers free-plan ceiling) with a 16-byte hex salt from `randomHex`, constant-time compare. `passwordProblem` in shared is the one password rule, used by the sign-up form, the change form and both endpoints. Sign-in answers the same for an unknown email and a wrong password, hashes against a decoy salt when the email is unknown so the timing matches, and is limited per address and per email by the numbers in `apps/api/src/limits.ts`. `POST /me/password` needs the current password and deletes every other session. `secureHeaders()` runs on every API answer. Password reset: `POST /auth/forgot` answers the same for any email and mails a one-time link (32 random bytes, stored as a SHA-256 hash in `password_resets`, thirty minutes, one open per account); `POST /auth/reset` checks the rule, claims the token, sets the hash and deletes every session. Founder addresses in `apps/api/src/admins.ts` and `ADMIN_EMAILS` get the admin role on sign-up or sign-in (`promoteBootstrapAdmin`, audited); until mail verifies addresses, whoever signs up with one first gets it. Magic links were tried and removed (migrations 0018 and 0019). Sign-up also asks for a phone number and a date of birth (`phoneProblem`, `birthDateProblem` and `MIN_AGE` 18 in shared, migration 0026): nobody under eighteen gets an account, the phone can be changed under Profile, the date of birth cannot, and neither is ever shown to anyone else.

A video from another site waits behind `EmbedGate` until videos are allowed or the person taps it. `sendEmail(..., { marketing: true })` sends only with a recorded yes and a business address, adds a one-click unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` headers (`/unsubscribe/:token`, `POST /public/unsubscribe/:token`); receipts and resets are never marketing. The business that runs kreators.app (name, address, email, registration, tax number, the Grievance Officer for India in `BUSINESS_GRIEVANCE_NAME`, `BUSINESS_GRIEVANCE_EMAIL` and the optional `BUSINESS_GRIEVANCE_PHONE`, copyright agent) is set as Worker variables (`BUSINESS_*`, `DMCA_*` in env.ts) and read from `GET /public/business`, never written in code; an empty field is shown as "not set yet", never invented. Public pages: `/terms`, `/privacy` (its table of how long things are kept is `RETENTION`), `/refunds`, `/contact`, `/pricing`, `/subprocessors` (`SUBPROCESSORS`), `/copyright` (notices and counter-notices go to `copyright_notices` and the admins' inbox, `POST /public/copyright-notice`, limit `notice`), `/licences` (generated by `scripts/licences.mjs` into `public/licences.json`). `tidyOld` in cron.ts applies `RETENTION_DAYS` every run; the export and the delete cover every table that holds what a person wrote (messages, tickets, posts, finance lists, the Companion), and a request to export or delete is logged without a name in `data_requests`. India first, open to all (2026-11-03): the privacy page leads with the DPDP Act (the data principal's rights, consent or a use the Act allows in the ground table, the Grievance Officer first and then the Data Protection Board of India) and keeps the EU and UK ground and rights below, labelled for those countries; refunds lead with UPI, card or bank through Razorpay; prices are in rupees with GST shown before paying; the privacy popup carries one quiet line pointing questions to the Grievance Officer on `/contact`; a reply time is never stated unless the business sets one. Never claim a certification, a SOC 2, a compliance label, an uptime number or a customer logo in any copy.
- Security (the full table is docs/security/SECURITY_REPORT.md; map, runbook, secrets list and restore steps sit beside it): the site's own headers come from `apps/web/public/_headers` for static files and `apps/api/src/headers.ts` for what the Worker answers itself, and the two carry the same Content-Security-Policy (same-origin scripts, styles and fonts, Razorpay's script and frames, YouTube and Vimeo frames, no inline script). It is enforcing and was checked by browsing 118 pages (2026-11-03) with `node scripts/smoke/csp-browse.mjs`; a new third-party origin needs a line in both places and a new run of that script. HSTS has no preload. There is no CORS: the API is same-origin, so an unknown Origin gets no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`, and a write with a foreign `Origin` header answers 403 `wrong origin` (no Origin, the site's own, `PUBLIC_URL`, loopback and webhooks pass). Authenticated API answers are `Cache-Control: no-store`. Paths that should hold nothing (dotfiles, backups, dumps, config, source maps, `/src`) answer 404 from the Worker, never the app shell (`blockedPath` in headers.ts, mirrored in `run_worker_first` in wrangler.jsonc); `scripts/check-exposed.sh <domain>` checks a live site. A 5xx answers a plain line with a `requestId` (also `X-Request-Id`), never the error text. Any URL the Worker fetches that a person influenced goes through `safeFetch` in `safeurl.ts` (https only, no IP literal, localhost, internal name, credentials or odd port, redirects checked, timeout and size cap). A link a person types for a meeting, replay, video or event goes through `httpUrl` in db.ts (http or https only). `securityAlerts` in alerts.ts tells every admin's inbox about bursts of failed sign-ins, 429s, 5xx and role changes, once an hour per kind. A password-reset link is built from `PUBLIC_URL`, never from the request's Host, and the outbox keeps no live link (`private: true` on `sendEmail`). `scripts/hooks/pre-commit` refuses a commit that carries a secret pattern (install it by copying it to `.git/hooks`). Session tokens in localStorage stay a recorded, mitigated risk. Never print or paste a secret value.
- Performance (docs/performance.md): every call out of the API goes through `fetchT` with a named `TIMEOUT` (http.ts) and is never retried; independent reads run together; new indexes live in their own migration with the reason beside each (0060, then 0083 for Emerging's agency read); the module gate keeps flag settings for five seconds (`forgetAccessContext()` runs where an admin changes one). The web app loads page by page: every screen except Home, Daily, sign-in, Welcome, the setup chat, the landing page and the tab bar is `lazyNamed` (lib/lazy.ts, reloads once on a failed chunk), under one `Suspense` and `RouteFallback`; vendor code is split in vite.config.ts (shared is one chunk, with the line of the day split off as `shared-lines` and the legal lists as `shared-legal`, 2026-11-05, which keeps it at 395 kB; a file split off from shared must import nothing from it, or the two chunks cycle and the app stops); `pnpm perf:budget` fails the build when the entry exceeds 480 kB, the first download 1,500 kB or any other chunk 400 kB (after the 2026-11-05 type pass and Emerging's one list: entry 467 kB, first download 1,454 kB, shared 395 kB; `lib/api.ts` is preloaded in a chunk of its own, `app-api`, and motion's animation code loads after it, see Animation). Statements that would run once per row in a loop go fifty to a round trip through `inBatches` (and `notifyMany`) in db.ts; D1 runs each fifty as one transaction, so only idempotent writes go in one batch. Loading placeholders are `Skeleton` (components/Skeleton.tsx), search boxes use `useDebounced` (300 ms), pictures are shrunk to WebP where the browser can, and images below the first screen are `loading="lazy"`. Real-visitor timing (`lib/vitals.ts`, `POST /api/public/vitals`, `web_vitals`, 90 days, no id, address or agent; staff read the 75th percentile at `GET /api/internal/vitals`) is skipped under Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. `node scripts/smoke/perf-probe.mjs` loads pages with a slow CPU and Slow 4G.
- Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA): ink and fill tokens in app.css were tuned for 4.5:1 and the contrast probe now reports against AA (3,375 hits became 30); `components/SkipLink.tsx` is the first stop on every screen; every dialog (`Sheet`, the assistant, the reminders bubble) uses `lib/useDialogFocus.ts` (focus moves in, Tab stays inside, Escape closes the top one, focus returns); `scripts/scan.sh` fails on an `<img>` without `alt`; the type is one family, Google Sans Flex (the founder's pick, 2026-11-05: "use only this font family"), self-hosted as two woff2 subsets under `public/fonts` (latin and latin-ext, the rupee in latin-ext; weight 300 to 800 and roundness only, about 72 kB for latin, the one font preloaded in index.html; no third party sees a visitor on load), SIL OFL, listed in `LICENSE.txt` and on `/licences`. `--font-display`, `--font-body` and `--font-mono` all name it so each role can still be tuned: eyebrows in capitals with tracking, and figures (`.font-mono`, `.type-eyebrow`, `.type-figure`) in tabular numbers. It is the plain face everywhere (ROND 0); only the two wordmarks are rounded (ROND 100, the founder's pick): the kreators.app logo at the top (`BrandMark`, `.brand-mark-face`, weight 700) and the big fluted wordmark in the public footer (`.site-footer-mark`). Never add another family and never round anything else; the one other face is Caveat, kept for a guide's handwritten line on the public site (`.guide-hand`, `site/GuideLine.tsx`, `caveat-latin.woff2`). Type follows Apple's app text styles set in Flex (2026-11-05, the founder: "follow apple best apps ui design to determine thickness and sizes of all text", with umbrel.com measured for Google Sans tracking): one semantic scale in `@theme` in app.css, each utility carrying size, leading, tracking and weight together, in rem so the browser's text size applies (`html` is 100%): `text-large-title` 34/41, `text-title1` 28, `text-title2` 22 (every plain section title and sheet title, one style per app), `text-title3` 20 (a title inside a card), `text-headline` 17 at 500 (card and two-line row titles, buttons), `text-body` 17 (plain list rows, inputs), `text-callout` 16, `text-prose` 16/24 (reading text), `text-subhead` 15 (the everyday secondary line, ink labels with `font-semibold`), `text-footnote` 13, `text-caption` 12, `text-caption2` 11 (the floor), `text-eyebrow` 12 caps at +0.06em, `text-figure-lg` 40 and `text-figure-hero` 64 for numbers (with `.type-figure` for tabular figures), and for the public site `text-display-1` to `-3` (hooks, 40 to 64 and smaller, growing at `md` and `lg` by the height-aware queries) and `text-lede` 18 to 20 (with `.type-context`). Tracking is per step: tight on large sizes (−0.024em at 64 to −0.008em at 20), zero at body, open on small text (+0.004em at 15 to +0.02em at 11); kerning, `liga` and `calt` stay on, `text-rendering: optimizeLegibility` only on large type, and grayscale smoothing on dark surfaces. No px font size on screen text: a screen uses a role, emphasis is `font-semibold` on a role (never `font-bold`), text in `ink-soft` or `ink-mute` is Regular (the colour makes it secondary; only a value that is its row's main fact keeps semibold), a link drawn as a button is semibold like `Button`, every tab root opens at `text-large-title` (Home's greeting too), a selected state never changes weight, and hierarchy comes from size and ink colour first. - Devices: an account can be signed in on at most `MAX_DEVICES` (3) at once. `newSession` in apps/api/src/routes/auth.ts is the one place a session is made, and it signs the oldest ones out (by `created_at`, then `rowid`) when a new one makes it four; the signed-out tokens go to `session_evictions` (migration 0051, thirty days, cleared by the cron and with the account), so a device that was pushed out gets `{ error: 'unauthorized', reason: 'devices' }` instead of a bare 401, the client keeps `kreators.signedOutNote` and the sign-in screen says why (`SignedOutNote`). A password change still signs out every other device. Help has a line on it.
- Every owner-scoped query filters by the session user id. Staff powers come from `can(user, permission)` in apps/api/src/db.ts.
- A switched-off module is refused by the server, not only hidden: `MODULE_FOR_PREFIX` and `modulesForPath` in apps/api/src/access.ts map every API prefix to its module or modules, and the middleware in index.ts answers 403 when none of them is available to the person (global flag, override, rollout or minimum role). The person's own on/off is a preference the client keeps with `Gate`. A new route file gets a line in that map or it is treated as core and always open.
- Rate limits live in one file, `apps/api/src/limits.ts`: `LIMITS` holds every name with its count and window, `ROUTE_LIMITS` says which method and path each one guards, and index.ts wires them (the doors without a session per address before the session check, everything else per person after it). Change a number there and nowhere else; `rateLimit(name)` in ops.ts reads it. Posts, replies, likes, follows, chat messages, offers, listings, tickets, applications, run entries, attendee code claims (`claim`), coaching asks (`coachAsk`) and what a coach writes (`coachWrite`) are limited as well as the auth doors.
- Lists are served a page at a time: `PAGE_SIZES` (10, 25, 50, 100) and `Page` in shared; `pageOf(c, prefix)` and `paged(rows, ask)` in db.ts on every list route (one extra row is fetched so `more` says whether a next page exists); `Pager` and `usePage` in `components/Pager.tsx` on the screen (the page lives in the URL, a prefix tells two lists on one screen apart); `page` and `onPage` on `DataTable` for the admin tables. - Club benefits are code, not words: an opportunity can be `club_only` (migration 0027) and is then listed, opened and applied to only by an approved, unexpired member (`isClubMember` in db.ts), and never shown on the public site; a reviewer names a staff member as a member's manager (`POST /club/memberships/:id/manager`), shown on their Club page and sent to their inbox. A reward can change after launch only through `PATCH /win/:id` (its creator, or review or talent staff): the version goes up, the audit row says what changed, every entrant gets an inbox note, and the pool never drops under what was already paid.
- Not every product takes points: `productPayWays` in shared is the one rule (points when it has a points price, is free or is pay-what-you-want; money when it has a money price). `POST /store/:id/checkout` answers 400 "This one is paid for with money, not points" for a points order on a money-only product, before any coupon is used, and the buy box, the Studio editor's hint and the Studio list show only the ways that product can be paid.

- Webhooks: verify the signature on the raw body, store the raw payload in `webhook_events`, then process. Duplicates are no-ops.
- The /go redirect writes the click after the redirect is sent and never stores a raw IP, only a salted hash.
- Journal and wellbeing data never reach brands, staff, the leaderboard or Club selection. The one other door is a life coach the person said yes to: wellbeing numbers (never a tracker's note) and saved reviews (never their snapshot) reach that coach only while the person's own Reflection switch on that link is on; the journal never reaches a coach (see Life coaches).

A task cannot be ticked before its do date (see Tasks below): `POST /nodes/:id/complete` takes the person's date and refuses an early tick, and `TaskRow` locks the box with "Tick it on the day"; the due date never holds a tick back. A ticked task stays in sight: `GET /today` returns the tasks ticked on the person's day (`done`, bounded by their zone through `zonedToUtc`), and Daily lists them under Done today, struck through with the time; tapping one unticks it, which takes back its xp.

- Tasks (migrations 0048 and 0049, `routes/nodes.ts`, `components/TaskPlan.tsx`, `components/TaskRow.tsx`, `screens/Today.tsx`): a task has a **do date** (`do_date`, the day it will be done; it opens on Daily from that day and is the only date that holds a tick back) and an optional **due date** (`due_date`, the deadline; shown as "Due" and "Overdue", never a lock). The do date cannot come after the due date (`DATES_PROBLEM`, checked on create and edit). `openDay` is the day a task opens (do date, else due date); `GET /today` lists what is open on or before the person's day. A task with neither date is in **Not scheduled yet** (`backlog` and `backlogTotal` on `TodayResponse`, a folded list under Tasks on Daily), and never in the main list until a do date or a due date is picked; `BacklogTask` offers Today, Tomorrow and a date field, and nothing auto-fills undated tasks into the main list. A task can **repeat** (`Repeat`: daily, weekdays, weekly on chosen days, monthly, yearly, every 1 to 52, an optional end day; `parseRepeat`, `nextOccurrence` and `repeatLabel` in shared are the one rule, a month end is clamped and a leap day falls on the 28th): it needs a do date or a due date to count from, ticking it makes the next one in `spawnNext` (same place, words, note, estimate, time, priority, remind and subtasks unticked, the do to due gap kept, `spawned_from` so a tick never makes two) before the project-clear check, and unticking deletes the untouched spawned child.
